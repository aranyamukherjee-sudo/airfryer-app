Dal Baati (Baati Only)

Airfryer – Bake 190°C 28 min makes ~8

Vegetarian • Rajasthani

Prep ahead: Rest the dough for 20 minutes before shaping so it firms up and holds together while baking.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups whole wheat flour (coarse, if available)
  • 1/2 cup semolina (sooji)
  • 1/2 cup ghee, melted, plus extra for dunking
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • Salt to taste
  • Water as needed

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 190°C for 4 minutes before adding the food to the basket.
2Mix flour, semolina, melted ghee, baking soda and salt; add water gradually to form a firm, tight dough. Rest 20 minutes.
3Divide into 8 balls, pressing each firmly so it doesn’t crack while baking.
4Arrange the balls in the basket, spaced apart.
5Bake at 190°C for 28 minutes, turning every 10 minutes, until deep golden and firm all over.
6While still hot, dunk each baati briefly in melted ghee before serving with dal.

Tip: Serve with the Everyday Toor Dal or Cholar Dal recipe elsewhere in this book for the full dal-baati experience.
