Vegetable Au Gratin

Airfryer – Bake 180°C 19 min — Serves 4

Ingredients

  • 2 cups mixed boiled vegetables (broccoli, carrot, beans, cauliflower)
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 tbsp flour
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1/2 cup grated cheese
  • Salt & pepper to taste
  • 2 tbsp breadcrumbs

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 180°C for 4 minutes before adding the food to the basket.
2Make a white sauce: melt butter, whisk in flour, then milk gradually until thick; season.
3Layer vegetables in an oven-safe dish and pour sauce over.
4Top with cheese and breadcrumbs.
5Bake at 180°C for 19 minutes until bubbling and golden on top.
