Chicken Hakka Noodles

Chef Magic – Boil then Sauté Auto 22 min — Serves 3

High-Protein • Everyday • Indo-Chinese

🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic🍽 Serves 3
IngredientsStepsTips

Prep ahead: Rinse the boiled noodles under cold water and toss with a little oil to stop them sticking together.

Ingredients

  • 200g hakka or thin noodles
  • 200g boneless chicken, thinly sliced
  • 1 cup thinly sliced mixed vegetables (cabbage, carrot, capsicum)
  • 2 spring onions, sliced (whites and greens separated)
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste (adrak-lehsun paste)
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp vinegar
  • 1 tsp chilli sauce (optional)
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt & pepper to taste

Method

1Add water and a pinch of salt to the jar; select Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C) and cook the noodles until just done, about 6 minutes; drain, rinse in cold water, and toss with a little oil.
2Add oil to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and cook the chicken with spring onion whites and ginger-garlic paste for 6 minutes until cooked through; remove and set aside.
3Add the mixed vegetables to the same jar and sauté for 5 minutes, keeping them crisp-tender.
4Return the chicken to the jar with the noodles, soy sauce, vinegar, chilli sauce and pepper; toss on Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) for 5 minutes until well combined.
5Garnish with spring onion greens and serve hot.

Tip: Cook the chicken separately first and add it back at the end — this keeps it from overcooking while the noodles toss.
