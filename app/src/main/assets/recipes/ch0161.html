Vegetable Manchurian Gravy

Airfryer (Fry the balls) → Chef Magic (Make 200°C Fry 15 min + Gravy 12 min gravy) — Serves 4

Vegan • Everyday • Indo-Chinese

Ingredients

  • 2 cups finely shredded cabbage, carrot & capsicum
  • 3 tbsp cornflour
  • 2 tbsp maida (plain flour)
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste (adrak-lehsun paste), divided
  • Salt to taste
  • Oil spray
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp chilli sauce
  • 1 tbsp tomato ketchup
  • 1 tbsp vinegar
  • 2 cups vegetable stock or water
  • 2 tsp cornflour, mixed with water, for the slurry
  • Spring onion greens to garnish

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 200°C for 4 minutes before adding the food.
2Mix shredded vegetables with cornflour, maida, half the ginger-garlic paste and salt; shape into small balls.
3Spray the balls generously with oil and arrange in the Airfryer basket.
4Air-fry at 200°C for 15 minutes, shaking halfway, until golden and crisp.
5In Chef Magic, add a little oil; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and cook the onion with the remaining ginger-garlic paste for 3 minutes.
6Add soy sauce, chilli sauce, ketchup, vinegar and stock; select Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C) and simmer for 4 minutes.
7Stir in the cornflour slurry and cook 3 minutes until the gravy turns glossy and slightly thick, then add the fried balls and simmer gently for 2 minutes so they soften just slightly in the gravy. Garnish with spring onion.

Tip: Unlike the dry version, the balls are meant to soak a little here — a thin, silky gravy is the goal, not a thick one.
