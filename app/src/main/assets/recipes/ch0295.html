Homemade Mozzarella (Stretchy Fresh Cheese)

Chef Magic – Sauté 20 min + Stretch 10 min — Makes ~250g

Gluten-Free • Vegetarian • Homemade

🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic
IngredientsStepsTips

Ingredients

  • 2 litres whole milk (not ultra-pasteurised)
  • 1.5 tsp citric acid, dissolved in 1/4 cup cool water
  • 1/4 tablet rennet, dissolved in 1/4 cup cool water (or 1/4 tsp liquid rennet)
  • 1 tsp salt
  • Extra water, for stretching (heated separately)

Method

1Add the milk to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 1, ~30–35°C) and warm gently. Add the citric acid solution, stirring to combine.
2Continue warming to about 32°C, then add the rennet solution and stir gently for 30 seconds. Turn off and let it sit undisturbed for 5 minutes until it sets into a custard-like curd.
3Cut the curd into 2cm cubes with a knife. Select Sauté (Speed 1, ~40°C) and gently heat for 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the curds firm up and separate further from the whey.
4Scoop out the curds with a slotted spoon into a bowl, pressing gently to remove excess whey.
5Heat a separate bowl of water to just below boiling. Submerge the curd ball in the hot water for 30 seconds, then remove and knead/stretch it like taffy, folding and pulling until smooth and glossy, dipping back into the hot water as needed to keep it pliable.
6Add the salt, shape into a ball, and place in ice water to firm up. Store refrigerated in its whey for up to a week.

Tip: Mozzarella needs true dairy (not ultra-pasteurised) milk to set properly — check the carton before you start.
