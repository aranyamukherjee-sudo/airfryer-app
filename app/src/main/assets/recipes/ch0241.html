Jalapeño Poppers

Airfryer – Bake 180°C 11 min makes ~12

Ingredients

  • 6 jalapeños, halved and seeded
  • 1/2 cup cream cheese, softened
  • 1/4 cup grated cheddar
  • 1/4 tsp garlic (lehsun) powder
  • 6 strips bacon, halved (optional)

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 180°C for 4 minutes before adding the food to the basket.
2Mix cream cheese, cheddar and garlic powder; spoon into jalapeño halves.
3Wrap each with a piece of bacon if using, securing with a toothpick.
4Arrange in the basket, cut-side up.
5Bake at 180°C for 11 minutes until peppers soften and the topping is bubbling.
