Pizza Dough

Chef Magic – Knead 5 min + Rise 1 hr — makes 2 pizza bases

🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic
IngredientsStepsTips

Ingredients

  • 2.5 cups all-purpose flour, plus extra for dusting
  • 1 tsp instant yeast
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 cup warm water (105–110°F / 40–43°C)
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp salt

Method

1In Chef Magic, dissolve yeast and sugar in the warm water heated to 105–110°F (40–43°C) — warm to the touch, not hot; anything above 120°F (49°C) will kill the yeast; let sit 5–10 minutes until frothy.
2Add the flour, olive oil and salt to the jar with the yeast mixture; select Knead (Speed 2–3, no heat) for 5 minutes until a smooth, elastic dough forms.
3Lightly oil the dough, cover, and let it rise for about 1 hour, until doubled.
4Punch down and divide into 2 portions. Roll or stretch each into a base, ready for sauce, cheese and toppings of your choice.
5Use immediately, or wrap and refrigerate for up to 2 days — bring to room temperature before shaping.

Tip: For an even better base, let the shaped dough rest for 10 minutes before adding toppings — it relaxes the gluten and stops the base from shrinking back as you stretch it.
