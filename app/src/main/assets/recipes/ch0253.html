Chicken Changezi

Chef Magic (Grill marinade) → Airfryer 200°C Grill 15 min → Chef Magic Gravy 18 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • High-Protein • Everyday

Prep ahead: Marinate the chicken for at least 30 minutes before grilling.

Ingredients

  • 500g boneless chicken, cubed
  • 1/2 cup yogurt
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste (adrak-lehsun paste)
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder (lal mirch powder)
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric (haldi)
  • 2 onions, sliced
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 10 cashews, soaked
  • 1 tbsp chopped mixed dry fruits (almonds, raisins)
  • 1 tsp coriander powder (dhania powder)
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 3 tbsp ghee or oil
  • 1/4 cup cream
  • Salt to taste
  • Coriander leaves (hara dhania) to garnish

Method

1Preheat: When you reach the Airfryer step, preheat it to 200°C for 4 minutes before adding the food.
2Mix the chicken with yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, chilli powder, turmeric and salt; marinate for at least 30 minutes.
3Arrange the chicken in a single layer in the Airfryer basket and grill at 200°C for 15 minutes, turning once, until charred at the edges and cooked through.
4Meanwhile, in Chef Magic, add ghee; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and fry the onions until golden. Blend with the soaked cashews and a splash of water into a smooth paste.
5Return the paste to the jar with tomatoes, coriander powder and salt; cook on Sauté for 8 minutes until the oil separates.
6Add the grilled chicken and 1/2 cup water; select Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C) and simmer for 6 minutes.
7Stir in cream, garam masala and chopped dry fruits; simmer for 2 minutes and garnish with coriander before serving.

Tip: Grilling the chicken first (rather than boiling it straight in the gravy) is what gives Changezi its char and keeps the dry-fruit gravy from turning watery.
