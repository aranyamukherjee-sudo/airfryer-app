Soya Chaap Tikka Masala

Airfryer (Grill the tikka) → Chef Magic (Make the 200°C Grill 15 min + Gravy 16 min gravy) — Serves 4

High-Protein • Vegetarian • Everyday

Prep ahead: Marinate the soya chaap pieces for at least 30 minutes before grilling.

Ingredients

  • 500g soya chaap, cut into pieces
  • 1/2 cup yogurt
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste (adrak-lehsun paste)
  • 1.5 tsp tikka masala
  • 2 onions, chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, pureed
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1/2 cup cream
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • Salt to taste

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 200°C for 4 minutes before adding the food.
2Mix the soya chaap with yogurt, ginger-garlic paste and tikka masala; marinate for at least 30 minutes.
3Arrange the marinated chaap in a single layer in the Airfryer basket.
4Grill at 200°C for 15 minutes, turning once, until charred at the edges.
5In Chef Magic, add butter; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and cook onions until golden, then add tomatoes and cook until the oil separates, about 9 minutes.
6Add the grilled chaap, cream and garam masala; simmer on Sauté (Speed 1–2, ~100–110°C) for 5 minutes and serve hot.
