Popcorn Tofu

Airfryer – Fry 190°C 14 min — Serves 4

Vegetarian • High-Protein • Everyday

Ingredients

  • 400g firm tofu, cut into bite-sized cubes
  • 1/2 cup cornflour
  • 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • Salt to taste
  • 1/2 cup buttermilk (or plant milk with 1 tsp vinegar)
  • 1 cup breadcrumbs
  • Oil spray

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 190°C for 4 minutes before adding the food to the basket.
2Pat the tofu dry, then soak in the buttermilk for 10 minutes.
3Mix the cornflour, flour, paprika, garlic powder and salt in a bowl.
4Drain the tofu, toss in the flour mix, then coat in breadcrumbs.
5Spray lightly with oil and arrange in the basket in a single layer.
6Air-fry at 190°C for 12–14 minutes, shaking halfway, until golden and crisp.

Tip: Pressing the tofu for 15 minutes before cutting helps remove excess water, so the coating crisps up instead of steaming off.
