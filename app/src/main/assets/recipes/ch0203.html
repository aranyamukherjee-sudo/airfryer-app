Bread Omelette Sandwich

Chef Magic (Make omelette) → Airfryer 180°C Toast 4 min — Serves 1

Eggetarian • Everyday

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs
  • 2 slices bread
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 1 small tomato, finely chopped
  • 1 green chilli (hari mirch), finely chopped (optional)
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper (kali mirch)
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • Salt to taste
  • Tomato ketchup to serve

Method

1Preheat: When you reach the Airfryer step, preheat it to 180°C for 3 minutes.
2In Chef Magic, whisk the eggs with onion, tomato, green chilli, pepper and salt.
3Add butter to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 1–2, ~100–110°C) and pour in the egg mixture, cooking for 3 minutes until just set into a flat omelette.
4Sandwich the omelette between the two bread slices.
5Air-fry at 180°C for 4 minutes, flipping halfway, until the bread is golden and toasted.
6Slice diagonally and serve hot with ketchup.

Tip: Cook the omelette to fit the bread slice — a flat, sandwich-shaped omelette holds together far better than a folded one.
