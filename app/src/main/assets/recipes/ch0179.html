Hummus

Chef Magic – Boil then Whisk Auto 22 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • Vegan • High-Protein • Mediterranean

🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic🍽 Serves 4
IngredientsSteps

Prep ahead: Soak dried chickpeas overnight, or use 1 can (400g) drained chickpeas to skip the boiling step.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup chickpeas, soaked overnight (or 1 can, drained)
  • 3 tbsp tahini
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 3 tbsp olive oil, plus extra to serve
  • 1/2 tsp cumin
  • Salt to taste
  • Water as needed

Method

1If using dried chickpeas, add to the jar with water to cover; select Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C) and cook for 18 minutes until very soft. Drain, reserving some liquid.
2Add the chickpeas, tahini, lemon juice, garlic, olive oil, cumin and salt to the jar.
3Select Whisk/Blend (Speed 8–10), adding a little reserved liquid or water as needed, until smooth and creamy.
4Spoon into a bowl, drizzle with olive oil, and serve with pita or vegetables.
