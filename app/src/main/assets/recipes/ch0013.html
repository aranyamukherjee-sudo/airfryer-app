Popcorn Mushroom

Airfryer – Fry 190°C 12 min — Serves 3

Vegetarian • Everyday

🔥 Airfryer🍽 Serves 3
IngredientsStepsTips

Ingredients

  • 300g button mushrooms, halved or quartered
  • 1/2 cup cornflour
  • 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • Salt to taste
  • 1/2 cup buttermilk
  • 1 cup breadcrumbs
  • Oil spray

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 190°C for 4 minutes before adding the food to the basket.
2Soak the mushrooms in buttermilk for 10 minutes.
3Mix the cornflour, flour, pepper, garlic powder and salt in a bowl.
4Drain the mushrooms, toss in the flour mix, then coat in breadcrumbs.
5Spray lightly with oil and arrange in the basket in a single layer.
6Air-fry at 190°C for 10–12 minutes, shaking halfway, until golden and crisp.

Tip: Mushrooms release moisture as they cook, so don't overcrowd the basket — cook in batches if needed for maximum crunch.
