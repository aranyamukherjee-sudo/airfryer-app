Achari Paneer (Pickle-Spiced)

Chef Magic – Sauté Auto 20 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • Vegetarian • High-Protein • Everyday

🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic🍽 Serves 4
IngredientsStepsTips

Ingredients

  • 250g paneer, cubed
  • 1 tsp mustard seeds (rai)
  • 1 tsp fennel seeds (saunf)
  • 1/2 tsp fenugreek (methi) seeds
  • 1/2 tsp nigella (kalonji) seeds
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 tomato, chopped
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric (haldi)
  • 1.5 tsp red chilli powder (lal mirch powder)
  • 2 tbsp mustard oil (sarson ka tel)
  • 1 tbsp yogurt
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • Salt to taste

Method

1Add mustard oil to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and add mustard, fennel, fenugreek and nigella seeds; cook 1 minute until fragrant.
2Add the onion and cook for 5 minutes until golden.
3Add tomato, turmeric and chilli powder; sauté 6 minutes until the tomato breaks down and the oil separates.
4Whisk in the yogurt and salt; simmer on Sauté (Speed 1–2, ~100–110°C) for 3 minutes.
5Gently fold in the paneer cubes; sauté for 4 minutes until well coated, then finish with lemon juice.

Tip: Toast the whole spices just until they smell fragrant — a few seconds too long and the fenugreek turns bitter.
