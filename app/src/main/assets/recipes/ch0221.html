Kosha Mangsho (Bengali Slow-Cooked Mutton Curry)

Chef Magic – Sauté + Boil Auto 48 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • Bengali • High-Protein

🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic🍽 Serves 4
IngredientsStepsTips

Prep ahead: Marinate mutton with yogurt, ginger-garlic paste and spices for at least 1 hour (overnight is best) before cooking.

Ingredients

  • 500g mutton, cut into pieces
  • 1/2 cup yogurt
  • 2 onions, finely sliced
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste (adrak-lehsun paste)
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder (lal mirch powder)
  • 1 tsp cumin (jeera)-coriander powder (dhania powder)
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • 2 tbsp mustard oil (sarson ka tel)
  • 2 bay leaves (tej patta)
  • Salt to taste

Method

1Marinate mutton with yogurt, ginger-garlic paste and spices for at least 1 hour.
2Add oil, bay leaves and onions to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and cook until deep golden-brown.
3Add the marinated mutton; continue on Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C), stirring occasionally, for 18 minutes until well browned and the marinade has darkened.
4Add a little water, select Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C), and cook covered until the mutton is tender and the gravy has thickened and turned deep brown.

Tip: Kosha Mangsho is meant to be a thick, ‘dry-ish’ curry — resist adding too much water.
