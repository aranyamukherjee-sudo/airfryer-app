Aloo Tikki (Potato Cutlets)

Airfryer or Chef Magic — pick whichever you have free — 22 min makes ~8

🔥 Airfryer🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic
IngredientsStepsTips

Prep ahead: Boil and cool potatoes fully (or use leftover boiled potatoes) before mixing — warm potato makes a sticky dough.

Ingredients

  • 4 medium potatoes, boiled & mashed
  • 2 tbsp cornflour
  • 1 green chilli (hari mirch), chopped
  • 1 tsp ginger (adrak), grated
  • 1/2 tsp roasted cumin powder (bhuna jeera powder)
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • 2 tbsp chopped coriander (dhania)
  • Salt to taste
  • Oil (for both methods)

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 190°C for 4 minutes before adding the food.
2Mix all ingredients into a firm dough and shape into patties.
3Option A – Airfryer: brush or spray both sides lightly with oil, arrange in a single layer in the basket, and air-fry at 190°C for 16 minutes, flipping halfway, until golden and crisp.
4Option B – Chef Magic: add oil to the jar, select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C), and cook the patties, turning occasionally, for 11 minutes until golden on both sides.

Tip: Chef Magic’s sauté gives a slightly richer, more traditionally fried crust; the Airfryer version uses far less oil.
