Chicken Tikka (marinated & grilled)

Chef Magic (Chop & mix marinade) → Airfryer 190°C Marinate 30 min + Grill 13 min (Grill) — Serves 4

🔥 Airfryer🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic🍽 Serves 4
IngredientsSteps

Prep ahead: Marinate the chicken for at least 30 minutes before grilling.

Ingredients

  • 500g boneless chicken, cubed
  • 1/2 cup thick yogurt
  • 1 onion, roughly chopped
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste (adrak-lehsun paste)
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder (lal mirch powder)
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt to taste

Method

1Preheat: When you reach the Airfryer step, preheat it to 190°C for 4 minutes before adding the food.
2In Chef Magic, chop the onion briefly, then add yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, spices, oil and salt; mix to combine into a marinade.
3Coat chicken pieces in the marinade and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes.
4Skewer or arrange chicken in the Airfryer basket.
5Grill at 190°C for 13 minutes, turning once, until charred at the edges and cooked through.
