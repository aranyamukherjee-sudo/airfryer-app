Popcorn Fish Bites

Airfryer – Fry 190°C 10 min — Serves 4

High-Protein • Everyday

🔥 Airfryer🍽 Serves 4
IngredientsStepsTips

Ingredients

  • 400g boneless white fish (basa or similar), cut into bite-sized pieces
  • 1/2 cup cornflour
  • 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 tsp paprika
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 1 cup breadcrumbs
  • Oil spray

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 190°C for 4 minutes before adding the food to the basket.
2Pat the fish pieces dry. Mix the cornflour, flour, paprika, salt and pepper in a bowl.
3Toss the fish in the flour mix, dip in the beaten egg, then coat in breadcrumbs.
4Spray lightly with oil and arrange in the basket in a single layer.
5Air-fry at 190°C for 8–10 minutes, shaking halfway, until golden and crisp and the fish flakes easily.

Tip: Choose a firm white fish like basa or tilapia — flakier fish can fall apart during the toss-and-coat steps.
