Anda Curry (North Indian Egg Curry)

Chef Magic – Sauté + Boil Auto 24 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • High-Protein • Everyday

🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic🍽 Serves 4
IngredientsSteps

Prep ahead: Lightly score the boiled eggs before adding them to the gravy so they soak up more flavour.

Ingredients

  • 6 hard-boiled eggs, peeled
  • 2 onions, chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, pureed
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste (adrak-lehsun paste)
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder (lal mirch powder)
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric (haldi)
  • 1 tsp coriander powder (dhania powder)
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt to taste
  • Chopped coriander (dhania) to garnish

Method

1Add oil to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and cook onions for 6 minutes until golden.
2Add ginger-garlic paste, tomato puree, turmeric, chilli powder and coriander powder; sauté for 8 minutes until the oil separates.
3Add a splash of water and salt; select Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C) and simmer for 5 minutes until the gravy thickens.
4Gently add the boiled eggs and garam masala; simmer on Sauté (Speed 1–2, ~100–110°C) for 5 minutes so the eggs soak up the gravy. Garnish with coriander.
