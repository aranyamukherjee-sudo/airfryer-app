One-Pot Chicken Paella

Chef Magic – Sauté + Boil Auto 34 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • High-Protein • Spanish

Ingredients

  • 500g chicken thighs, cubed
  • 1.5 cups short-grain rice
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 bell pepper, chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1/2 cup peas
  • A few saffron strands in warm water
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 3 cups chicken stock
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt & pepper to taste
  • Lemon wedges to serve

Method

1Add olive oil to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and brown the chicken for 8 minutes; remove and set aside.
2In the same jar, cook onion and bell pepper for 5 minutes, then add tomatoes and smoked paprika, cooking 3 minutes.
3Add rice, stock, saffron water, salt and pepper, and return the chicken to the jar.
4Select Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C) and cook for 16 minutes until rice is tender and liquid is absorbed; stir in peas for the last 2 minutes. Serve with lemon wedges.
