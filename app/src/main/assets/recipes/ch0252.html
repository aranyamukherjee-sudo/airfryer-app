Punjabi Dhaba-Style Murgh Curry (Bone-In)

Chef Magic – Sauté + Boil Auto 34 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • High-Protein • Everyday

🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic🍽 Serves 4
IngredientsStepsTips

Prep ahead: Bone-in chicken gives the best flavour here — ask for curry-cut pieces with bone.

Ingredients

  • 600g chicken, bone-in curry-cut pieces
  • 2 onions, finely chopped
  • 3 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste (adrak-lehsun paste)
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder (lal mirch powder)
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric (haldi)
  • 1 tsp coriander powder (dhania powder)
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 4 tbsp mustard oil (sarson ka tel)
  • 1 bay leaf (tej patta), 2 cloves (laung), 1 black cardamom (elaichi)
  • Salt to taste
  • Coriander leaves (hara dhania) to garnish

Method

1In Chef Magic, add mustard oil; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and heat until it just starts to smoke, then let it settle for 30 seconds — this takes the rawness out of the oil.
2Add the bay leaf, cloves and black cardamom; sauté for 1 minute until fragrant.
3Add onions and cook for 8 minutes until deep golden.
4Add ginger-garlic paste and cook for 1 minute, then add tomatoes, chilli powder, turmeric, coriander powder and salt; cook for 8 minutes until the oil separates.
5Add the chicken and sauté for 6 minutes until well coated and lightly browned.
6Add 1.5 cups water; select Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C) and simmer covered for 15 minutes until the chicken is falling-off-the-bone tender.
7Sprinkle garam masala and coriander leaves before serving with tandoori roti.

Tip: This is the heavier, ghee-and-mustard-oil dhaba cousin of an everyday chicken curry — bone-in pieces and a longer simmer are what give it that roadside-stall depth.
