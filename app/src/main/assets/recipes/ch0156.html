Kerala Avial

Chef Magic – Boil then Sauté Auto 24 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • Vegetarian • Kerala

Ingredients

  • 3 cups mixed vegetables (carrot, beans, drumstick, raw banana), cut into batons
  • 1/2 cup grated coconut
  • 1/2 tsp cumin seeds (jeera)
  • 2 green chillies (hari mirch)
  • 1/2 cup yogurt
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric (haldi)
  • 2 tbsp coconut oil
  • 9 curry leaves (kadi patta)
  • Salt to taste

Method

1Add the mixed vegetables, turmeric and enough water to just cover to the jar; select Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C) and cook for 12 minutes until tender.
2Grind the grated coconut, cumin seeds and green chillies with a little water into a coarse paste.
3Stir the coconut paste and whisked yogurt into the cooked vegetables; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) briefly for 5 minutes to warm through without boiling hard.
4Finish with coconut oil and curry leaves; season with salt and serve.
