Goan Fish Curry (Air Fryer-Seared)

Airfryer (Sear the fish) → Chef Magic (Make the 200°C Sear 8 min + Curry 18 min curry) — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • High-Protein

🔥 Airfryer🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic🍽 Serves 4
IngredientsStepsTips

Prep ahead: Marinate the fish in a little turmeric, chilli powder and salt for 15 minutes before searing.

Ingredients

  • 500g firm fish fillets (pomfret, kingfish or basa), cut into large pieces
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric (haldi)
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder (lal mirch powder), divided
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 tomato, chopped
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste (adrak-lehsun paste)
  • 1 cup coconut milk
  • 1 tbsp tamarind (imli) paste
  • 9 curry leaves (kadi patta)
  • 2 tbsp coconut oil
  • Salt to taste
  • Oil spray

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 200°C for 4 minutes before adding the food to the basket.
2Rub the fish pieces with turmeric, half the chilli powder and salt; rest 15 minutes.
3Spray the fish lightly with oil and arrange in a single layer in the basket.
4Grill at 200°C for 8 minutes, turning once, until lightly seared and firm but not fully cooked through.
5Meanwhile, add coconut oil and curry leaves to Chef Magic’s jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and cook the onion for 6 minutes until soft.
6Add ginger-garlic paste, tomato and the remaining chilli powder; sauté for 5 minutes until the tomato breaks down.
7Add the coconut milk and tamarind paste; select Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C) and simmer for 5 minutes.
8Gently slide in the seared fish and simmer a further 5 minutes, stirring as little as possible, until just cooked through.

Tip: Searing the fish first means it only needs a brief, gentle simmer in the curry — that’s what keeps the pieces intact instead
