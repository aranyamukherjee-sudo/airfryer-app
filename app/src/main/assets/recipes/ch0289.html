Chicken Shawarma (Airfryer)

Chef Magic (Mix marinade) → Airfryer 200°C Marinate 30 min + Grill 16 min — Serves 4

High-Protein • Everyday

🔥 Airfryer🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic🍽 Serves 4
IngredientsStepsTips

Prep ahead: Marinate the chicken for at least 30 minutes before grilling.

Ingredients

  • 500g boneless chicken thighs, sliced
  • 2 tbsp yogurt
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp cumin powder (jeera powder)
  • 1 tsp coriander powder (dhania powder)
  • 1/2 tsp paprika
  • 1/4 tsp cinnamon (dalchini) powder
  • 2 cloves garlic (lehsun), minced
  • Salt to taste
  • Pita or flatbread, sliced onion, tomato and garlic (lehsun) sauce to serve

Method

1Preheat: When you reach the Airfryer step, preheat it to 200°C for 4 minutes before adding the food.
2In Chef Magic, mix the yogurt, lemon juice, oil, cumin, coriander, paprika, cinnamon, garlic and salt into a marinade.
3Coat the chicken slices well and rest for at least 30 minutes.
4Arrange the chicken in a single layer in the Airfryer basket.
5Grill at 200°C for 16 minutes, tossing halfway, until charred at the edges and cooked through.
6Pile into warm pita or flatbread with sliced onion, tomato and garlic sauce.

Tip: Slicing the chicken thin before marinating means it grills fast and shreds easily, just like a spit-roasted shawarma.
