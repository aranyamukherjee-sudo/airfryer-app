Popcorn Shrimp

Airfryer – Fry 190°C 9 min — Serves 4

High-Protein • Everyday

Ingredients

  • 400g shrimp/prawns, peeled and deveined
  • 1/2 cup cornflour
  • 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 tsp paprika
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 1 cup breadcrumbs
  • Oil spray

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 190°C for 4 minutes before adding the food to the basket.
2Pat the shrimp dry. Mix the cornflour, flour, paprika, garlic powder and salt in a bowl.
3Toss the shrimp in the flour mix, dip in the beaten egg, then coat in breadcrumbs, pressing to help them stick.
4Spray lightly with oil and arrange in the basket in a single layer.
5Air-fry at 190°C for 7–9 minutes, shaking halfway, until golden and crisp — take care not to overcook, as shrimp turn rubbery quickly.

Tip: Shrimp cook very fast — pull them the moment they turn opaque and the coating is golden, even if that's a minute earlier than the recipe states.
