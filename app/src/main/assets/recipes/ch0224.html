Tandoori Prawn Skewers

Airfryer – Grill 200°C 9 min — Serves 3

Gluten-Free • High-Protein

🔥 Airfryer🍽 Serves 3
IngredientsSteps

Prep ahead: Marinate the prawns for 20–30 minutes only — much longer and the yogurt-lemon marinade starts to ‘cook’ the

Ingredients

  • 500g prawns, peeled and deveined
  • 1/2 cup thick yogurt
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste (adrak-lehsun paste)
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder (lal mirch powder)
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp mustard oil (sarson ka tel)
  • Salt to taste

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 200°C for 4 minutes before adding the food to the basket.
2Whisk together yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, chilli powder, garam masala, lemon juice, mustard oil and salt.
3Coat the prawns in the marinade and rest for 20–30 minutes.
4Skewer or arrange in a single layer in the basket.
5Grill at 200°C for 9 minutes, turning once, until charred at the edges and just cooked through.
6Serve with mint chutney and lemon wedges.
