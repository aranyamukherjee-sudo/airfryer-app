Bibimbap Bowl

Stovetop Prep – Assemble 25 min — Serves 2

Eggetarian • Korean • High-Protein • Everyday

🍽 Serves 2
IngredientsStepsTips

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked short-grain rice
  • 1 carrot, julienned
  • 1 cup spinach, blanched
  • 1 cup bean sprouts, blanched
  • 1 zucchini, julienned and sautéed
  • 4 shiitake or button mushrooms, sliced and sautéed
  • 2 eggs, fried sunny-side up
  • 2 tbsp gochujang, per bowl
  • 1 tsp sesame oil, per bowl
  • Toasted sesame seeds, to garnish
  • Salt to taste, for each vegetable

Method

1Prepare each vegetable separately: sauté the zucchini and mushrooms lightly with a pinch of salt; blanch the spinach and bean sprouts, then season each with a few drops of sesame oil and salt.
2Divide the warm rice between two bowls.
3Arrange the carrot, spinach, bean sprouts, zucchini and mushrooms in separate sections on top of the rice.
4Top each bowl with a fried egg.
5Add a generous spoonful of gochujang in the centre, drizzle with sesame oil, and scatter over sesame seeds.
6Mix everything together well just before eating.

Tip: The joy of bibimbap is in the mixing — stir everything together vigorously right before you eat so every bite gets a little of each ingredient.
