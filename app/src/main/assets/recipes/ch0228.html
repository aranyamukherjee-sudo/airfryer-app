Crispy Prawn Pakoras

Airfryer or Chef Magic — pick whichever you have free — 18 min — Serves 4

High-Protein • Everyday

Ingredients

  • 500g prawns, peeled and deveined (tails on)
  • 3/4 cup besan (gram flour (besan))
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric (haldi)
  • 1/2 tsp red chilli powder (lal mirch powder)
  • 1/4 tsp ajwain (carom seeds (ajwain))
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • Salt to taste
  • Water as needed
  • Oil (for both methods)

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 190°C for 4 minutes before adding the food to the basket.
2Whisk besan, turmeric, chilli powder, ajwain, lemon juice, salt and just enough water into a thick batter; coat the prawns.
3Option A – Airfryer: spray generously with oil, arrange in a single layer in the basket, and air-fry at 190°C for 10 minutes, shaking halfway, until golden and crisp.
4Option B – Chef Magic: add 3 tbsp oil to the jar, select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C), and shallow-fry the prawns, turning gently, for 8 minutes until golden on both sides. Combo Recipes — Chef Magic + Airfryer
