Palak Dal (Spinach Dal)

Chef Magic – Sauté + Boil Auto 22 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • Vegan • High-Fibre • Everyday

Ingredients

  • 1 cup toor or moong dal, washed
  • 2 cups spinach, chopped
  • 3 cups water
  • 1/4 tsp turmeric (haldi)
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds (jeera)
  • 2 cloves garlic (lehsun), chopped
  • 1 dried red chilli (lal mirch)
  • 1 tbsp ghee or oil
  • Salt to taste

Method

1Add dal, water, turmeric and salt to the jar; select Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C) and cook until nearly soft.
2Add the chopped spinach and continue on Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C) for 5 minutes until the dal is fully soft and the spinach has wilted in.
3Add ghee, cumin seeds, garlic and dried red chilli directly to the cooked dal; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) for 2 minutes to temper until the garlic turns golden.
4Stir well and serve hot with rice or roti.
