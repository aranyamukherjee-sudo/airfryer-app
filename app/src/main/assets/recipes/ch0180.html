Crispy Falafel

Airfryer – Fry 200°C 16 min makes ~14

Vegan • High-Protein • Mediterranean

🔥 Airfryer
IngredientsSteps

Prep ahead: Use dried chickpeas soaked overnight (not canned) — canned chickpeas make the mixture too wet to hold

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups dried chickpeas, soaked overnight
  • 1 small onion, roughly chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 1 cup chopped parsley & coriander
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • 2 tbsp flour
  • Salt to taste
  • Oil spray

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 200°C for 4 minutes before adding the food to the basket.
2In a food processor or Chef Magic’s Chop function, blend the soaked (uncooked) chickpeas, onion, garlic and herbs to a coarse, sticky paste.
3Mix in cumin, coriander powder, baking soda, flour and salt; shape into small patties.
4Spray the patties with oil and arrange in the basket.
5Air-fry at 200°C for 16 minutes, flipping halfway, until deep golden and crisp.
