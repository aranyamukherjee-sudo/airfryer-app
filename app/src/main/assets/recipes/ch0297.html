Classic Eggless Mayonnaise

Chef Magic – Whisk/Blend 4 min — Makes ~1.5 cups

Gluten-Free • Vegetarian • Eggless • Homemade

🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic
IngredientsStepsTips

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup full-fat milk, cold
  • 1 cup neutral oil (sunflower or vegetable)
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice or white vinegar
  • 1/2 tsp mustard powder (optional)
  • 1/2 tsp sugar
  • Salt to taste

Method

1Add the cold milk to the jar; select Whisk/Blend (Speed 8–10) and blend for 20 seconds.
2With the machine running on Whisk/Blend (Speed 8–10), slowly drizzle in the oil in a thin, steady stream over 2–3 minutes until the mixture thickens and emulsifies into a mayonnaise-like consistency.
3Add the lemon juice, mustard powder, sugar and salt; blend for another 15 seconds to combine.
4Taste and adjust salt or lemon juice as needed. Transfer to a clean jar and refrigerate.

Tip: Everything works best cold — chilled milk with room-temperature oil added slowly is the secret to a mayo that doesn't split.
