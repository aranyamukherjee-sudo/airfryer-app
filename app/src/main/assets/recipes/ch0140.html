Amritsari Paneer Tikka

Chef Magic (Marinade) → Airfryer (Grill) 200°C Marinate 30 min + Grill 14 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • Vegetarian • High-Protein • Punjabi

Prep ahead: Marinate the paneer for at least 30 minutes — the gram flour coating needs time to set and cling well.

Ingredients

  • 400g paneer, cubed
  • 1/2 cup thick yogurt, hung for 10 minutes to thicken
  • 2 tbsp besan (gram flour (besan)), lightly roasted
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste (adrak-lehsun paste)
  • 1 tsp ajwain (carom seeds (ajwain))
  • 1.5 tsp red chilli powder (lal mirch powder)
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tbsp mustard oil (sarson ka tel)
  • Salt to taste
  • Chaat masala & lemon wedges to finish

Method

1Preheat: When you reach the Airfryer step, preheat it to 200°C for 4 minutes before adding the food.
2In Chef Magic, whisk the hung yogurt with roasted besan, ginger-garlic paste, ajwain, chilli powder, garam masala, mustard oil and salt into a thick marinade.
3Coat the paneer cubes in the marinade and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes.
4Skewer or arrange in the Airfryer basket.
5Grill at 200°C for 14 minutes, turning once, until the coating is lightly charred and crisp at the edges.
6Dust with chaat masala and serve with lemon wedges and mint chutney.

Tip: Roasting the besan for a minute before mixing it in removes the raw taste that can otherwise come through in the coating.
