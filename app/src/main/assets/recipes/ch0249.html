Chicken Do Pyaza

Chef Magic – Sauté + Boil Auto 30 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • High-Protein • Everyday

🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic🍽 Serves 4
IngredientsStepsTips

Ingredients

  • 500g chicken, curry-cut pieces
  • 3 onions — 2 sliced, 1 cubed into petals
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste (adrak-lehsun paste)
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder (lal mirch powder)
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric (haldi)
  • 1 tsp coriander powder (dhania powder)
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • Salt to taste
  • Coriander leaves (hara dhania) to garnish

Method

1In Chef Magic, add oil; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and cook the sliced onions for 8 minutes until deep golden. Blend to a smooth paste with a splash of water; set aside.
2Add a little more oil to the jar; sauté the onion petals for 3 minutes until just softened and lightly charred at the edges. Set aside for garnish.
3Add ginger-garlic paste to the jar and cook for 1 minute, then add tomatoes, chilli powder, turmeric, coriander powder and salt; cook for 5 minutes until the oil separates.
4Stir in the onion paste, then add the chicken; sauté for 6 minutes until well coated and lightly browned.
5Add 1 cup water; select Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C) and simmer covered for 12 minutes until the chicken is cooked through.
6Fold in the reserved onion petals and garam masala; simmer for 2 minutes and garnish with coriander before serving.

Tip: 'Do pyaza' means double onion — the smooth onion paste builds the base, and the petals on top add bite and colour.
