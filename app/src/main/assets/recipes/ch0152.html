Dum Aloo (Kashmiri Style)

Airfryer (Fry potatoes) → Chef Magic (Make 190°C Fry 15 min + Gravy 18 min gravy) — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • Vegetarian • Kashmiri

Ingredients

  • 500g baby potatoes, peeled and pricked
  • 2 tbsp oil, divided
  • 1/2 cup yogurt, whisked
  • 1 tbsp Kashmiri chilli powder (mirch powder)
  • 1 tsp fennel (saunf) powder
  • 1/2 tsp ginger (adrak) powder
  • 1 bay leaf (tej patta)
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds (jeera)
  • Salt to taste

Method

1Preheat: When you reach the Airfryer step, preheat it to 190°C for 4 minutes before adding the food.
2Toss the baby potatoes with oil and arrange in the Airfryer basket.
3Air-fry at 190°C for 15 minutes, shaking halfway, until lightly golden and just tender.
4In Chef Magic, add oil, bay leaf and cumin seeds; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) until fragrant.
5Add whisked yogurt, Kashmiri chilli powder, fennel powder, ginger powder and salt; simmer on Sauté (Speed 1–2, ~100–110°C) for 8 minutes, stirring, until thick.
6Add the fried potatoes and simmer a further 5 minutes so they soak up the gravy.
