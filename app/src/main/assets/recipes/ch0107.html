Chef Magic – Sauté + Boil Auto 16 min — Serves 3

Vegetarian • Everyday • Low-Oil

🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic🍽 Serves 3
IngredientsSteps

Ingredients

  • 1 cup semolina (sooji)
  • 1/2 cup mixed vegetables (carrot, peas, beans), chopped
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 tsp mustard seeds (rai)
  • 9 curry leaves (kadi patta)
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 2 cups water
  • Salt to taste
  • Lemon juice to finish

Method

1Add oil, mustard seeds and curry leaves to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) until seeds splutter.
2Add onion and vegetables; sauté 4 minutes.
3Add semolina and roast 2 minutes, stirring, until lightly toasted.
4Add water and salt; select Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C) and cook, stirring, until the water is absorbed and the mixture turns fluffy. Finish with lemon juice.
