Malai Kofta

Airfryer or Chef Magic — pick whichever you have free — Fry koftas 16 min (Airfryer) or 11 min (Chef Magic); Gravy 20 min in Chef Magic — Serves 4

Vegetarian • Everyday

🔥 Airfryer🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic🍽 Serves 4
IngredientsSteps

Ingredients

  • 2 potatoes, boiled & mashed
  • 150g paneer, grated
  • 2 tbsp cornflour
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 10 cashews
  • 1 onion
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste (adrak-lehsun paste)
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • 2 tbsp cream
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • Oil (for frying koftas)

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 190°C for 4 minutes before adding the food.
2Mix mashed potato, grated paneer, cornflour and salt; shape into small balls.
3Option A – Airfryer: spray generously with oil, arrange in the basket, air-fry at 190°C for 16 minutes, turning once, until golden and firm.
4Option B – Chef Magic: add oil to the jar, select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C), and shallow-fry the koftas, turning gently, for 11 minutes until golden on all sides. Remove and set aside.
5Make the gravy in Chef Magic (butter, onion, tomato, cashews, ginger-garlic — sauté, blend smooth, then simmer with cream and garam masala for 15 minutes) and pour hot over the koftas just before serving so they stay crisp longer.
