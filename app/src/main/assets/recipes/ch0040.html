Marble Cake (Vanilla-Chocolate Swirl)

Airfryer – Bake 160°C 26 min — Serves 6

Vegetarian • Everyday

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 1/2 cup butter, softened
  • 3/4 cup milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 2 tbsp milk (extra, for the cocoa batter)

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 160°C for 4 minutes before adding the food to the basket.
2Whisk the flour, baking powder and salt together in a bowl.
3Cream the butter and sugar until light and fluffy, then beat in the eggs one at a time, followed by the vanilla.
4Fold in the dry ingredients alternately with the milk, mixing until just combined and smooth.
5Divide the batter in half. Whisk the cocoa powder and extra milk into one half until evenly coloured.
6Spoon dollops of vanilla and chocolate batter alternately into a greased oven-safe pan, then swirl gently with a knife or skewer to create a marbled pattern.
7Bake at 160°C for 24–26 minutes, until a toothpick inserted in the centre comes out clean.
8Cool in the pan for 10 minutes before turning out onto a rack to cool completely.

Tip: Just 3–4 swirls with the knife gives the best marbling — over-swirling blends the two batters together and you lose the pattern.
