Chicken Burger (Crispy Chicken Patty Burger)

Airfryer – Fry 200°C 18 min — Serves 4

High-Protein • Everyday • Continental

Prep ahead: Chill the shaped patties for at least 30 minutes (or freeze for 10 minutes) before air-frying so they hold together and stay juicy.

Ingredients

  • 500g boneless chicken, minced
  • 1/2 cup breadcrumbs, plus extra for coating
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1/2 tsp red chilli powder
  • 1 tbsp chopped coriander
  • Salt to taste
  • Oil spray
  • 4 burger buns
  • Butter, for toasting the buns
  • Lettuce leaves, tomato & onion slices
  • Mayonnaise & ketchup, to serve
  • 4 cheese slices (optional)

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 200°C for 4 minutes before adding the food.
2In a bowl, combine minced chicken, breadcrumbs, egg, ginger-garlic paste, black pepper, chilli powder, coriander and salt; mix until just combined (don't overwork it).
3Shape into 4 patties and chill for at least 30 minutes.
4Coat each patty lightly in extra breadcrumbs and spray generously with oil.
5Air-fry at 200°C for 18 minutes, flipping halfway, until golden, crisp and cooked through.
6In the last 2 minutes, halve the buns and toast lightly with a little butter; melt a cheese slice onto each patty if using.
7Assemble: bun base, sauces, lettuce, tomato, onion, the patty, and the bun top. Serve hot.

Tip: Press a small dimple into the centre of each patty before cooking — chicken patties puff up in the middle as they cook, and the dimple keeps them flat.
