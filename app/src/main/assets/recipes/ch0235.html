Chicken Momos

Chef Magic (Steam) → Airfryer (Fry, for a crisp finish) 190°C — Steam 14 min + Fry 7 min — makes ~15

High-Protein • Everyday

🔥 Airfryer🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic
IngredientsStepsTips

Ingredients

  • 250g minced chicken
  • 1 small cabbage, finely shredded
  • 1 carrot, grated
  • 2 spring onions, chopped
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste (adrak-lehsun paste)
  • 1 tsp soy sauce
  • 1/2 tsp white pepper (safed mirch)
  • 20 momo/dumpling wrappers
  • Salt to taste
  • Oil spray (for the fried version)

Method

1In Chef Magic, mix the minced chicken, cabbage, carrot, spring onions, ginger-garlic paste, soy sauce, white pepper and salt into a filling.
2Place a spoonful of filling in the centre of each wrapper; pleat and seal into momo shapes.
3Arrange the momos in the Chef Magic steaming basket without touching; select Steam and cook for 14 minutes until the wrapper turns glossy and the filling is cooked through.
4For a crisp finish: spray the steamed momos with oil and air-fry at 190°C for 7 minutes, shaking halfway, until golden and lightly blistered.
5Serve hot with spicy chilli-garlic dip or momo chutney.

Tip: Don't overfill the wrappers — a slightly under-stuffed momo seals better and steams more evenly than an overstuffed one.
