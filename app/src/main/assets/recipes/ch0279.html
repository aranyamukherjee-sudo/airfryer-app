French Onion Soup

Chef Magic (Caramelise & simmer) → Airfryer 200°C 42 min total (Melt cheese topping) — Serves 4

Vegetarian • French

🔥 Airfryer🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic🍽 Serves 4
IngredientsSteps

Prep ahead: Slice onions thinly and evenly — this helps them caramelise at the same rate.

Ingredients

  • 4 large onions, thinly sliced
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 4 cups mutton or vegetable stock
  • 1 tbsp flour
  • 4 slices baguette
  • 1 cup grated Gruyère or mozzarella
  • Salt & pepper to taste

Method

1Preheat: When you reach the Airfryer step, preheat it to 200°C for 4 minutes before adding the food.
2Add butter to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and cook the sliced onions with sugar for 25 minutes, stirring occasionally, until deep golden-brown and caramelised.
3Stir in flour and cook 2 minutes, then add stock, salt and pepper.
4Select Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C) and simmer for 10 minutes.
5Ladle into oven-safe bowls, top with baguette slices and cheese, then air-fry at 200°C for 5 minutes until the cheese melts and bubbles.
