Pasta Aglio e Olio

Chef Magic – Boil then Sauté Auto 16 min — Serves 2

Vegan • Everyday • Italian

🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic🍽 Serves 2
IngredientsSteps

Ingredients

  • 200g spaghetti
  • 4 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
  • 1/2 tsp red chilli flakes
  • 4 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt to taste
  • Chopped parsley
  • Grated Parmesan (optional)

Method

1Add water and a pinch of salt to the jar; select Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C) and cook spaghetti until al dente, about 10 minutes; drain and reserve some pasta water.
2Add olive oil to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and cook sliced garlic and chilli flakes for 2 minutes until fragrant, not browned.
3Add the drained pasta and reserved pasta water; toss on Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) for 2 minutes to coat.
4Finish with parsley, salt and Parmesan if using.
