Crispy Chicken Bites (Chilli-Garlic)

Airfryer + Chef Magic – Fry 16 min + Sauce 3 min — Serves 4

High-Protein • Indo-Chinese • Everyday

🔥 Airfryer🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic🍽 Serves 4
IngredientsStepsTips

Ingredients

  • 500g boneless chicken thighs, cut into bite-sized pieces
  • 1/2 cup cornflour
  • 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • Salt to taste
  • Oil spray
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 5 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 green chillies, slit
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp red chilli sauce
  • 1 tbsp vinegar
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 2 spring onions, sliced

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 190°C for 4 minutes before adding the food to the basket.
2Dip the chicken pieces in the beaten egg, then toss in a mix of cornflour, flour and salt, coating well.
3Spray lightly with oil and arrange in the basket in a single layer.
4Air-fry at 190°C for 14–16 minutes, shaking halfway, until golden and crisp.
5While the chicken cooks, add the oil, garlic and green chillies to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–3, ~140–150°C) and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
6Add the soy sauce, chilli sauce, vinegar and sugar; simmer for 2 minutes until slightly thickened, then toss the hot chicken through the sauce. Garnish with spring onions and serve immediately.

Tip: Toss the chicken in the sauce just before serving so the coating stays crisp rather than going soft.
