Tteokbokki (Spicy Rice Cakes)

Chef Magic – Boil + Sauté 20 min — Serves 3

Eggetarian • Korean • Everyday

🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic🍽 Serves 3
IngredientsStepsTips

Ingredients

  • 400g Korean rice cakes (tteok)
  • 2 cups water or vegetable stock
  • 3 tbsp gochujang
  • 1 tbsp gochugaru (Korean chilli flakes, optional)
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 2 spring onions, cut into batons
  • 1 boiled egg per serving (optional)

Method

1If using refrigerated or frozen rice cakes, soak in warm water for 10 minutes to soften.
2Add the water/stock to the jar; select Boil (Speed 1, 100°C) and bring to a simmer.
3Whisk in the gochujang, gochugaru, soy sauce and sugar until dissolved.
4Add the onion and rice cakes; select Sauté (Speed 1–2, ~100–110°C) and simmer for 10–12 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the sauce thickens and coats the rice cakes.
5Add the spring onions in the last 2 minutes of cooking.
6Serve hot, topped with a halved boiled egg if using.

Tip: If the sauce gets too thick before the rice cakes are tender, splash in a little extra hot water to loosen it.
