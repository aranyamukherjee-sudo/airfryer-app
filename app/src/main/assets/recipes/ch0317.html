Oyster Mushroom Tikka

Chef Magic (Marinate) → Airfryer (Grill, skewered) — Serves 4

Vegetarian • High-Protein • Party Snack

🔥 Airfryer🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic🍽 Serves 4
IngredientsStepsTips

Ingredients

  • 300g oyster mushrooms (dhingri), torn into large pieces
  • 1/2 cup thick curd (dahi), whisked smooth
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste (adrak-lehsun paste)
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder (lal mirch)
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric (haldi)
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp roasted kasuri methi, crushed
  • 1 tbsp gram flour (besan)
  • 1 tbsp lemon (nimbu) juice
  • 1 tbsp mustard oil or any neutral oil
  • Salt (namak), to taste
  • 1 capsicum and 1 onion, cubed (for skewering)
  • Oil spray
  • Wooden or metal skewers

Method

1In Chef Magic, select Mix (no heat) and whisk together curd, ginger-garlic paste, chilli powder, turmeric, garam masala, kasuri methi, besan, lemon juice, oil and salt into a smooth marinade.
2Add the mushrooms, capsicum and onion; toss gently to coat well. Cover and rest 20–30 minutes (longer improves flavour).
3Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 200°C for 4 minutes before adding the food.
4Thread the marinated mushrooms alternately with capsicum and onion onto skewers.
5Spray the skewers with oil and arrange in the Airfryer basket, without overcrowding.
6Air-fry at 200°C for 12–14 minutes, turning once halfway, until charred at the edges and cooked through.
7Rest 2 minutes, squeeze over a little extra lemon juice, and serve hot with mint chutney.

Tip: If using wooden skewers, soak them in water for 20 minutes first so they don't scorch in the Airfryer.
