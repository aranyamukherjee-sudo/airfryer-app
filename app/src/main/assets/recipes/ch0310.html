Basic White Bread Dough

Chef Magic (Knead) → Airfryer (Bake) 180°C — Knead 6 min + Rise 1.5 hr + Bake 25 min — makes 1 small loaf

🔥 Airfryer🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic
IngredientsStepsTips

Ingredients

  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 tsp instant yeast
  • 1.5 tbsp sugar
  • 1 cup warm water (105–110°F / 40–43°C)
  • 2 tbsp oil or melted butter
  • 1 tsp salt

Method

1In Chef Magic, dissolve yeast and sugar in the warm water heated to 105–110°F (40–43°C) — warm to the touch, not hot; anything above 120°F (49°C) will kill the yeast; let sit 5–10 minutes until frothy.
2Add the flour, oil and salt to the jar with the yeast mixture; select Knead (Speed 2–3, no heat) for 6 minutes until a smooth, elastic dough forms.
3Lightly grease the dough, cover, and let it rise for about 1 hour, until doubled.
4Punch down the dough, shape into a loaf, and place in a greased oven-safe pan that fits the Airfryer basket. Cover and let rise for another 30 minutes.
5Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 180°C for 4 minutes before adding the food to the basket.
6Bake at 180°C for 22–25 minutes, until golden and the loaf sounds hollow when tapped on the base.
7Cool on a rack for at least 20 minutes before slicing.

Tip: Tent the loaf loosely with foil after the first 15 minutes if the top is browning faster than the inside is cooking through.
