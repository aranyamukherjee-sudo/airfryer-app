Coconut Ladoo (No-Cook Finish)

Chef Magic – Sauté Auto 12 min makes ~14

Gluten-Free • Vegetarian • No-Cook Finish

🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic
IngredientsStepsTips

Ingredients

  • 2 cups desiccated coconut, plus extra for rolling
  • 1 cup condensed milk
  • 1/2 tsp cardamom powder (elaichi powder)
  • 1 tbsp ghee
  • A few chopped pistachios (optional)

Method

1Add ghee and desiccated coconut to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and roast for 4 minutes until fragrant, not browned.
2Add the condensed milk and cardamom powder; continue on Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) for 6 minutes, stirring, until the mixture thickens and starts to pull away from the sides.
3Cool for 10 minutes until just warm enough to handle.
4Shape into small balls, roll in extra desiccated coconut, and top with a pistachio sliver.
5Cool completely before storing in an airtight container.

Tip: The mixture firms up a lot as it cools — shape the ladoos while still slightly warm.
