Chicken Karahi (Punjabi Wok-Style)

Chef Magic – Sauté Auto 24 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • High-Protein • Everyday

Ingredients

  • 500g chicken, curry-cut pieces
  • 4 tomatoes, chopped
  • 2 green chillies (hari mirch), slit
  • 1 tbsp ginger (adrak), julienned (plus extra to garnish)
  • 1 tbsp garlic (lehsun), finely chopped
  • 1 tsp coriander seeds (sookha dhania), crushed
  • 1 tsp red chilli (lal mirch) flakes
  • 3 tbsp oil or ghee
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tbsp kasuri methi (dried fenugreek (methi)), crushed
  • Salt to taste
  • Coriander leaves (hara dhania) to garnish

Method

1In Chef Magic, add oil; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and cook the garlic for 1 minute until fragrant, without browning.
2Add the chicken and sauté for 7 minutes on high (Speed 3–4) until lightly seared on the outside.
3Add the tomatoes, green chillies, crushed coriander seeds, chilli flakes and salt; cook on Sauté (Speed 2–4) for 10 minutes, mashing the tomatoes down, until the oil separates and the masala turns thick and dry-ish.
4Add half the ginger julienne and cook for 4 minutes more until the chicken is cooked through and the karahi is fairly dry, not gravy-like.
5Sprinkle garam masala and kasuri methi; toss for 1 minute.
6Garnish with the remaining ginger julienne and coriander leaves before serving.

Tip: Karahi is meant to be cooked hot and fast with no added water — let the tomatoes' own moisture do the work so the masala stays thick and clingy.
