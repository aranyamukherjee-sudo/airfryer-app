Paneer Lababdar

Chef Magic – Sauté Auto 24 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • Vegetarian • High-Protein • Everyday

Ingredients

  • 250g paneer, cubed
  • 2 onions, roughly chopped
  • 3 tomatoes, chopped
  • 12 cashews, soaked
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste (adrak-lehsun paste)
  • 1 tsp kashmiri chilli powder (mirch powder)
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp kasuri methi (dried fenugreek (methi)), crushed
  • 3 tbsp cream
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • Salt to taste

Method

1Add butter to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and cook the onions for 6 minutes until soft.
2Add tomatoes, cashews and ginger-garlic paste; cook for 8 minutes until the tomatoes break down completely.
3Select Whisk/Blend (Speed 8–10) to purée the mixture smooth, then return it to the jar.
4Select Sauté (Speed 1–2, ~100–110°C); stir in chilli powder, garam masala, sugar and salt, and simmer for 4 minutes.
5Add the paneer cubes, cream and crushed kasuri methi; sauté gently for 4 minutes until the gravy turns rich and glossy.

Tip: Lababdar should be thicker and richer than a standard makhani gravy — resist adding water; let the cashews and cream
