Spanish Tortilla (Potato Omelette)

Chef Magic (Cook potatoes) → Airfryer (Set the 170°C Sauté 15 min + Bake 15 min omelette) — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • Vegetarian • Spanish

🔥 Airfryer🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic🍽 Serves 4
IngredientsSteps

Ingredients

  • 3 potatoes, thinly sliced
  • 1 onion, thinly sliced
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 6 eggs
  • Salt & pepper to taste

Method

1Preheat: When you reach the Airfryer step, preheat it to 170°C for 4 minutes before adding the food.
2Add olive oil to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and cook the sliced potatoes and onion for 15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until soft (not browned).
3Whisk eggs with salt and pepper; fold in the cooked potatoes and onion.
4Pour the mixture into a greased oven-safe dish that fits the basket.
5Bake at 170°C for 15 minutes until just set in the centre. Rest 5 minutes before slicing.
