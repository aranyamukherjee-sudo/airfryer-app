Tandoori Soya Chaap

Airfryer – Grill 200°C 17 min — Serves 4

High-Protein • Vegetarian • Everyday

🔥 Airfryer🍽 Serves 4
IngredientsSteps

Prep ahead: Marinate the soya chaap for at least 1 hour (or overnight in the fridge) for the best flavour.

Ingredients

  • 500g soya chaap (stick or boneless), cut into pieces
  • 1/2 cup thick yogurt
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste (adrak-lehsun paste)
  • 1.5 tsp red chilli powder (lal mirch powder)
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp kasuri methi (dried fenugreek (methi)), crushed
  • 1 tbsp mustard oil (sarson ka tel)
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 200°C for 4 minutes before adding the food to the basket.
2Whisk together yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, chilli powder, garam masala, crushed kasuri methi, mustard oil, salt and lemon juice.
3Add the soya chaap pieces to the marinade, coat well, and refrigerate for at least 1 hour.
4Skewer or arrange the marinated chaap pieces in a single layer in the basket.
5Grill at 200°C for 17 minutes, turning once halfway, until charred at the edges.
6Serve hot with onion rings and mint chutney.
