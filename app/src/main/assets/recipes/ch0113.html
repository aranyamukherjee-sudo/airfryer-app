Shukto (Bengali Mixed Vegetable Stew)

Chef Magic – Sauté + Boil Auto 28 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • Vegetarian • Bengali • Gut-Friendly

🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic🍽 Serves 4
IngredientsStepsTips

Ingredients

  • 2 cups mixed vegetables (drumstick, potato, brinjal, bitter gourd, raw banana), chopped
  • 1 tbsp mustard-poppy seed paste (or plain mustard paste (sarson paste))
  • 1/2 tsp panch phoron (Bengali five-spice)
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 tsp ghee
  • 1 tbsp mustard oil (sarson ka tel)
  • Salt & sugar to taste

Method

1Add mustard oil and panch phoron to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) until fragrant.
2Add the vegetables and sauté 4 minutes.
3Add the mustard-poppy paste, milk, salt and a pinch of sugar; select Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C) and cook until vegetables are tender and the gravy thickens slightly.
4Finish with a drizzle of ghee before serving.

Tip: Shukto is traditionally eaten first with rice at the start of a Bengali meal — it’s meant to be mild and slightly bitter.
