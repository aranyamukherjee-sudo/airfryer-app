Vegetable Biryani

Airfryer or Chef Magic — pick whichever you have free — Prep 15 min + Cook 28 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • Vegetarian • Everyday

🔥 Airfryer🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic🍽 Serves 4
IngredientsSteps

Prep ahead: Soak basmati rice for 20 minutes before cooking for the fluffiest, most separate grains.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups basmati rice, soaked 20 min
  • 2 cups mixed vegetables, chopped
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 1/2 cup yogurt
  • 1 tbsp biryani masala
  • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste (adrak-lehsun paste)
  • 2 tbsp ghee
  • A few saffron (kesar) strands in warm milk
  • 2.5 cups water or stock
  • Salt to taste
  • Fried onions & coriander (dhania) to garnish

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 180°C for 4 minutes before adding the food.
2Par-cook vegetables with yogurt, biryani masala and ginger-garlic paste using Chef Magic’s Sauté for 9 minutes.
3Option A – Airfryer (layered): layer the par-cooked vegetables and soaked rice in an oven-safe dish, pour over hot water/stock, drizzle with saffron milk and ghee, cover tightly with foil, and bake at 180°C for 28 minutes until rice is fluffy; rest covered for 5 minutes before fluffing and garnishing.
4Option B – Chef Magic (one-pot): add ghee to the jar, select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and cook onions until golden, then add vegetables, yogurt, biryani masala and ginger-garlic paste, cooking 9 minutes; add soaked rice, water/stock, saffron milk and salt; select Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C) and cook until the water is absorbed and rice is fluffy, about 16 minutes; rest 5 minutes, then fluff and garnish with fried onions and coriander.
