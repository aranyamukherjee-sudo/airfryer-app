Rui Macher Jhol (Light Bengali Rohu Curry)

Chef Magic – Sauté + Boil Auto 22 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • High-Protein • Bengali • Everyday

Prep ahead: Rub the fish pieces with a little turmeric and salt and let them sit for 10 minutes before frying.

Ingredients

  • 6 pieces rohu (rui) fish
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric (haldi), plus extra for rubbing
  • 2 potatoes, cut into wedges
  • 1 tomato, chopped
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds (jeera)
  • 1/2 tsp nigella seeds (kalonji)
  • 1 tsp cumin (jeera)-coriander powder (dhania powder)
  • 1–2 green chillies (hari mirch), slit
  • 3 tbsp mustard oil (sarson ka tel)
  • Salt to taste
  • Coriander leaves (hara dhania) to garnish

Method

1Rub the fish pieces with turmeric and salt; let sit for 10 minutes.
2In Chef Magic, add mustard oil; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and shallow-fry the fish pieces for 2 minutes per side until lightly golden. Remove and set aside.
3In the same oil, crackle the cumin and nigella seeds for 30 seconds.
4Add the potato wedges and fry for 5 minutes until lightly golden at the edges.
5Add the tomato, turmeric, cumin-coriander powder, green chillies and salt; cook for 4 minutes until the tomato softens.
6Add 2 cups water; select Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C) and simmer covered for 8 minutes until the potatoes are nearly tender.
7Slide the fried fish back in and simmer for 4 minutes more, spooning the thin gravy over the top without stirring hard.
8Garnish with coriander leaves and serve with steamed rice.

Tip: Jhol is meant to be thin and brothy, not thick — this is the everyday, light version of Bengali fish curry, not a special-occasion one.
