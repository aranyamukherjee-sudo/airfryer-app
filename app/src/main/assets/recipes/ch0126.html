Malai Soya Chaap (Creamy)

Chef Magic – Sauté Auto 24 min — Serves 4

High-Protein • Vegetarian • Everyday

🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic🍽 Serves 4
IngredientsSteps

Ingredients

  • 500g soya chaap, cut into pieces
  • 1 onion, pureed
  • 10 cashews, soaked and ground to a paste
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste (adrak-lehsun paste)
  • 1/2 cup fresh cream
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 green chilli (hari mirch), chopped
  • Salt to taste

Method

1Add butter to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and cook the onion puree for 6 minutes until the raw smell disappears.
2Add ginger-garlic paste and the ground cashew paste; cook for 4 minutes.
3Add the soya chaap pieces and chopped green chilli; sauté for 10 minutes, stirring occasionally.
4Stir in cream, garam masala and salt; simmer on Sauté (Speed 1–2, ~100–110°C) for 4 minutes until the gravy thickens.
