Palak Paneer

Chef Magic – Boil + Whisk + Sauté Auto 21 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • Vegetarian • High-Protein • Everyday

Ingredients

  • 250g spinach, washed
  • 200g paneer, cubed
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 tomato, chopped
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste (adrak-lehsun paste)
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • 2 tbsp cream
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 green chilli (hari mirch)
  • Salt to taste

Method

1Add spinach and a splash of water to the jar; select Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C) for 2 minutes just until wilted. Select Whisk/Blend (Speed 8–10) with the green chilli to puree; set aside.
2Add butter to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and cook onion until golden, then add ginger-garlic paste and tomato, cooking until soft.
3Add garam masala and the spinach puree; simmer on Sauté (Speed 1–2, ~100–110°C) for 5 minutes.
4Gently fold in paneer cubes and cream; sauté 2 minutes just to warm through.
