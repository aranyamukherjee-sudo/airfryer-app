Classic Vanilla Cake

Airfryer – Bake 160°C 25 min — Serves 6

Vegetarian • Everyday

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 1/2 cup butter, softened
  • 3/4 cup milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 eggs (or see tip for eggless option)

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 160°C for 4 minutes before adding the food to the basket.
2Whisk the flour, baking powder and salt together in a bowl.
3In a separate bowl, cream the butter and sugar until light and fluffy, then beat in the eggs one at a time, followed by the vanilla.
4Fold in the dry ingredients alternately with the milk, mixing gently until just combined and smooth.
5Pour into a greased oven-safe pan that fits the basket.
6Bake at 160°C for 22–25 minutes, until a toothpick inserted in the centre comes out clean.
7Cool in the pan for 10 minutes before turning out onto a rack to cool completely.

Tip: For an eggless version, swap the eggs for 1/2 cup thick yogurt whisked with 1/4 tsp baking soda — the texture stays just as soft.
