Chicken Manchurian (Dry)

Airfryer (Fry the chicken) → Chef Magic (Toss in 200°C Fry 16 min + Toss 8 min sauce) — Serves 4

High-Protein • Everyday • Indo-Chinese

Prep ahead: Marinate the chicken pieces for at least 20 minutes for the best flavour.

Ingredients

  • 500g boneless chicken, cubed
  • 3 tbsp cornflour
  • 2 tbsp maida (plain flour)
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste (adrak-lehsun paste), divided
  • Salt to taste
  • Oil spray
  • 1 onion, cubed
  • 1 capsicum, cubed
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp chilli sauce
  • 1 tbsp tomato ketchup
  • 1 tbsp vinegar
  • 1/2 cup water mixed with 1 tsp cornflour
  • Spring onion greens to garnish

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 200°C for 4 minutes before adding the food.
2Toss the chicken with cornflour, maida, beaten egg, half the ginger-garlic paste and salt; rest 20 minutes.
3Spray generously with oil and arrange in a single layer in the Airfryer basket.
4Air-fry at 200°C for 16 minutes, shaking halfway, until golden and cooked through.
5In Chef Magic, add a little oil; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and cook onion and capsicum with the remaining ginger-garlic paste for 4 minutes.
6Add soy sauce, chilli sauce, ketchup, vinegar and the cornflour-water mix; cook 2 minutes until glossy and thickened.
7Toss in the fried chicken just before serving so it stays crisp; garnish with spring onion.
