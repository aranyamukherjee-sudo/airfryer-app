Egg Bhurji (Spiced Scrambled Eggs)

Chef Magic – Sauté Auto 14 min — Serves 3

Gluten-Free • Eggetarian • High-Protein • Everyday

Ingredients

  • 5 eggs
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 1 tomato, finely chopped
  • 1 green chilli (hari mirch), chopped
  • 1/4 tsp turmeric (haldi)
  • 1/2 tsp red chilli powder (lal mirch powder)
  • 1 tbsp oil or butter
  • Salt to taste
  • Chopped coriander (dhania) to garnish

Method

1Add oil to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and cook the onion and green chilli for 5 minutes until soft.
2Add tomato, turmeric and chilli powder; sauté 4 minutes until the tomato breaks down.
3Whisk the eggs with salt and pour into the jar; select Sauté (Speed 1–2, ~100–110°C) and cook, stirring gently, for 4 minutes until softly scrambled.
4Garnish with coriander and serve with toast or pav.

Tip: Keep the heat gentle once the eggs go in — Bhurji should stay soft and moist, not dry and rubbery.
