Chilli Oyster Mushroom (Dry)

Airfryer (Fry mushrooms) → Chef Magic (Sauté 130–160°C 4 min + Toss 2 min in sauce) — Serves 4

Vegan • Indo-Chinese • Party Snack

🔥 Airfryer🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic🍽 Serves 4
IngredientsStepsTips

Ingredients

  • 300g oyster mushrooms (dhingri), torn into thick strips
  • 3 tbsp cornflour
  • 2 tbsp maida (plain flour)
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce, divided
  • Oil spray
  • 1 capsicum, cubed
  • 1 onion, cubed
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste (adrak-lehsun paste)
  • 1 tbsp chilli sauce
  • 1 tbsp vinegar
  • 2 green chillies (hari mirch), slit
  • 1/2 cup water mixed with 1 tsp cornflour
  • Spring onion greens, to garnish

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 200°C for 4 minutes before adding the food.
2Toss the torn mushrooms with cornflour, maida and half the soy sauce until lightly and evenly coated.
3Spray with oil and arrange in a single layer in the Airfryer basket.
4Air-fry at 200°C for 10–12 minutes, shaking halfway, until crisp and lightly browned at the edges.
5In Chef Magic, add a little oil; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and cook capsicum, onion, ginger-garlic paste and green chillies for 4 minutes.
6Add remaining soy sauce, chilli sauce, vinegar and the cornflour-water mix; cook 2 minutes until thickened.
7Toss in the fried mushrooms just before serving; garnish with spring onion greens.

Tip: Oyster mushrooms have a meatier, stringier bite than button mushrooms and hold their shape better in a dry sauce — don't over-toss or they'll shred.
