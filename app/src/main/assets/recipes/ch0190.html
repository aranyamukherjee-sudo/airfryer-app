Kimchi Pancake (Kimchi Jeon)

Chef Magic – Pan-Fry 16 min — Serves 3

Vegetarian • Korean • Everyday

🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic🍽 Serves 3
IngredientsStepsTips

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 tbsp rice flour (for extra crispness)
  • 1 cup kimchi, chopped
  • 3 tbsp kimchi juice
  • 3/4 cup water
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • Neutral oil, for pan-frying
  • Soy-vinegar dipping sauce (soy sauce, rice vinegar and a little sesame oil), to serve

Method

1Whisk together the flour, rice flour, kimchi juice, water and sugar into a smooth batter.
2Fold in the chopped kimchi and spring onions.
3Heat a generous layer of oil in a pan; select Sauté (Speed 2–3, ~150–160°C).
4Pour in a ladleful of batter, spreading it thin, and cook for 3–4 minutes until the underside is golden and crisp.
5Flip carefully and cook the other side for 3–4 minutes, pressing down gently for even crisping.
6Cut into wedges and serve hot with the dipping sauce.

Tip: A thinner pancake crisps up far better than a thick one — resist the urge to pile the batter high in the pan.
