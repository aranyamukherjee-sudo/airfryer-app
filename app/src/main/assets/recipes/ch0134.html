Paneer Pasanda

Chef Magic – Sauté Auto 26 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • Vegetarian • High-Protein • Mughlai

Prep ahead: Slice the paneer into thin, flat pieces rather than cubes — this is what gives Pasanda its signature look.

Ingredients

  • 300g paneer, cut into thin flat slices
  • 2 tbsp chopped nuts & raisins, for the stuffing (optional)
  • 1 onion, roughly chopped
  • 12 cashews, soaked
  • 1 tomato, chopped
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste (adrak-lehsun paste)
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • 1/4 tsp white pepper (safed mirch)
  • 3 tbsp cream
  • 2 tbsp ghee
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • Salt to taste

Method

1If stuffing, sandwich a little chopped nuts and raisins between two thin paneer slices; set aside.
2Add ghee to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and cook the onion for 5 minutes until soft.
3Add soaked cashews, tomato and ginger-garlic paste; cook for 6 minutes, then select Whisk/Blend (Speed 8–10) to purée smooth.
4Return to Sauté (Speed 1–2, ~100–110°C); add garam masala, white pepper, sugar and salt, and simmer for 6 minutes.
5Gently slide in the paneer slices and cream; sauté 5 minutes just to warm through, spooning gravy over the top instead of stirring hard so the slices stay intact.
