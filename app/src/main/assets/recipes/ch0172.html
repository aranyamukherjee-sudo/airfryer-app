Mushroom Risotto

Chef Magic – Sauté + Boil Auto 26 min — Serves 3

Gluten-Free • Vegetarian • Italian

Ingredients

  • 1 cup arborio (risotto) rice
  • 200g mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 3 cups hot vegetable stock
  • 1/2 cup white wine or extra stock
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • Salt & pepper to taste

Method

1Add butter to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and cook the onion and mushrooms for 6 minutes until softened.
2Add garlic and rice; sauté 2 minutes until the rice grains turn glossy.
3Add wine, then gradually add hot stock; select Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C) and cook, stirring occasionally, for 16 minutes until rice is creamy and tender.
4Stir in remaining butter, Parmesan, salt and pepper before serving.
