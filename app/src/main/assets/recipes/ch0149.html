Kothimbir Vadi

Chef Magic (Steam) → Airfryer (Fry, for a crisp finish) 190°C — Steam 16 min + Fry 8 min — makes ~16

Gluten-Free • Vegan • Maharashtrian

🔥 Airfryer🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic
IngredientsSteps

Ingredients

  • 2 cups besan (gram flour (besan))
  • 1 cup chopped coriander leaves (hara dhania)
  • 1 tsp ginger (adrak)-green chilli (hari mirch) paste
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric (haldi)
  • 1 tsp sesame seeds
  • 1 tbsp oil, plus extra for frying
  • Salt to taste
  • Water as needed

Method

1Preheat: When you reach the Airfryer step, preheat it to 190°C for 4 minutes before adding the food.
2Mix besan, coriander, ginger-chilli paste, turmeric, sesame seeds, oil and salt with just enough water to form a thick, spreadable batter.
3Spread into a greased dish that fits the steamer tray, about 2cm thick.
4Steam in Chef Magic (100°C steam, paddle off) for 16 minutes until firm and a toothpick comes out clean; cool and cut into small squares or diamonds.
5Spray the pieces with oil and air-fry at 190°C for 8 minutes until golden and crisp.
