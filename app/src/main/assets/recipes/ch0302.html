Peri Peri Sauce

Chef Magic – Whisk/Blend 5 min — Makes ~1 cup

Gluten-Free • Vegetarian • Homemade

Ingredients

  • 4–5 dried red chillies (lal mirch), soaked in warm water
  • 2 fresh red chillies (lal mirch)
  • 4 cloves garlic (lehsun)
  • 1 tbsp paprika
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder (lal mirch powder)
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp vinegar
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • Salt to taste

Method

1Drain the soaked dried chillies and add to the jar along with the fresh chillies, garlic, paprika, chilli powder, lemon juice, vinegar, sugar and salt.
2Select Whisk/Blend (Speed 8–10) and blend until smooth, scraping down the sides as needed.
3With the machine running on Whisk/Blend (Speed 6–8), slowly drizzle in the olive oil to emulsify the sauce.
4Transfer to a clean bottle and refrigerate for up to 2 weeks; the flavour deepens after a day.

Tip: This sauce doubles as a fantastic marinade — toss chicken or paneer in it for at least an hour before air-frying.
