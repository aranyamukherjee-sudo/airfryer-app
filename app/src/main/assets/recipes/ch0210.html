Shakshuka (Baked Eggs in Tomato-Pepper Sauce)

Chef Magic (Make sauce) → Airfryer 180°C Bake 10 min — Serves 2

Gluten-Free • Eggetarian • Everyday

Ingredients

  • 4 eggs
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 red capsicum, chopped
  • 3 tomatoes, chopped (or 1 cup crushed tomatoes)
  • 2 cloves garlic (lehsun), minced
  • 1/2 tsp cumin powder (jeera powder)
  • 1/2 tsp paprika
  • Pinch of chilli flakes
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Coriander (dhania) or parsley to garnish

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 180°C for 4 minutes before adding the food.
2In Chef Magic, add olive oil; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and cook the onion and capsicum for 6 minutes until softened.
3Add garlic, cumin, paprika and chilli flakes; sauté for 1 minute until fragrant.
4Add the tomatoes and salt; simmer on Sauté (Speed 1–2) for 8 minutes until thickened into a chunky sauce.
5Pour the sauce into an Airfryer-safe dish, make small wells in the sauce and crack an egg into each well.
6Bake at 180°C for 10 minutes, until the whites are just set and the yolks are still soft.
7Garnish with coriander or parsley and serve straight from the dish with crusty bread.

Tip: Pull it out while the yolks still jiggle slightly — they'll keep firming up from residual heat for a minute after baking.
