Dragon Paneer (Dry)

Airfryer (Fry the paneer) → Chef Magic (Toss in 200°C Fry 14 min + Toss 8 min sauce) — Serves 4

Vegetarian • Everyday • Indo-Chinese

🔥 Airfryer🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic🍽 Serves 4
IngredientsStepsTips

Ingredients

  • 250g paneer, cut into batons
  • 3 tbsp cornflour
  • 2 tbsp maida (plain flour)
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce, divided
  • Oil spray
  • 1 capsicum, julienned
  • 1 onion, julienned
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste (adrak-lehsun paste)
  • 2 tbsp chilli-garlic (lehsun) sauce
  • 1 tbsp tomato ketchup
  • 1 tsp vinegar
  • 1/2 tsp red chilli (lal mirch) flakes
  • Roasted cashews, roughly crushed, to garnish

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 200°C for 4 minutes before adding the food.
2Toss the paneer batons with cornflour, maida and half the soy sauce until coated.
3Spray with oil and arrange in a single layer in the Airfryer basket.
4Air-fry at 200°C for 14 minutes, shaking halfway, until golden and crisp.
5In Chef Magic, add a little oil; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and cook capsicum, onion and ginger-garlic paste for 4 minutes, keeping the vegetables crisp.
6Add remaining soy sauce, chilli-garlic sauce, ketchup, vinegar and chilli flakes; cook 2 minutes until glossy.
7Toss in the fried paneer just before serving; scatter with crushed cashews.

Tip: Dragon-style dishes lean spicier and drier than Manchurian, with no cornflour slurry thickening the sauce — keep the glaze
