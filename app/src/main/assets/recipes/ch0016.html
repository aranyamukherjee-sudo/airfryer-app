Cheese Corn Nuggets

Airfryer – Fry 190°C 12 min — Makes ~14

Vegetarian • Everyday

🔥 Airfryer
IngredientsStepsTips

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups boiled sweet corn, roughly mashed
  • 1/2 cup grated cheese
  • 3 tbsp cornflour
  • 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 cup breadcrumbs
  • Oil spray

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 190°C for 4 minutes before adding the food to the basket.
2Mix the corn, cheese, cornflour, flour, pepper and salt into a thick, shapeable mixture.
3Shape into small nugget-sized ovals.
4Roll each nugget in breadcrumbs, pressing to coat evenly.
5Spray lightly with oil and arrange in the basket, spaced apart.
6Air-fry at 190°C for 10–12 minutes, turning halfway, until golden and crisp.

Tip: If the mixture feels too soft to shape, chill it in the fridge for 15 minutes first — it firms up and holds together much better.
