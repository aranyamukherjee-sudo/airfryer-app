Pyaz Tamatar ki Sabzi (Onion-Tomato Sabzi)

Chef Magic – Sauté Auto 16 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • Vegan • Everyday • Low-Oil

Ingredients

  • 3 onions, sliced
  • 3 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds (jeera)
  • 1/4 tsp turmeric (haldi)
  • 1/2 tsp red chilli powder (lal mirch powder)
  • 1/2 tsp coriander powder (dhania powder)
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt to taste
  • Chopped coriander (dhania) to garnish

Method

1Add oil and cumin seeds to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) until the seeds splutter.
2Add the sliced onions and cook for 7 minutes until soft and lightly golden.
3Add tomatoes, turmeric, chilli powder and coriander powder; continue on Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) for 7 minutes until the tomatoes fully break down.
4Season with salt and garnish with coriander; serve with roti or dal-rice.

Tip: One of the simplest everyday sabzis — barely needs a shopping list beyond onions and tomatoes.
