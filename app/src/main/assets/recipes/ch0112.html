Karela Fry (Bitter Gourd Fry)

Airfryer or Chef Magic — pick whichever you have free — 22 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • Vegan • Bengali

Prep ahead: Salt sliced karela and rest 18 minutes, then squeeze out the bitter liquid before cooking.

Ingredients

  • 3 karela (bitter gourd), thinly sliced
  • 1 tsp salt (for resting)
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric (haldi)
  • 1/2 tsp red chilli powder (lal mirch powder)
  • 1 tbsp mustard oil (or any oil)
  • Salt to taste (for cooking)

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 190°C for 4 minutes before adding the food.
2Option A – Airfryer: squeeze salted karela dry, toss with oil, turmeric and chilli powder, spread in a single layer in the basket, and air-fry at 190°C for 16 minutes, shaking every 5 minutes, until deep brown and crisp.
3Option B – Chef Magic: add oil to the jar, select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C), add squeezed karela, turmeric and chilli powder, and cook, stirring occasionally, for 16 minutes until deep brown and crisp, uncovered so moisture escapes.

Tip: Salting and squeezing out the liquid is what cuts the bitterness — don’t skip this step.
