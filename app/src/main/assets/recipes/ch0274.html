Dragon Chicken

Airfryer (Fry the chicken) → Chef Magic (Toss in 200°C Fry 16 min + Toss 8 min sauce) — Serves 4

High-Protein • Everyday • Indo-Chinese

Prep ahead: Marinate the chicken pieces for at least 20 minutes for the best flavour.

Ingredients

  • 500g boneless chicken, cut into thin strips
  • 3 tbsp cornflour
  • 2 tbsp maida (plain flour)
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • Salt to taste
  • Oil spray
  • 1 capsicum, julienned
  • 1 onion, julienned
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste (adrak-lehsun paste)
  • 2 tbsp chilli-garlic (lehsun) sauce
  • 1 tbsp tomato ketchup
  • 1 tsp vinegar
  • 1/2 tsp red chilli (lal mirch) flakes
  • Roasted cashews, roughly crushed, to garnish

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 200°C for 4 minutes before adding the food.
2Toss the chicken strips with cornflour, maida, beaten egg and salt; rest 20 minutes.
3Spray with oil and arrange in a single layer in the Airfryer basket.
4Air-fry at 200°C for 16 minutes, shaking halfway, until golden and cooked through.
5In Chef Magic, add a little oil; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and cook capsicum, onion and ginger-garlic paste for 4 minutes, keeping the vegetables crisp.
6Add chilli-garlic sauce, ketchup, vinegar and chilli flakes; cook 2 minutes until glossy.
7Toss in the fried chicken just before serving; scatter with crushed cashews.

Tip: Cutting the chicken into thin strips rather than cubes is what gives Dragon Chicken its characteristic long, crispy shred.
