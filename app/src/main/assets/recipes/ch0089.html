Patta Gobi Sabzi (Simple Cabbage Stir-Fry)

Chef Magic – Sauté Auto 14 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • Vegan • Everyday • Low-Oil

🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic🍽 Serves 4
IngredientsStepsTips

Ingredients

  • 3 cups shredded cabbage
  • 1/4 cup grated carrot
  • 1 tsp mustard seeds (rai)
  • 1/2 tsp cumin seeds (jeera)
  • 1/4 tsp turmeric (haldi)
  • 1 green chilli (hari mirch), chopped
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 tbsp grated fresh coconut (optional)

Method

1Add oil, mustard seeds and cumin seeds to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) until the seeds splutter.
2Add the green chilli and turmeric; sauté 30 seconds.
3Add the shredded cabbage, carrot and salt; continue on Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) for 10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until just tender but still with a little crunch.
4Toss with fresh coconut if using, and serve warm.

Tip: Don’t overcook the cabbage — a few minutes past tender-crisp and it turns watery and loses its everyday appeal.
