Mint-Coriander Chutney

Chef Magic – Whisk/Blend — 6 min makes ~1 cup

Gluten-Free • Vegan • No-Cook

🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic
IngredientsSteps

Ingredients

  • 1 cup mint leaves
  • 1 cup coriander leaves (hara dhania)
  • 1 green chilli (hari mirch)
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1/2 tsp roasted cumin powder (bhuna jeera powder)
  • 1 tbsp yogurt (optional, for a milder version)
  • Salt to taste
  • Water as needed

Method

1Add all ingredients to Chef Magic’s jar with a splash of water.
2Select Whisk/Blend (Speed 8–10) and run until smooth, adding a little more water if needed to loosen.
3Serve alongside any air-fried snack, tikka or kebab.
