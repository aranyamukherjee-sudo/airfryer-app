Korean Chicken Bulgogi

Chef Magic (Marinade) → Airfryer 200°C Marinate 1 hr + Fry 16 min — Serves 4

High-Protein • Korean • Everyday

Ingredients

  • 600g boneless chicken thighs, thinly sliced
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 3 tbsp brown sugar
  • 2 tbsp sesame oil
  • 1 Asian pear or apple, grated
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 2 spring onions, cut into batons
  • Toasted sesame seeds, to garnish

Method

1In the jar, combine the soy sauce, brown sugar, sesame oil, grated pear, garlic and ginger; select Whisk/Blend (Speed 4–6) and blend into a smooth marinade.
2Toss the chicken and onion in the marinade, cover, and refrigerate for at least 1 hour, or overnight for best flavour.
3Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 200°C for 4 minutes before adding the food to the basket.
4Arrange the marinated chicken and onion in the basket in a single layer.
5Air-fry at 200°C for 14–16 minutes, shaking halfway, until caramelised and cooked through.
6Garnish with spring onions and sesame seeds; serve with steamed rice and lettuce wraps.

Tip: Slicing the chicken thinly, against the grain, helps it soak up the marinade faster and cook quickly and evenly.
