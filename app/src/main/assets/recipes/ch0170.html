Honey Chilli Cauliflower

Airfryer (Fry the florets) → Chef Magic (Toss in 200°C Fry 15 min + Toss 6 min glaze) — Serves 4

Vegetarian • Everyday • Indo-Chinese

Ingredients

  • 1 medium cauliflower, cut into florets
  • 3 tbsp cornflour
  • 2 tbsp maida (plain flour)
  • Salt to taste
  • Oil spray
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste (adrak-lehsun paste)
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp chilli sauce
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp toasted sesame seeds
  • Spring onion greens to garnish

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 200°C for 4 minutes before adding the food.
2Toss the cauliflower florets with cornflour, maida, salt and a splash of water to form a light coating.
3Spray generously with oil and arrange in a single layer in the Airfryer basket.
4Air-fry at 200°C for 15 minutes, shaking halfway, until golden and crisp.
5In Chef Magic, add a little oil; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and cook the ginger-garlic paste for 1 minute.
6Add soy sauce, chilli sauce and honey; simmer on Sauté (Speed 1–2, ~100–110°C) for 2 minutes until it turns into a light, sticky glaze.
7Toss in the crispy florets just before serving; sprinkle with sesame seeds and spring onion.

Tip: This is the sweeter, honey-glazed cousin of Gobi Manchurian elsewhere in this chapter — less tangy, more caramelised.
