Chicken Alfredo Pasta

Airfryer (Grill chicken) → Chef Magic (Boil pasta + Make sauce) 190°C Grill 14 min + Boil 11 min + Sauce 8 min — Serves 3

High-Protein • Everyday • Italian

🔥 Airfryer🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic🍽 Serves 3
IngredientsStepsTips

Prep ahead: Slice the chicken only after it rests for a couple of minutes off the heat — it stays juicier and slices more cleanly.

Ingredients

  • 2 boneless chicken breasts
  • 1 tbsp olive oil, plus extra for the pasta water
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • Salt to taste
  • 250g fettuccine or penne
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 3 cloves garlic, finely chopped
  • 1 cup fresh cream
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan, plus extra to serve
  • 1/4 tsp nutmeg (optional)
  • Chopped parsley, to garnish

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 190°C for 4 minutes before adding the food.
2Rub the chicken breasts with olive oil, black pepper and salt; air-fry at 190°C for 14 minutes, flipping halfway, until cooked through. Rest, then slice.
3Meanwhile, add water and a pinch of salt to the Chef Magic jar; select Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C) and cook the pasta until al dente, about 11 minutes; drain and set aside.
4Add butter to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and cook the garlic for 1 minute until fragrant, not browned.
5Add the cream, Parmesan and nutmeg if using; simmer on Sauté (Speed 1–2, ~100–110°C) for 6 minutes until the sauce thickens slightly.
6Add the drained pasta to the sauce and toss for 2 minutes until well coated. Top with the sliced chicken, extra Parmesan and parsley; serve hot.

Tip: The sauce will look thin at first but keeps thickening off the heat as the Parmesan sets — don't over-reduce it in the jar.
