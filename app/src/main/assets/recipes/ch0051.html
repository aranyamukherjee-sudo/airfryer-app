Air Fryer Gulab Jamun

Chef Magic (Knead & syrup) → Airfryer (Fry) 180°C Knead 5 min + Fry 12 min makes ~12

Prep ahead: Rest the dough for 10 minutes before shaping so the khoya hydrates and the balls don’t crack.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup milk powder (khoya substitute)
  • 3 tbsp flour
  • 1/4 tsp baking soda
  • 2 tbsp milk
  • 2 tbsp ghee, melted, plus extra for brushing
  • 1.5 cups sugar
  • 1.5 cups water
  • 3–4 green cardamom (elaichi) pods, crushed
  • A few saffron (kesar) strands (optional)

Method

1Preheat: When you reach the Airfryer step, preheat it to 180°C for 4 minutes before adding the food.
2In Chef Magic, add milk powder, flour and baking soda; select Knead (Speed 2–3, no heat), adding milk and melted ghee gradually until a smooth, crack-free dough forms. Rest 10 minutes.
3Meanwhile, add sugar, water and crushed cardamom to a clean jar; select Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C) and simmer for 8 minutes until a light, one-string syrup forms. Keep warm.
4Divide the dough into 12 smooth balls, brushing your palms with ghee to prevent cracking.
5Brush the balls generously with ghee and arrange in the basket, spaced apart.
6Air-fry at 180°C for 12 minutes, turning every 4 minutes, until deep golden-brown all over.
7Drop the hot fried balls straight into the warm syrup and soak for at least 30 minutes before serving.

Tip: A crack in the dough ball means it will split open in the syrup — smooth every ball fully before frying.
