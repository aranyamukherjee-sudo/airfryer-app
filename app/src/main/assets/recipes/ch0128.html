Soya Chunks (Nutrela) Curry

Chef Magic – Boil + Sauté Auto 26 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • High-Protein • Vegetarian • Everyday

Prep ahead: Soak dried soya chunks in hot water for 15 minutes, then squeeze out the water before cooking.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups dried soya chunks
  • 2 onions, chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, pureed
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste (adrak-lehsun paste)
  • 1.5 tsp curry powder or garam masala
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric (haldi)
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 cup water
  • Salt to taste
  • Coriander (dhania) to garnish

Method

1Soak soya chunks in hot water for 15 minutes; squeeze out excess water thoroughly.
2Add oil to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and cook onions for 6 minutes until golden.
3Add ginger-garlic paste, tomatoes, turmeric and curry powder; sauté for 7 minutes until the oil separates.
4Add the soaked soya chunks, water and salt; select Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C) and cook for 9 minutes until the gravy thickens. Garnish with coriander.
