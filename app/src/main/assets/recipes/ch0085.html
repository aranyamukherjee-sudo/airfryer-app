Baked Vegetable & Egg Muffins (High-Protein)

Airfryer – Bake 160°C 13 min makes 6

Gluten-Free • High-Protein • Low-Carb • Everyday

Ingredients

  • 6 eggs
  • 1/4 cup chopped spinach
  • 1/4 cup chopped bell pepper
  • 2 tbsp grated cheese
  • Salt & pepper to taste
  • Oil spray for the cups

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 160°C for 4 minutes before adding the food to the basket.
2Whisk eggs with salt and pepper; stir in spinach, bell pepper and cheese.
3Pour into greased muffin cups that fit the basket.
4Bake at 160°C for 13 minutes until set and lightly golden.
5Cool slightly before removing; refrigerate leftovers for up to 3 days.
