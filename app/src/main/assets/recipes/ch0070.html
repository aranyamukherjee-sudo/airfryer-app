Soft Dinner Rolls

Chef Magic (Knead) → Airfryer (Bake) 180°C — Knead 5 min + Rest 45 min + Bake 14 min — makes 8 rolls

🔥 Airfryer🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic
IngredientsSteps

Ingredients

  • 2.5 cups flour
  • 1 tsp instant yeast
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 3/4 cup warm milk (105–110°F / 40–43°C)
  • 2 tbsp melted butter
  • 1 egg
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • Extra butter for brushing

Method

1Preheat: When you reach the Airfryer step, preheat it to 180°C for 4 minutes before adding the food.
2In Chef Magic, mix warm milk (105–110°F / 40–43°C — warm to the touch, not hot), yeast and sugar; rest 5–10 minutes until frothy.
3Add flour, melted butter, egg and salt; select Knead (Speed 2–3, no heat) until a smooth, soft dough forms.
4Shape into 8 balls, place in a greased pan that fits the basket, and rest covered for 45 minutes until puffy.
5Bake in the Airfryer at 180°C for 14 minutes until golden; brush with butter while warm.
