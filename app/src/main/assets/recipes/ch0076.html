Paneer 65

Airfryer or Chef Magic — pick whichever you have free — Marinate 30 min + Cook 14 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • Vegetarian • High-Protein • Everyday

Prep ahead: Marinate the paneer for at least 30 minutes for the best flavour.

Ingredients

  • 400g paneer, cubed
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste (adrak-lehsun paste)
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder (lal mirch powder)
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric (haldi)
  • 2 tbsp curd
  • 2 tbsp cornflour
  • 1 tbsp rice flour
  • Salt to taste
  • Curry leaves (kadi patta) & 1 green chilli (hari mirch), slit, for tempering
  • 1 tbsp oil

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 200°C for 4 minutes before adding the food.
2Marinate paneer with ginger-garlic paste, spices, curd, cornflour and rice flour for 30 minutes.
3Option A – Airfryer: spray with oil, arrange in the basket in a single layer, air-fry at 200°C for 12 minutes, shaking halfway, until crisp and golden; toss with a quick curry leaf tempering before serving.
4Option B – Chef Magic: add oil to the jar, select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C), add curry leaves and green chilli briefly, then add marinated paneer and cook, stirring gently, for 10 minutes until crisp outside.
