Korean Gochujang Chicken Wings

Airfryer + Chef Magic – Fry 25 min + Sauce 2 min — Serves 4

High-Protein • Korean • Everyday

🔥 Airfryer🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic🍽 Serves 4
IngredientsStepsTips

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken wings
  • 1 tbsp baking powder (for extra crispness)
  • Salt to taste
  • 3 tbsp gochujang
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • Toasted sesame seeds and sliced spring onion, to garnish

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 200°C for 4 minutes before adding the food to the basket.
2Pat the wings completely dry, then toss with the baking powder and salt.
3Arrange in the basket in a single layer, without overcrowding.
4Air-fry at 200°C for 22–25 minutes, shaking every 8 minutes, until deeply golden and crisp.
5Meanwhile, combine the gochujang, honey, soy sauce, rice vinegar, garlic and sesame oil in the jar; select Sauté (Speed 1, ~90–100°C) and warm for 2 minutes.
6Toss the hot wings in the sauce, garnish with sesame seeds and spring onion, and serve immediately.

Tip: The baking powder coating (not baking soda) is the secret to shatter-crisp wing skin in the airfryer, without any deep frying.
