Sarson ka Saag

Chef Magic – Boil then Sauté Auto 32 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • Vegan • Punjabi • High-Fibre

Ingredients

  • 3 cups mustard greens (sarson), chopped
  • 1 cup spinach, chopped
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 1 tomato, chopped
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste (adrak-lehsun paste)
  • 2 tbsp cornmeal (makki atta)
  • 2 tbsp ghee
  • 1 green chilli (hari mirch)
  • Salt to taste

Method

1Add mustard greens, spinach and a splash of water to the jar; select Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C) and cook for 12 minutes until fully wilted and soft.
2Select Whisk/Blend (Speed 8–10) briefly to mash coarsely (not fully smooth); set aside.
3Add ghee to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and cook onion, tomato, ginger-garlic paste and green chilli for 8 minutes.
4Stir the mashed greens back in with cornmeal and salt; simmer on Sauté (Speed 1–2, ~100–110°C) for 8 minutes until thick. Top with a spoon of ghee before serving.
