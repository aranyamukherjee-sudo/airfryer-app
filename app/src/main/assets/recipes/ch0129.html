Soya Keema (Minced Soya Curry)

Chef Magic – Sauté Auto 19 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • High-Protein • Vegan • Everyday

Prep ahead: Soak soya granules (keema) in hot water for 10 minutes, then drain and squeeze dry.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups soya granules (keema)
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 1 tomato, finely chopped
  • 1/2 cup peas
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste (adrak-lehsun paste)
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric (haldi)
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt to taste
  • Coriander (dhania) to garnish

Method

1Soak soya granules in hot water for 10 minutes; drain and squeeze out excess water.
2Add oil to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and cook onion for 4 minutes until golden.
3Add ginger-garlic paste, tomato, turmeric and garam masala; sauté for 6 minutes.
4Add the soaked soya granules and peas; sauté for 9 minutes, stirring occasionally, until dry and well combined. Garnish with coriander.
