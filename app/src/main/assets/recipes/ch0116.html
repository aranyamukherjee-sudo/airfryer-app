Begun Bhaja (Crispy Fried Eggplant Slices)

Airfryer or Chef Magic — pick whichever you have free — 16 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • Vegan • Bengali

Prep ahead: Salt eggplant slices and rest 10 minutes to draw out excess moisture, then pat dry.

Ingredients

  • 1 large eggplant (brinjal), sliced into rounds
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric (haldi)
  • 1/2 tsp red chilli powder (lal mirch powder)
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 tbsp mustard oil (or rice flour for extra crispness)

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 190°C for 4 minutes before adding the food.
2Option A – Airfryer: toss eggplant slices with turmeric, chilli powder, salt and oil (coat lightly in rice flour if using), arrange in a single layer in the basket, and air-fry at 190°C for 14 minutes, flipping halfway, until deep golden and crisp at the edges.
3Option B – Chef Magic: add oil to the jar, select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C), add coated eggplant slices, and cook, turning occasionally, for 11 minutes until golden-brown on both sides.
