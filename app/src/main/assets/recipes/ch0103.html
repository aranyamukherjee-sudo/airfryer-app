Baingan Bharta (Smoky Roasted Eggplant Mash)

Airfryer or Chef Magic — pick whichever you have free — Roast 22 min + Cook 14 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • Vegan • Everyday

🔥 Airfryer🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic🍽 Serves 4
IngredientsStepsTips

Ingredients

  • 1 large eggplant (brinjal)
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 tomato, chopped
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste (adrak-lehsun paste)
  • 1/2 tsp red chilli powder (lal mirch powder)
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric (haldi)
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 green chilli (hari mirch), chopped
  • Salt to taste
  • Coriander (dhania) to garnish

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 200°C for 4 minutes before adding the food.
2Airfryer: prick the eggplant a few times with a fork, place in the basket, and roast at 200°C for 22 minutes, turning halfway, until the skin is charred and the flesh is soft. Cool slightly, peel off the charred skin, and mash the flesh.
3Chef Magic: add oil to the jar, select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C), and cook onions until golden.
4Add ginger-garlic paste, tomato, green chilli, turmeric and chilli powder; sauté until the oil separates.
5Fold in the mashed roasted eggplant and salt; sauté for 9 minutes until well combined. Garnish with coriander.

Tip: The charred skin is where the smokiness comes from — don’t skip the roasting step even if you’re short on time.
