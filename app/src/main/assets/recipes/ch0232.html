Chicken Cutlets (Crispy Airfryer Croquettes)

Chef Magic (Mince & mix) → Airfryer 200°C 16 min — makes 10

High-Protein • Everyday

🔥 Airfryer🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic
IngredientsStepsTips

Prep ahead: Chill the shaped cutlets for 15 minutes before air-frying so they hold together.

Ingredients

  • 250g boneless chicken, minced or finely chopped
  • 1 potato, boiled and mashed
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste (adrak-lehsun paste)
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • 1/2 tsp red chilli powder (lal mirch powder)
  • 2 tbsp coriander leaves (hara dhania), chopped
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 1 cup breadcrumbs
  • Salt to taste
  • Oil spray

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 200°C for 4 minutes before adding the food.
2In Chef Magic, pulse the chicken briefly to mince if using whole pieces, then transfer to a bowl.
3Mix the minced chicken with mashed potato, onion, ginger-garlic paste, garam masala, chilli powder, coriander and salt.
4Shape the mixture into small flat patties; chill for 15 minutes.
5Dip each patty in beaten egg, then coat in breadcrumbs, pressing gently to stick.
6Spray generously with oil and arrange in a single layer in the Airfryer basket.
7Air-fry at 200°C for 16 minutes, flipping halfway, until golden and crisp on both sides.

Tip: Chilling the shaped patties is the step people skip — don't; it's what stops them falling apart in the basket.
