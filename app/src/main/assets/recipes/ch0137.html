Paneer Korma

Chef Magic – Sauté Auto 24 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • Vegetarian • High-Protein • Mughlai

Ingredients

  • 250g paneer, cubed
  • 1 onion, roughly chopped
  • 1/4 cup cashews, soaked
  • 2 tbsp yogurt, whisked
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste (adrak-lehsun paste)
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • 1/4 tsp white pepper (safed mirch)
  • 2 green cardamom (elaichi), 1 cinnamon stick (dalchini)
  • 2 tbsp ghee
  • 1/2 cup warm milk
  • Salt to taste
  • Fried onions & coriander (dhania) to garnish

Method

1Add ghee, cardamom and cinnamon to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) for 1 minute until fragrant.
2Add the onion and cook for 6 minutes until soft and lightly golden.
3Add cashews and ginger-garlic paste; cook 4 minutes, then select Whisk/Blend (Speed 8–10) with a splash of milk to purée smooth.
4Return to Sauté (Speed 1–2, ~100–110°C); whisk in the yogurt off the direct heat edge, then stir in garam masala, white pepper and salt, simmering for 5 minutes.
5Add the paneer and remaining warm milk; sauté gently for 4 minutes until the gravy is pale, rich and creamy. Garnish with fried onions and coriander.

Tip: Add yogurt only once the pan has come off high heat, stirring constantly — this is what stops a korma gravy from splitting.
