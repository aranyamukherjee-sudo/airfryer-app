Stuffed Kachori

Chef Magic (Knead) → Airfryer (Fry) 190°C Knead 5 min + Fry 16 min makes ~10

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 3 tbsp oil (for dough)
  • Water as needed
  • 1 cup boiled moong dal, mashed
  • 1 tsp fennel seeds (saunf)
  • 1/2 tsp red chilli powder (lal mirch powder)
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • Salt to taste
  • Oil spray

Method

1Preheat: When you reach the Airfryer step, preheat it to 190°C for 4 minutes before adding the food.
2In Chef Magic, add flour, oil and a pinch of salt; select Knead (Speed 2–3, no heat), adding water gradually until a firm dough forms. Rest 15 minutes.
3Mix mashed moong dal with fennel, chilli powder, garam masala and salt for the filling.
4Roll dough into small discs, stuff with filling, and seal into balls; flatten slightly.
5Spray lightly with oil and air-fry in the Airfryer at 190°C for 16 minutes, turning once, until golden and flaky.
