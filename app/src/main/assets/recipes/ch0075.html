Vegetable Pakoras

Airfryer or Chef Magic — pick whichever you have free — 19 min — Serves 4

🔥 Airfryer🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic🍽 Serves 4
IngredientsStepsTips

Ingredients

  • 2 cups mixed vegetables (onion, potato, spinach), thinly sliced
  • 1 cup besan (gram flour (besan))
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric (haldi)
  • 1/2 tsp red chilli powder (lal mirch powder)
  • 1/4 tsp ajwain (carom seeds (ajwain))
  • Salt to taste
  • Water as needed
  • Oil (for both methods)

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 190°C for 4 minutes before adding the food.
2Mix vegetables with besan, spices, salt and just enough water to bind into a thick batter-coating.
3Option A – Airfryer: shape into small fritters, spray generously with oil, arrange in the basket, and air-fry at 190°C for
4minutes, shaking halfway, until golden and crisp.
5Option B – Chef Magic: add 4 tbsp oil to the jar, select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C), and drop spoonfuls of batter into the hot jar, turning occasionally, for 11 minutes until golden and crisp on all sides.

Tip: The Airfryer version uses roughly a third of the oil of the traditional Chef Magic sauté version.
