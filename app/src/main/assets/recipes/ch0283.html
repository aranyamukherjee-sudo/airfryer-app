Shepherd’s Pie

Chef Magic (Cook filling & mash) → Airfryer 190°C Sauté 18 min + Bake 20 min (Bake topping) — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • High-Protein • British

Ingredients

  • 500g minced lamb or mutton
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 carrot, diced
  • 1/2 cup peas
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 cup stock
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce (optional)
  • 4 potatoes, boiled & mashed
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 tbsp milk
  • Salt & pepper to taste

Method

1Preheat: When you reach the Airfryer step, preheat it to 190°C for 4 minutes before adding the food.
2Add a little oil to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and cook the onion and carrot for 6 minutes, then add the minced meat and brown for 8 minutes.
3Stir in tomato paste, stock and Worcestershire sauce; simmer on Sauté (Speed 1–2, ~100–110°C) for 8 minutes until thick, then fold in peas.
4Separately, mash boiled potatoes with butter, milk, salt and pepper until smooth.
5Spread the meat filling in an oven-safe dish, top with mashed potato, and bake in the Airfryer at 190°C for 20 minutes until the top is golden.
