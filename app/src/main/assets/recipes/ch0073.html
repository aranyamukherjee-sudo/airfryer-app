Bhindi Fry (Crispy Okra)

Airfryer or Chef Magic — pick whichever you have free — 16 min — Serves 4

🔥 Airfryer🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic🍽 Serves 4
IngredientsSteps

Prep ahead: Wash and dry okra completely before slicing — any moisture makes it slimy instead of crisp.

Ingredients

  • 300g okra (bhindi), sliced lengthwise
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric (haldi)
  • 1/2 tsp red chilli powder (lal mirch powder)
  • 1/2 tsp amchur (dry mango powder)
  • Salt to taste

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 200°C for 4 minutes before adding the food.
2Option A – Airfryer: toss okra with oil and all spices, spread in a single layer in the basket, and air-fry at 200°C for 14 minutes, shaking every 5 minutes, until crisp and lightly charred.
3Option B – Chef Magic: add oil to the jar, select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C), add okra and all spices, and cook, stirring occasionally, for 14 minutes until crisp and no longer sticky.
