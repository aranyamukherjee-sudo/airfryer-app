Chettinad Chicken

Chef Magic – Sauté + Boil Auto 28 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • High-Protein • Chettinad

Ingredients

  • 500g chicken, curry cut
  • 1 tbsp coriander seeds (sookha dhania)
  • 1 tsp fennel seeds (saunf)
  • 4 dried red chillies (lal mirch)
  • 1/2 tsp peppercorns
  • 2 tbsp grated coconut
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 tomato, chopped
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste (adrak-lehsun paste)
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 9 curry leaves (kadi patta)
  • Salt to taste

Method

1Dry-roast coriander seeds, fennel seeds, dried red chillies, peppercorns and coconut briefly, then grind with a little water into a paste.
2Add oil and curry leaves to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and cook the onion for 6 minutes until golden.
3Add ginger-garlic paste and tomato; cook 5 minutes, then add the ground masala paste and cook 3 minutes until fragrant.
4Add the chicken and salt; sauté 6 minutes to sear, then select Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C) and cook covered for 8 minutes until the chicken is cooked through and the gravy thickens.
