Rasgulla (Spongy Cottage Cheese Balls in Syrup)

Chef Magic – Boil Auto 26 min makes ~10

Vegetarian • Bengali

Prep ahead: Use paneer made from full-fat milk curdled fresh that day — the fresher the paneer, the spongier the rasgulla.

Ingredients

  • 1 litre full-fat milk
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice or vinegar
  • 1.5 cups sugar
  • 4 cups water
  • 1/2 tsp cardamom powder (elaichi powder)
  • 1 tbsp flour or semolina (for binding)

Method

1Bring milk to a boil in a separate pan, add lemon juice, and stir until it curdles into paneer and clear whey. Strain, rinse under cold water, and hang in muslin for 30 minutes.
2Knead the paneer with flour on a smooth surface for 8–10 minutes until soft and completely smooth, with no grains left.
3Divide into 10 smooth, crack-free balls.
4Add sugar, water and cardamom to Chef Magic’s jar; select Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C) until the syrup is bubbling well.
5Gently slide in the paneer balls; continue on Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C), covered, for 15 minutes — they will roughly double in size.
6Cool in the syrup for at least 1 hour before serving chilled.

Tip: Boiling hard and keeping the jar covered is what makes the rasgullas puff up — resist peeking too often.
