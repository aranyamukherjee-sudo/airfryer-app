Sorshe Rui (Rohu in Mustard Gravy)

Chef Magic – Sauté + Boil Auto 18 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • High-Protein • Bengali • Everyday

Prep ahead: Soak the mustard seeds in warm water for 20 minutes before grinding — this takes the bitterness out.

Ingredients

  • 6 pieces rohu (rui) fish
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric (haldi), plus extra for rubbing
  • 3 tbsp yellow mustard seeds (rai), soaked
  • 1–2 green chillies (hari mirch), plus extra to garnish
  • 1/2 tsp nigella seeds (kalonji)
  • 4 tbsp mustard oil (sarson ka tel)
  • Salt to taste

Method

1Rub the fish pieces with turmeric and salt; let sit for 10 minutes.
2Blend the soaked mustard seeds with the green chillies, a pinch of salt and a splash of water into a smooth paste.
3In Chef Magic, add mustard oil; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and shallow-fry the fish pieces for 2 minutes per side until lightly golden. Remove and set aside.
4In the same oil, crackle the nigella seeds for 30 seconds.
5Add the mustard paste, turmeric and 1 cup water; select Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C) and simmer for 4 minutes, stirring gently.
6Slide the fried fish back in, top with slit green chillies and simmer for 4 minutes more without stirring hard.
7Finish with a drizzle of raw mustard oil and serve with steamed rice.

Tip: Never over-boil a mustard gravy — it turns bitter. Keep the simmer gentle and short once the paste goes in.
