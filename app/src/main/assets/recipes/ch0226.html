Chilli Garlic Prawns

Airfryer or Chef Magic — pick whichever you have free — 16 min — Serves 4

High-Protein • Everyday

🔥 Airfryer🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic🍽 Serves 4
IngredientsSteps

Prep ahead: Marinate the prawns for 15 minutes with a little cornflour, salt and pepper for a lightly crisp coating.

Ingredients

  • 500g prawns, peeled and deveined
  • 1 tbsp cornflour
  • 1 capsicum, sliced
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste (adrak-lehsun paste)
  • 2 tbsp chilli-garlic (lehsun) sauce
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 green chillies (hari mirch), sliced
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt & pepper to taste

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 200°C for 4 minutes before adding the food to the basket.
2Option A – Airfryer: toss prawns with cornflour, salt and pepper, spray with oil, and air-fry at 200°C for 8 minutes, shaking halfway, until just opaque. Meanwhile sauté capsicum, onion, ginger-garlic paste and green chillies in oil in Chef Magic for 5 minutes, stir in chilli-garlic sauce and soy sauce, then toss in the air-fried prawns for 2 minutes before serving.
3Option B – Chef Magic only: sauté capsicum, onion, ginger-garlic paste and green chillies in oil for 5 minutes, add the prawns, chilli-garlic sauce and soy sauce, and sauté for 7 minutes, stirring gently, until the prawns are just cooked through.
