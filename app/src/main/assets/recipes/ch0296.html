Homemade Cream Cheese

Chef Magic – Boil + Whisk/Blend 20 min + Draining 1 hr — Makes ~300g

Gluten-Free • Vegetarian • Homemade

Ingredients

  • 1 litre full-fat milk
  • 500ml fresh cream
  • 3 tbsp lemon juice or white vinegar
  • 1/4 tsp salt

Method

1Add the milk and cream to the jar; select Boil (Speed 1, 100°C) and bring to a gentle boil, stirring occasionally.
2Once boiling, reduce to Sauté (Speed 1, ~90°C) and slowly add the lemon juice, stirring gently until it curdles and clear whey separates.
3Turn off and let it sit for 2 minutes, then pour through a fine muslin-lined strainer.
4Rinse briefly under cold water, then tie the cloth and hang to drain for 1 hour.
5Transfer the drained curds to Chef Magic; select Whisk/Blend (Speed 6–8) with the salt, blending for 1–2 minutes until completely smooth and spreadable, scraping down the sides as needed.
6Transfer to an airtight container and refrigerate for up to a week.

Tip: For a tangier, deli-style cream cheese, let the curds drain for a full 2 hours before blending.
