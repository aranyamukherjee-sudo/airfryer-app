Chicken Biryani (Dum-Style)

Chef Magic (Cook rice + gravy) → Airfryer (Dum finish) 160°C — 45 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • High-Protein • Everyday

🔥 Airfryer🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic🍽 Serves 4
IngredientsStepsTips

Prep ahead: Marinate the chicken in yogurt and spices for at least 30 minutes (longer is better).

Ingredients

  • 500g chicken, curry-cut pieces
  • 1/2 cup thick yogurt
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste (adrak-lehsun paste)
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder (lal mirch powder)
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric (haldi)
  • 1 tsp biryani masala
  • 2 cups basmati rice, soaked 20 minutes
  • 2 onions, thinly sliced
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 2 green chillies (hari mirch), slit
  • 1/4 cup mint leaves, chopped
  • 1/4 cup coriander leaves (hara dhania), chopped
  • 3 tbsp ghee or oil
  • 1 tsp shahi jeera (caraway) or cumin seeds (jeera)
  • 2 bay leaves (tej patta), 4 cloves (laung), 1 cinnamon stick (dalchini)
  • Salt to taste
  • A few strands of saffron (kesar) soaked in 2 tbsp warm milk (optional)

Method

1Marinate the chicken with yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, chilli powder, turmeric and biryani masala for at least 30 minutes.
2In Chef Magic, add ghee; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and fry the onions until deep golden. Set half aside for garnish.
3Add shahi jeera, bay leaves, cloves and cinnamon; sauté for 1 minute until fragrant.
4Add tomatoes and green chillies; cook for 4 minutes until soft.
5Add the marinated chicken and cook on Sauté for 8 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the chicken is half-cooked and the oil separates.
6Add the soaked rice, 3 cups hot water, salt, mint and coriander; select Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C) and cook covered for 12 minutes until the rice is just tender and water is absorbed.
7Transfer to an Airfryer-safe dish, drizzle with saffron milk and top with the reserved fried onions.
8Cover tightly and Airfryer-dum at 160°C for 10 minutes to let the flavours settle before serving.

Tip: Resist stirring the rice once it goes in — biryani rice should stay in separate, fluffy grains, not turn mushy.
