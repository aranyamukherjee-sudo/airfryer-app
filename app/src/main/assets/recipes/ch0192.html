Air-Fried Korean Cauliflower

Airfryer + Chef Magic – Fry 18 min + Sauce 2 min — Serves 4

Vegetarian • Korean • Everyday

🔥 Airfryer🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic🍽 Serves 4
IngredientsStepsTips

Ingredients

  • 1 medium cauliflower, cut into florets
  • 1/2 cup cornflour
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • Salt to taste
  • 3 tbsp gochujang
  • 2 tbsp honey or sugar syrup
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • Toasted sesame seeds, to garnish

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 200°C for 4 minutes before adding the food to the basket.
2Whisk the cornflour, water, garlic powder and salt into a smooth, thick batter.
3Dip each cauliflower floret in the batter, letting excess drip off, then arrange in the basket in a single layer.
4Air-fry at 200°C for 15–18 minutes, shaking halfway, until golden and crisp.
5While the cauliflower cooks, combine the gochujang, honey, soy sauce, rice vinegar and garlic in the jar; select Sauté (Speed 1, ~90–100°C) and warm through for 2 minutes.
6Toss the hot cauliflower in the sauce and garnish with sesame seeds. Serve immediately.

Tip: Toss the cauliflower in the sauce just before serving, not earlier — sitting in the sauce too long will soften the crispy coating.
