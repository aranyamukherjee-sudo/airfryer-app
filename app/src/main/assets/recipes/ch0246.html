Kadai Chicken

Chef Magic – Sauté + Boil Auto 28 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • High-Protein • Everyday

🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic🍽 Serves 4
IngredientsStepsTips

Ingredients

  • 500g chicken, boneless or curry-cut
  • 2 onions, cubed
  • 2 capsicums, cubed
  • 2 tomatoes, pureed
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste (adrak-lehsun paste)
  • 1 tbsp kadai masala (coriander seeds (sookha dhania) + dried red chillies (lal mirch), crushed)
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric (haldi)
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • Salt to taste
  • Coriander leaves (hara dhania) to garnish

Method

1In Chef Magic, add oil; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and cook onions for 3 minutes until just translucent.
2Add ginger-garlic paste and cook for 1 minute, then add the chicken; sauté for 6 minutes until lightly browned.
3Add tomato puree, kadai masala, turmeric and salt; cook for 6 minutes until the oil separates.
4Add 1/2 cup water; select Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C) and simmer covered for 10 minutes until the chicken is nearly cooked.
5Add the capsicum and continue on Sauté (Speed 2–4) for 4 minutes until just tender-crisp.
6Garnish with coriander leaves and serve hot.

Tip: Keep the capsicum in big cubes and add it late — it should stay crunchy, not go soft.
