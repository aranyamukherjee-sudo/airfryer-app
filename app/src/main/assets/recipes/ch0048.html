Air Fryer Chocolate Chip Cookies

Airfryer – Bake 160°C 9 min makes ~10

🔥 Airfryer
IngredientsSteps

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • 1/2 cup butter, softened
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • Pinch of salt
  • 1/2 cup chocolate chips

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 160°C for 4 minutes before adding the food to the basket.
2Cream butter and sugar until fluffy; beat in egg and vanilla.
3Fold in flour, baking soda, salt and chocolate chips to form a soft dough.
4Scoop into balls and place on parchment in the basket, spaced well apart.
5Bake at 160°C for 9 minutes until edges are golden but centres look slightly underdone; cool on the tray before moving.
