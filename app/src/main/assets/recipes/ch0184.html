Baked Mac and Cheese

Chef Magic (Cook pasta & sauce) → Airfryer 180°C Boil 10 min + Bake 15 min (Bake topping) — Serves 4

Vegetarian • American • Everyday

Ingredients

  • 2 cups macaroni
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 tbsp flour
  • 2 cups milk
  • 1.5 cups grated cheddar
  • 1/2 cup breadcrumbs
  • Salt & pepper to taste

Method

1Preheat: When you reach the Airfryer step, preheat it to 180°C for 4 minutes before adding the food.
2Add water and salt to the jar; select Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C) and cook macaroni until just tender, about 8 minutes; drain and set aside.
3Add butter to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C), whisk in flour, then gradually add milk, stirring until thickened, about 5 minutes.
4Stir in cheddar, salt and pepper, then fold in the cooked macaroni.
5Transfer to an oven-safe dish, top with remaining cheddar and breadcrumbs, and bake in the Airfryer at 180°C for 15 minutes until golden and bubbling.
