Kashmiri Rogan Josh

Chef Magic – Sauté + Boil Auto 48 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • High-Protein • Kashmiri

🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic🍽 Serves 4
IngredientsSteps

Prep ahead: Marinate the lamb with yogurt for at least 30 minutes before cooking for a more tender result.

Ingredients

  • 500g lamb, cut into pieces
  • 1/2 cup yogurt
  • 2 tbsp Kashmiri chilli powder (for colour, mild heat)
  • 1 tsp fennel (saunf) powder
  • 1/2 tsp ginger (adrak) powder (saunth)
  • 2 bay leaves (tej patta)
  • 3 cloves (laung), 2 green cardamom (elaichi)
  • 3 tbsp mustard oil (sarson ka tel)
  • Salt to taste

Method

1Marinate the lamb with yogurt for at least 30 minutes.
2Add mustard oil, bay leaves and whole spices to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) until fragrant.
3Add the marinated lamb; sauté for 12 minutes, stirring occasionally, until browned.
4Add Kashmiri chilli powder, fennel powder, ginger powder and salt with a little water; select Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C) and cook covered until the lamb is tender and the gravy is thick and deep red.
