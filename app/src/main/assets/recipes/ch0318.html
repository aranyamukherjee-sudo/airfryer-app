Oyster Mushroom Manchurian

Airfryer (Fry balls) → Chef Magic (Sauté 130–160°C 3 min + Simmer 4 min sauce) — Serves 4

Vegan • Indo-Chinese • Party Snack

Ingredients

  • 250g oyster mushrooms (dhingri), finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp cornflour
  • 2 tbsp maida (plain flour)
  • 1 tbsp finely chopped garlic (lehsun)
  • 1 tsp finely chopped ginger (adrak)
  • 2 tbsp finely chopped spring onion (white part)
  • Salt (namak) and pepper, to taste
  • Oil spray
  • 1 tbsp oil (for the sauce)
  • 1 tbsp garlic (lehsun), finely chopped
  • 1 tbsp ginger (adrak), finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp spring onion (white part), chopped
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp chilli sauce
  • 1 tbsp tomato ketchup
  • 1 cup vegetable stock or water
  • 1 tbsp cornflour mixed with 3 tbsp water
  • Spring onion greens, to garnish

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 200°C for 4 minutes before adding the food.
2Pulse the chopped mushrooms briefly (or chop very fine by hand) so the mixture holds together; mix with cornflour, maida, garlic, ginger, spring onion whites, salt and pepper into a rough dough.
3Shape into small balls, roughly the size of a walnut, pressing firmly so they don't fall apart.
4Spray the balls with oil and arrange in a single layer in the Airfryer basket.
5Air-fry at 200°C for 12 minutes, shaking gently halfway, until firm and golden-brown.
6In Chef Magic, heat oil; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and cook garlic, ginger and spring onion whites for 1 minute until fragrant.
7Add soy sauce, chilli sauce, ketchup and stock; bring to a simmer for 3 minutes.
8Stir in the cornflour-water mix and cook 1–2 minutes until glossy and thickened.
9Add the fried mushroom balls just before serving, tossing gently to coat; garnish with spring onion greens.

Tip: For a "gravy" style Manchurian instead of dry, double the sauce quantities and serve the balls floating in it with fried rice or noodles.
