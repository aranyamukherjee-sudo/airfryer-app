Homemade White Butter (Makkhan)

Chef Magic – Whisk/Blend 8 min — Makes ~150g

Gluten-Free • Vegetarian • Homemade

🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic
IngredientsStepsTips

Ingredients

  • 2 cups fresh cream (malai), chilled, or store-bought heavy cream
  • 1/2 cup ice-cold water
  • Ice cubes

Method

1Add the chilled cream to the jar; select Whisk/Blend (Speed 6–8) and whip for 3–4 minutes until it thickens.
2Continue whisking — the mixture will pass through a whipped-cream stage and then separate into solid butter grains and thin buttermilk (chaas), taking another 4–5 minutes.
3Add a splash of the ice-cold water and pulse briefly to help the butter grains clump together.
4Strain off the buttermilk (save it — it's delicious!), then gather the butter and rinse under cold water, kneading gently to remove any remaining buttermilk.
5Shape into a block, wrap, and refrigerate. Use fresh within a week, or clarify into ghee for longer storage.

Tip: Keep everything cold — the cream, the bowl, even the water — so the butter forms quickly and cleanly.
