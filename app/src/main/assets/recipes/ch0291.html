Homemade Ghee (Clarified Butter)

Chef Magic – Sauté 18 min — Makes ~400ml

Gluten-Free • Vegetarian • Homemade

🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic
IngredientsStepsTips

Ingredients

  • 500g unsalted butter (or fresh malai/cream collected over a few days)

Method

1Add the butter to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 1–2, ~110–120°C) and let it melt completely.
2Continue cooking, stirring occasionally, as it foams and the milk solids separate and sink, slowly turning golden — this takes about 15–18 minutes.
3Once the ghee turns clear golden and smells nutty, with browned solids settled at the bottom, turn off immediately to avoid burning.
4Let cool for 5 minutes, then strain through a fine muslin cloth or fine sieve into a clean, dry glass jar.
5Cool completely before sealing; store at room temperature for up to 3 months.

Tip: Don't throw out the browned milk solids left in the strainer — stir them into hot rice or dal for a delicious treat.
