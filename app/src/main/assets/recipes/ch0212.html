Egg Drop Soup

Chef Magic – Boil Auto 12 min — Serves 3

Eggetarian • Everyday

🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic🍽 Serves 3
IngredientsStepsTips

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs, lightly beaten
  • 4 cups vegetable or chicken stock
  • 1 tbsp cornflour mixed with 2 tbsp water
  • 1/2 tsp white pepper (safed mirch)
  • 1 tsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • Spring onion greens, chopped, to garnish
  • Salt to taste

Method

1In Chef Magic, add the stock; select Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C) and bring to a simmer for 5 minutes.
2Stir in the cornflour slurry and cook for 2 minutes until the soup lightly thickens.
3Season with soy sauce, white pepper and salt.
4With the jar on a gentle Boil (Speed 1), slowly drizzle in the beaten egg in a thin stream while stirring in one direction, so it sets into silky ribbons.
5Drizzle with sesame oil and garnish with spring onion greens before serving.

Tip: Pour the egg in slowly and stir gently — pouring too fast or stirring hard turns the ribbons into scrambled bits.
