Kadhi (Yogurt & Gram Flour Curry)

Chef Magic – Sauté + Boil Auto 24 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • Vegetarian • Everyday

Prep ahead: Whisk the yogurt and besan together well before it goes near heat, so the curry stays smooth instead of curdling.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups sour yogurt, whisked
  • 3 tbsp besan (gram flour (besan))
  • 2.5 cups water
  • 1 tsp mustard seeds (rai)
  • 1/2 tsp fenugreek (methi) seeds
  • 9 curry leaves (kadi patta)
  • 2 dried red chillies (lal mirch)
  • 1/4 tsp turmeric (haldi)
  • 1 tbsp oil or ghee
  • Salt to taste
  • Chopped coriander (dhania) to garnish

Method

1Whisk the yogurt, besan and water together until completely lump-free.
2Add oil, mustard seeds, fenugreek seeds, curry leaves and dried red chillies to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) until the seeds splutter.
3Add the yogurt-besan mixture and turmeric; select Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C) and cook, stirring occasionally, for 18 minutes until thickened and no longer raw-tasting.
4Season with salt and garnish with coriander; serve hot with steamed rice.

Tip: Keep the heat gentle and stir often once the yogurt goes in — a hard boil is what makes kadhi split.
