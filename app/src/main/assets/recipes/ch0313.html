Quick Puff Pastry (Rough Puff)

Chef Magic – Mix 3 min + Fold & Chill 45 min — makes ~400g

🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic
IngredientsStepsTips

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 170g cold butter, cubed
  • 1/2 cup ice-cold water
  • 1 tsp lemon juice

Method

1Add the flour and salt to the jar; pulse briefly on Whisk/Blend (Speed 2) to combine.
2Add the cold butter cubes; pulse briefly, just 2–3 times, so large pea-sized chunks of butter remain visible — do not overmix.
3Mix the ice-cold water with the lemon juice, then add to the jar; pulse just until the dough comes together shaggily.
4Turn out onto a floured surface, shape into a rough rectangle, and roll out. Fold into thirds like a letter, then rotate 90 degrees.
5Repeat the roll-fold-rotate process 2 more times, chilling for 15 minutes between folds if the butter softens too much.
6Wrap and chill for at least 30 minutes before rolling out for use.

Tip: Visible chunks of cold butter are exactly what you want — they're what create the flaky layers as the pastry bakes.
