Chicken Popcorn (Crispy Airfryer Bites)

Chef Magic (Mix marinade) → Airfryer 200°C Marinate 20 min + Fry 14 min — Serves 4

High-Protein • Everyday

Prep ahead: Marinate the chicken for at least 20 minutes before coating and frying.

Ingredients

  • 400g boneless chicken, cut into bite-sized pieces
  • 1/2 cup yogurt
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste (adrak-lehsun paste)
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder (lal mirch powder)
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper (kali mirch)
  • 1/2 cup cornflour
  • 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 1 cup breadcrumbs or panko
  • Salt to taste
  • Oil spray

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 200°C for 4 minutes before adding the food.
2Mix the chicken pieces with yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, chilli powder, pepper and salt; marinate for at least 20 minutes.
3Toss the marinated chicken in cornflour and flour, then dip in beaten egg, then coat in breadcrumbs.
4Spray generously with oil and arrange in a single layer in the Airfryer basket, without overlapping.
5Air-fry at 200°C for 14 minutes, shaking the basket halfway, until golden and crisp.
6Serve hot with tomato ketchup or a spicy mayo dip.

Tip: Work in batches if the basket looks crowded — popcorn chicken needs airflow around each piece to crisp up properly.
