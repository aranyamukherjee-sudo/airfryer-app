Rui Maach Bhaja (Crispy Airfried Rohu Fry)

Airfryer – Marinate 15 min + Fry 200°C 12 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • High-Protein • Bengali • Everyday

🔥 Airfryer🍽 Serves 4
IngredientsStepsTips

Prep ahead: Marinate the fish for at least 15 minutes before frying.

Ingredients

  • 6 pieces rohu (rui) fish
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric (haldi)
  • 1/2 tsp red chilli powder (lal mirch powder)
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp rice flour or semolina (for the crust)
  • Salt to taste
  • Mustard oil (sarson ka tel) spray

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 200°C for 4 minutes before adding the food.
2Rub the fish pieces with turmeric, chilli powder, lemon juice and salt; marinate for at least 15 minutes.
3Coat each piece lightly in rice flour or semolina, pressing gently so it sticks.
4Spray generously with mustard oil and arrange in a single layer in the Airfryer basket.
5Air-fry at 200°C for 12 minutes, flipping halfway, until crisp and golden on both sides.
6Serve hot with a squeeze of lemon and sliced onions.

Tip: A light dusting of rice flour rather than a thick batter keeps this true to the classic Bengali maach bhaja — thin, crisp, and not deep-fried heavy.
