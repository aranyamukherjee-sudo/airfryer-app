Honey Chilli Potato

Airfryer (Fry the potatoes) → Chef Magic (Toss 200°C Fry 18 min + Toss 6 min in glaze) — Serves 4

Vegetarian • Everyday • Indo-Chinese

Prep ahead: Par-boil the potato strips for 3 minutes before coating, so the inside stays soft while the outside crisps up.

Ingredients

  • 4 medium potatoes, cut into thick matchsticks, par-boiled 3 min
  • 3 tbsp cornflour
  • 2 tbsp maida (plain flour)
  • Salt to taste
  • Oil spray
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste (adrak-lehsun paste)
  • 1 capsicum, sliced
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp chilli sauce
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp toasted sesame seeds
  • Spring onion greens to garnish

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 200°C for 4 minutes before adding the food.
2Pat the par-boiled potato strips dry and toss with cornflour, maida and salt until evenly coated.
3Spray generously with oil and arrange in a single layer in the Airfryer basket.
4Air-fry at 200°C for 18 minutes, shaking every 5 minutes, until deep golden and crisp.
5In Chef Magic, add a little oil; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and cook ginger-garlic paste and capsicum for 2 minutes.
6Add soy sauce, chilli sauce and honey; simmer on Sauté (Speed 1–2, ~100–110°C) for 2 minutes until it turns into a light, sticky glaze.
7Toss in the crispy potatoes just before serving; sprinkle with sesame seeds and spring onion.
