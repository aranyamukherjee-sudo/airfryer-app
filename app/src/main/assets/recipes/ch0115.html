Jhinge Aloo Bata Sorshe Diye (Ridge Gourd & Potato in Mustard Paste)

Chef Magic – Sauté + Boil Auto 19 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • Vegan • Bengali

Prep ahead: Soak mustard seeds in warm water for 18 minutes before grinding into a paste, with a green chilli, for a smoother,

Ingredients

  • 2 ridge gourds (jhinge), peeled and chopped
  • 1 potato, diced
  • 2 tbsp mustard seeds (rai), soaked and ground to a paste
  • 1/2 tsp nigella seeds (kalonji)
  • 1 green chilli (hari mirch), slit
  • 2 tbsp mustard oil (sarson ka tel)
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric (haldi)
  • Salt to taste

Method

1Add mustard oil and nigella seeds to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) for 1 minute.
2Add potato and turmeric; sauté 4 minutes.
3Add ridge gourd and sauté a further 4 minutes until it starts to soften and release water.
4Stir in the mustard paste, green chilli and salt; select Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C) and cook until vegetables are fully tender and the gravy is thickened.
