Popcorn Paneer

Airfryer – Fry 190°C 12 min — Serves 4

Vegetarian • High-Protein • Everyday

🔥 Airfryer🍽 Serves 4
IngredientsStepsTips

Ingredients

  • 400g paneer, cut into bite-sized cubes
  • 1/2 cup cornflour
  • 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • Salt to taste
  • 1/2 cup buttermilk or milk
  • 1 cup breadcrumbs
  • Oil spray

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 190°C for 4 minutes before adding the food to the basket.
2Soak the paneer cubes in buttermilk for 10 minutes.
3Mix the cornflour, flour, chilli powder, garlic powder and salt in a bowl.
4Drain the paneer, toss in the flour mix, then coat in breadcrumbs, pressing to help them stick.
5Spray lightly with oil and arrange in the basket, spaced apart.
6Air-fry at 190°C for 10–12 minutes, shaking halfway, until golden and crisp.

Tip: Soaking the paneer in buttermilk first keeps it soft and juicy inside while the coating turns delightfully crisp.
