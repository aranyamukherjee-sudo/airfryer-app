Dhaba-Style Egg Curry (Simple Everyday)

Chef Magic – Sauté + Boil Auto 22 min — Serves 3

Gluten-Free • Eggetarian • Everyday

🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic🍽 Serves 3
IngredientsStepsTips

Ingredients

  • 5 eggs, boiled and peeled
  • 2 onions, finely chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste (adrak-lehsun paste)
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder (lal mirch powder)
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric (haldi)
  • 1 tsp coriander powder (dhania powder)
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds (jeera)
  • Salt to taste
  • Coriander leaves (hara dhania) to garnish

Method

1In Chef Magic, add oil; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and crackle the cumin seeds for 30 seconds.
2Add onions and cook for 6 minutes until golden.
3Add ginger-garlic paste and cook for 1 minute, then add tomatoes, chilli powder, turmeric, coriander powder and salt; cook for 6 minutes until the oil separates.
4Add 1 cup water; select Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C) and simmer for 5 minutes.
5Slit the boiled eggs lightly and slide them into the gravy; simmer for 5 minutes so they soak up the flavour.
6Sprinkle garam masala and coriander leaves before serving with rice or rotis.

Tip: This is the quick, everyday dhaba version — no cashew paste, no cream, just onion-tomato masala and boiled eggs.
