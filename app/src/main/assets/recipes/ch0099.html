Dahi Bhindi (Okra in Yogurt Gravy)

Chef Magic – Sauté Auto 20 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • Vegetarian • Everyday

🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic🍽 Serves 4
IngredientsStepsTips

Prep ahead: Wash and dry the okra completely before slicing, and whisk the yogurt smooth before it goes in.

Ingredients

  • 300g okra (bhindi), sliced
  • 1 cup yogurt, whisked smooth
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds (jeera)
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric (haldi)
  • 1/2 tsp red chilli powder (lal mirch powder)
  • 1 tsp coriander powder (dhania powder)
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt to taste
  • Chopped coriander (dhania) to garnish

Method

1Add 1 tbsp oil to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and cook the okra for 8 minutes, stirring occasionally, until lightly browned and no longer sticky; remove and set aside.
2Add the remaining oil and cumin seeds; sauté until they splutter, then add onion and cook 5 minutes until soft.
3Add turmeric, chilli powder and coriander powder; sauté 1 minute.
4Lower to Sauté (Speed 1–2, ~100–110°C), stir in the whisked yogurt and salt, and simmer gently for 4 minutes, stirring often.
5Fold the cooked okra back in and warm through for 2 minutes. Garnish with coriander.

Tip: Pre-cooking the okra separately before adding the yogurt is what keeps it from turning slimy in this dish.
