Paneer Cutlets

Airfryer – Fry 190°C 16 min — Serves 4 (makes ~8)

Vegetarian • Everyday

Prep ahead: Chill the shaped cutlets for 15 minutes before air-frying so they hold together better.

Ingredients

  • 250g paneer, grated
  • 2 boiled potatoes, mashed
  • 1/4 cup boiled peas & carrot, chopped
  • 1 tsp ginger (adrak), grated
  • 1 green chilli (hari mirch), chopped
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • 2 tbsp cornflour
  • 1 cup breadcrumbs
  • Salt to taste
  • Oil spray

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 190°C for 4 minutes before adding the food to the basket.
2Mix grated paneer, mashed potato, vegetables, ginger, chilli, garam masala, cornflour and salt into a firm dough.
3Shape into flat patties and chill for 15 minutes.
4Coat each patty in breadcrumbs, pressing gently so they stick.
5Spray with oil and arrange in a single layer in the basket.
6Air-fry at 190°C for 16 minutes, flipping halfway, until golden and crisp on both sides.
