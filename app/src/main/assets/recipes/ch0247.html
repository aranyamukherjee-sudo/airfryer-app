Chicken Saag (Chicken with Spinach)

Chef Magic – Sauté + Boil Auto 30 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • High-Protein • Everyday

🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic🍽 Serves 4
IngredientsStepsTips

Ingredients

  • 500g chicken, boneless, cubed
  • 400g spinach, roughly chopped
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste (adrak-lehsun paste)
  • 1–2 green chillies (hari mirch)
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric (haldi)
  • 1 tsp coriander powder (dhania powder)
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • 3 tbsp oil or ghee
  • 2 tbsp cream (optional)
  • Salt to taste

Method

1In Chef Magic, add a splash of water; select Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C) and blanch the spinach with green chillies for 3 minutes. Blend to a smooth puree; set aside.
2Add oil to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and cook the onion for 5 minutes until golden.
3Add ginger-garlic paste and cook for 1 minute, then add tomatoes, turmeric, coriander powder and salt; cook for 5 minutes until soft.
4Add the chicken and sauté for 6 minutes until lightly browned and coated in the masala.
5Stir in the spinach puree and 1/4 cup water; select Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C) and simmer covered for 10 minutes until the chicken is cooked through.
6Stir in garam masala and cream if using; simmer for 2 minutes before serving.

Tip: Blanching the spinach briefly (rather than raw-blending it) keeps the saag a deep green instead of turning muddy.
