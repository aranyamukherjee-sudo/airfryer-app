Aloo Jeera (Dry Potato Sabzi)

Chef Magic – Sauté Auto 16 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • Vegan • Everyday • Low-Oil

Ingredients

  • 4 medium potatoes, boiled and cubed
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds (jeera)
  • 1/4 tsp turmeric (haldi)
  • 1/2 tsp red chilli powder (lal mirch powder)
  • 1/4 tsp amchur (dry mango powder)
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt to taste
  • Chopped coriander (dhania) to garnish

Method

1Add oil and cumin seeds to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) until the seeds splutter.
2Add the boiled potato cubes, turmeric, chilli powder and salt.
3Continue on Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) for 12 minutes, stirring gently, until the potatoes are lightly golden at the edges.
4Sprinkle with amchur and toss well; garnish with coriander before serving.
