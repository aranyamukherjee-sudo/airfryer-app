Shortcrust Pastry (Pie Crust)

Chef Magic – Mix 3 min + Chill 30 min — makes 1 pie base

🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic
IngredientsStepsTips

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp sugar (omit for savoury pies)
  • 115g cold butter, cubed
  • 3–4 tbsp ice-cold water

Method

1Add the flour, salt and sugar to the jar; pulse briefly on Whisk/Blend (Speed 2) to combine.
2Add the cold butter cubes; pulse in short bursts on Whisk/Blend (Speed 2–3) until the mixture resembles coarse breadcrumbs.
3Add the ice-cold water, 1 tablespoon at a time, pulsing briefly after each addition, until the dough just comes together.
4Turn out, shape into a flat disc, wrap in cling film, and chill for at least 30 minutes before rolling.
5Roll out on a floured surface and use to line a pie or tart tin as required by your recipe.

Tip: Keep everything cold — the butter, the water, even the bowl — for a flaky, tender crust instead of a tough one.
