Paneer Angara (Smoky Charred Paneer Curry)

Airfryer (Char paneer) → Chef Magic (Make 200°C Char 8 min + Gravy 18 min gravy) — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • Vegetarian • High-Protein • Everyday

Ingredients

  • 300g paneer, cubed
  • 1 tbsp oil, for charring
  • 2 onions, roughly chopped
  • 3 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste (adrak-lehsun paste)
  • 1.5 tsp Kashmiri chilli powder (mirch powder)
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp kasuri methi (dried fenugreek (methi)), crushed
  • 2 tbsp cream
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • Salt to taste
  • A small piece of charcoal & a few drops of oil (optional, for a smoky finish)

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 200°C for 4 minutes before adding the food to the basket.
2Toss the paneer cubes lightly in oil and air-fry at 200°C for 8 minutes, shaking once, until charred at the edges.
3In Chef Magic, add butter; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and cook the onions for 6 minutes until golden.
4Add tomatoes and ginger-garlic paste; cook for 8 minutes until soft, then select Whisk/Blend (Speed 8–10) to purée smooth.
5Return to Sauté (Speed 1–2, ~100–110°C); stir in chilli powder, garam masala and salt, and simmer 4 minutes.
6Add the charred paneer, cream and crushed kasuri methi; sauté gently for 3 minutes. For a traditional smoky finish, place a small piece of heated charcoal in a bowl in the centre of the curry, add a drop of oil, and cover for 1 minute before serving (optional, use tongs and care).
