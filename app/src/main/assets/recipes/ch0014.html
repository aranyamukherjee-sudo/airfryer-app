Popcorn Cauliflower (Gobi Popcorn)

Airfryer – Fry 190°C 14 min — Serves 4

Vegetarian • Everyday

🔥 Airfryer🍽 Serves 4
IngredientsStepsTips

Ingredients

  • 1 medium cauliflower, cut into small florets
  • 1/2 cup cornflour
  • 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder
  • 1/2 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • Salt to taste
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 1 cup breadcrumbs
  • Oil spray

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 190°C for 4 minutes before adding the food to the basket.
2Whisk the cornflour, flour, chilli powder, ginger-garlic paste, salt and water into a thick batter.
3Dip the cauliflower florets in the batter, letting excess drip off, then coat in breadcrumbs.
4Spray lightly with oil and arrange in the basket in a single layer.
5Air-fry at 190°C for 12–14 minutes, shaking halfway, until golden and crisp.

Tip: Cut the florets small and even — bite-sized pieces cook through and crisp up far more reliably than large chunks.
