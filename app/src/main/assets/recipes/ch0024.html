Boondi Raita

No-Cook — 6 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • Vegetarian • No-Cook • Everyday

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups thick yogurt, whisked
  • 1/2 cup boondi (fried gram flour (besan) balls)
  • 1/4 tsp roasted cumin powder (bhuna jeera powder)
  • Pinch of red chilli powder (lal mirch powder)
  • Salt to taste
  • Chopped coriander (dhania)

Method

1Soak the boondi in warm water for 5 minutes to soften slightly, then squeeze out excess water.
2Whisk the yogurt with salt until smooth; fold in the boondi.
3Sprinkle with roasted cumin powder, chilli powder and coriander before serving chilled.
