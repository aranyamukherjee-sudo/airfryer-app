Egg Bhurji Kathi Roll

Chef Magic (Make bhurji) → Airfryer 180°C Warm rotis 3 min — makes 4 rolls

Eggetarian • Everyday

Ingredients

  • 4 eggs
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 1 tomato, finely chopped
  • 1 green chilli (hari mirch), chopped
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric (haldi)
  • 1/2 tsp red chilli powder (lal mirch powder)
  • 4 rotis or flatbreads
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Sliced onions and mint chutney to serve
  • Salt to taste
  • Coriander leaves (hara dhania), chopped

Method

1Preheat: When you reach the Airfryer step, preheat it to 180°C for 3 minutes.
2In Chef Magic, add oil; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and cook the onion for 4 minutes until soft.
3Add tomato, green chilli, turmeric, chilli powder and salt; cook for 3 minutes until the tomato softens.
4Crack in the eggs and scramble on Sauté (Speed 1–2) for 3 minutes until just set. Stir in coriander leaves.
5Warm the rotis in the Airfryer at 180°C for 3 minutes.
6Spread mint chutney on each roti, spoon the egg bhurji down the centre, top with sliced onions, and roll tightly.

Tip: Roll while the bhurji is still warm and wrap the base in foil or parchment — it holds together much better for eating on the go.
