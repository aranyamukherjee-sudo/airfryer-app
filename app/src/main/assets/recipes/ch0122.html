Aloo Arbi Gravy (Potato & Colocasia Curry)

Chef Magic – Sauté + Boil Auto 24 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • Vegan • Everyday • North Indian

Prep ahead: Pat the boiled arbi completely dry and peel it while still warm — a wet or cold arbi turns slimy and won't take on colour.

Ingredients

  • 250g arbi (colocasia/taro root), boiled, peeled and halved
  • 2 medium potatoes, cubed
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste (adrak-lehsun paste)
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds (jeera)
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric (haldi)
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder (lal mirch powder)
  • 1 tsp coriander powder (dhania powder)
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • 1/2 tsp amchur (dry mango powder)
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt to taste
  • Chopped coriander (dhania) to garnish

Method

1Add oil and cumin seeds to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) until the seeds splutter.
2Add the onion and sauté for 5 minutes until golden.
3Add ginger-garlic paste, turmeric, chilli powder and coriander powder; sauté 2 minutes until fragrant.
4Add the tomatoes and cook for 5 minutes until soft and the oil separates at the edges.
5Add the potatoes and arbi with a splash of water; select Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C) and cook for 10 minutes until the potatoes are tender and the arbi is coated in a thick gravy.
6Stir in garam masala and amchur; cook 2 minutes more. Garnish with coriander and serve hot with roti.

Tip: Don't stir too vigorously once the arbi goes in — it breaks down easily and can make the gravy gluey rather than glossy.
