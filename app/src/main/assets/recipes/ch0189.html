Korean Fried Tofu (Dubu Jorim)

Chef Magic – Sauté 15 min — Serves 3

Vegetarian • Korean • High-Protein • Everyday

Ingredients

  • 400g firm tofu, cut into 1cm slabs
  • 2 tbsp cornflour
  • 2 tbsp neutral oil
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp gochugaru
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 spring onion, sliced
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • Toasted sesame seeds, to garnish

Method

1Pat the tofu slabs dry and dust lightly with cornflour on both sides.
2Add the oil to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–3, ~150–160°C) and pan-fry the tofu for 3–4 minutes per side until golden and crisp. Set aside.
3In the same jar, combine the soy sauce, gochugaru, sugar, garlic and 3 tbsp water; select Sauté (Speed 1–2, ~100–110°C) and simmer for 2 minutes until slightly thickened.
4Return the tofu to the jar, spooning the sauce over each piece, and simmer for 2–3 minutes until well coated.
5Stir in the sesame oil, sprinkle with spring onion and sesame seeds, and serve warm.

Tip: Pressing the tofu for 15 minutes before cooking (under a weight, wrapped in a towel) helps it crisp up beautifully instead of steaming in the pan.
