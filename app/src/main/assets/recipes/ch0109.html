Pav Bhaji

Chef Magic – Boil + Sauté Auto 32 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • Vegetarian • Everyday

🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic🍽 Serves 4
IngredientsSteps

Ingredients

  • 2 potatoes, chopped
  • 1 cup cauliflower florets
  • 1/2 cup peas
  • 1 capsicum, chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 tbsp pav bhaji masala
  • 1/2 tsp red chilli powder (lal mirch powder)
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • Salt to taste
  • Pav (bread rolls) & chopped onion, lemon, to serve

Method

1Add potatoes, cauliflower, peas and enough water to the jar; select Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C) and cook until fully soft.
2Add butter to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and cook onion until golden, then add capsicum and tomatoes, cooking until mushy.
3Add pav bhaji masala and chilli powder; sauté 2 minutes.
4Add the boiled vegetables with a little of their water; mash roughly and simmer on Sauté (Speed 1–2, ~100–110°C) for
5minutes until thick. Serve with buttered, toasted pav.
