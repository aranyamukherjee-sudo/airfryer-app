Fresh Pasta Dough

Chef Magic – Knead 6 min + Rest 30 min — makes ~400g

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour (or "00" flour), plus extra for dusting
  • 3 large eggs
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1/2 tsp salt

Method

1Add the flour, eggs, olive oil and salt to the jar.
2Select Knead (Speed 2–3, no heat) and mix until a shaggy dough forms, then continue kneading for 5–6 minutes until smooth and elastic.
3Wrap the dough in cling film and rest at room temperature for 30 minutes.
4Divide into portions and roll out thinly (by hand or pasta machine), dusting with flour as needed, then cut into your desired pasta shape.
5Cook in boiling salted water for 2–3 minutes for fresh pasta, until al dente.

Tip: Resting the dough is essential — skipping this step makes the pasta tough and hard to roll thin, since the gluten needs time to relax.
