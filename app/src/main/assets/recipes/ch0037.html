Eggless Chocolate Mug-Style Cake

Airfryer – Bake 160°C 21 min (small cake) — Serves 4

🔥 Airfryer🍽 Serves 4
IngredientsStepsTips

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • 1/3 cup cocoa powder
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1/4 cup oil
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/2 tsp vinegar

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 160°C for 4 minutes before adding the food to the basket.
2Whisk all dry ingredients together in a bowl.
3Add milk, oil, vanilla and vinegar; mix until smooth and lump-free.
4Pour into a greased oven-safe pan that fits the basket.
5Bake at 160°C for 21 minutes; check with a toothpick before removing.

Tip: Cover the pan loosely with foil for the first 10 minutes to stop the top browning too fast.
