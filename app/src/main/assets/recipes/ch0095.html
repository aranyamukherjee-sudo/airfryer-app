Gajar Matar Sabzi (Carrot & Peas Stir-Fry)

Chef Magic – Sauté Auto 14 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • Vegan • Everyday • Low-Oil

🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic🍽 Serves 4
IngredientsStepsTips

Ingredients

  • 3 medium carrots, diced
  • 1 cup green peas (fresh or frozen)
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds (jeera)
  • 1/4 tsp turmeric (haldi)
  • 1/2 tsp red chilli powder (lal mirch powder)
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt to taste
  • Chopped coriander (dhania) to garnish

Method

1Add oil and cumin seeds to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) until the seeds splutter.
2Add the diced carrots, turmeric and chilli powder; sauté for 6 minutes.
3Add the peas and salt, with a splash of water; continue on Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) for 6 minutes until the carrots are tender and the peas are cooked through.
4Garnish with coriander and serve as a simple side with dal-rice or roti.

Tip: A five-minute, everyday sabzi that barely needs watching — good for busy weeknights.
