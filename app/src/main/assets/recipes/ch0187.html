Veg Burger (Crispy Vegetable Patty Burger)

Airfryer – Bake 200°C 16 min — Serves 4

Vegetarian • Everyday • Continental

🔥 Airfryer🍽 Serves 4
IngredientsStepsTips

Prep ahead: Chill the shaped patties in the fridge for at least 20 minutes before air-frying — this is what keeps them from falling apart.

Ingredients

  • 3 medium potatoes, boiled & mashed
  • 1/2 cup boiled mixed vegetables (carrot, peas, corn), lightly mashed
  • 2 tbsp breadcrumbs, plus extra for coating
  • 1 tbsp cornflour
  • 1/2 tsp red chilli powder
  • 1/2 tsp roasted cumin powder
  • 1 tbsp chopped coriander
  • Salt to taste
  • Oil spray
  • 4 burger buns
  • Butter, for toasting the buns
  • Lettuce leaves, tomato & onion slices
  • Mayonnaise, ketchup & mustard, to serve
  • 4 cheese slices (optional)

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 200°C for 4 minutes before adding the food.
2Mix mashed potato, mashed mixed vegetables, breadcrumbs, cornflour, chilli powder, cumin powder, coriander and salt into a firm dough.
3Shape into 4 patties and chill for at least 20 minutes.
4Coat each patty lightly in extra breadcrumbs and spray generously with oil.
5Air-fry at 200°C for 16 minutes, flipping halfway, until golden and crisp.
6In the last 2 minutes, halve the buns and toast lightly (in the Airfryer or on a hot pan) with a little butter; melt a cheese slice onto each patty if using.
7Assemble: bun base, sauces, lettuce, tomato, onion, the patty, and the bun top. Serve hot.

Tip: If the mixture feels too soft to shape, add a little extra breadcrumbs rather than more cornflour — it keeps the patty light instead of dense.
