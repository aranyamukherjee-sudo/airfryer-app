Achari Soya Chaap (Pickle-Spiced)

Chef Magic – Sauté Auto 21 min — Serves 4

High-Protein • Vegetarian • Everyday

Ingredients

  • 500g soya chaap, cut into pieces
  • 1 tsp mustard seeds (rai)
  • 1 tsp fennel seeds (saunf)
  • 1/2 tsp fenugreek (methi) seeds
  • 1/2 tsp nigella (kalonji) seeds
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric (haldi)
  • 1.5 tsp red chilli powder (lal mirch powder)
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • Salt to taste

Method

1Add oil to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and add mustard seeds, fennel seeds, fenugreek seeds and nigella seeds; cook 1 minute until fragrant.
2Add the chopped onion and cook for 4 minutes until golden.
3Add turmeric, chilli powder and the soya chaap pieces; sauté for 13 minutes, stirring occasionally, until well coated and lightly browned.
4Finish with lemon juice and salt to taste; toss well before serving.
