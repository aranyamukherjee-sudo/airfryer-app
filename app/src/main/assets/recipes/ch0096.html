Aloo Paratha (Stuffed Potato Flatbread)

Chef Magic (Knead & filling) → Airfryer (Cook) 200°C Knead 5 min + Cook 8 min makes 4

Vegetarian • Everyday

🔥 Airfryer🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic
IngredientsStepsTips

Prep ahead: Rest the dough for 15 minutes before rolling so it’s easier to shape.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups whole wheat flour
  • Water as needed
  • 1/2 tsp salt (for dough)
  • 2 medium potatoes, boiled and mashed
  • 1 green chilli (hari mirch), finely chopped
  • 1 tsp ginger (adrak), grated
  • 1/2 tsp roasted cumin powder (bhuna jeera powder)
  • 1/4 tsp garam masala
  • 2 tbsp chopped coriander (dhania)
  • Salt to taste (for filling)
  • Oil or ghee, for cooking

Method

1Preheat: When you reach the Airfryer step, preheat it to 200°C for 4 minutes before adding the food.
2In Chef Magic, add flour, salt (for dough) and a little water; select Knead (Speed 2–3, no heat), adding water gradually until a soft dough forms. Rest 15 minutes.
3Mix mashed potato with green chilli, ginger, cumin powder, garam masala, coriander and salt for the filling.
4Divide dough into 4 balls; flatten each, place a portion of filling in the centre, and seal into a ball. Roll out gently into a flatbread, dusting with flour as needed.
5Brush both sides lightly with oil and place in the Airfryer basket.
6Cook at 200°C for 8 minutes, flipping halfway, until golden-brown spots appear on both sides.
7Serve hot with curd, pickle or butter.

Tip: Roll gently and evenly — pressing too hard can push the filling through the dough.
