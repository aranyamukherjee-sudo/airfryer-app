Gatte ki Sabzi

Chef Magic – Boil then Sauté Auto 26 min — Serves 4

Vegetarian • Rajasthani

🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic🍽 Serves 4
IngredientsSteps

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups besan (gram flour (besan))
  • 2 tbsp oil, divided
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric (haldi), divided
  • Salt to taste
  • Water as needed
  • 1 cup yogurt, whisked
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds (jeera)
  • 1/2 tsp red chilli powder (lal mirch powder)
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • Chopped coriander (dhania)

Method

1Mix besan, oil, turmeric and salt with water into a firm dough; roll into thin logs.
2Add water to the jar; select Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C) and cook the besan logs for 10 minutes until firm. Cool slightly and slice into pieces (gatte).
3Add oil and cumin seeds to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) until fragrant, then add turmeric, chilli powder and whisked yogurt.
4Add the gatte pieces and simmer on Sauté (Speed 1–2, ~100–110°C) for 10 minutes, stirring gently, until the gravy thickens. Sprinkle garam masala and garnish with coriander.
