Whole Wheat Pizza, Dough to Bake

Chef Magic (Knead) → Airfryer (Bake) 200°C — Knead 5 min + Rest 1 hr + Bake 11 min — makes 2 pizzas

Ingredients

  • 2 cups whole wheat flour
  • 1 tsp instant yeast
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 3/4 cup warm water (105–110°F / 40–43°C)
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • Pizza sauce, cheese & toppings of choice

Method

1Preheat: When you reach the Airfryer step, preheat it to 200°C for 4 minutes before adding the food.
2In Chef Magic, dissolve yeast and sugar in warm water heated to 105–110°F (40–43°C) — warm to the touch, not hot; anything above 120°F (49°C) will kill the yeast; let sit 5–10 minutes until frothy.
3Add flour, oil and salt to the jar with the yeast mixture; select Knead (Speed 2–3, no heat) until a smooth, elastic dough forms.
4Cover and let the dough rise for about 1 hour, until doubled.
5Divide, roll into bases, top with sauce, cheese and toppings, then bake in the Airfryer at 200°C for 11 minutes until crisp and golden.
