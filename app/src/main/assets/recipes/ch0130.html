Amritsari Chole Kulche (Chole Only)

Chef Magic – Boil + Sauté Auto 38 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • Vegan • High-Protein • Everyday

🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic🍽 Serves 4
IngredientsSteps

Prep ahead: Soak dried chickpeas overnight in plenty of water.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups chickpeas, soaked overnight
  • 2 tbsp Amritsari chole masala
  • 2 onions, chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, pureed
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste (adrak-lehsun paste)
  • 1 black tea bag
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • Chopped onion, ginger (adrak) juliennes and coriander (dhania) to garnish

Method

1Add soaked chickpeas, enough water to cover, the tea bag and salt to the jar; select Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C) and cook for 22 minutes until soft and darker in colour. Discard the tea bag.
2Add oil to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and cook onions for 6 minutes until deep golden.
3Add ginger-garlic paste, tomatoes and chole masala; sauté for 9 minutes until the oil separates.
4Add the boiled chickpeas with a little cooking liquid; simmer on Sauté (Speed 1–2, ~100–110°C) for 9 minutes until thick. Finish with lemon juice and garnish with onion, ginger and coriander.
