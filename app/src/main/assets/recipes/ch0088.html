Moong Dal Tadka (Everyday Yellow Dal)

Chef Magic – Sauté + Boil Auto 20 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • Vegetarian • High-Protein • Everyday • Low-Oil

🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic🍽 Serves 4
IngredientsStepsTips

Prep ahead: Wash and soak the moong dal for 15 minutes to shorten the cooking time.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup yellow moong dal, washed
  • 3 cups water
  • 1/4 tsp turmeric (haldi)
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds (jeera)
  • 1 dried red chilli (lal mirch)
  • 2 cloves garlic (lehsun), thinly sliced
  • 1 tbsp ghee
  • Salt to taste
  • Chopped coriander (dhania) to garnish

Method

1Add dal, water, turmeric and salt to the jar; select Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C) and cook until soft and slightly mushy.
2Add ghee, cumin seeds, dried red chilli and garlic directly to the cooked dal; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) for 2 minutes to temper until the garlic turns golden.
3Stir well and garnish with coriander before serving with rice or roti.

Tip: This is the everyday, no-fuss dal most Indian homes make on a busy weeknight — quick, light, and easy on the stomach.
