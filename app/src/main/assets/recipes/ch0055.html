Besan Ladoo

Chef Magic – Sauté Auto 20 min makes ~14

Vegetarian • Everyday

🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic
IngredientsStepsTips

Ingredients

  • 2 cups besan (gram flour (besan))
  • 3/4 cup ghee
  • 3/4 cup powdered sugar
  • 1/2 tsp cardamom powder (elaichi powder)
  • 10 cashews, chopped (optional)

Method

1Add ghee to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and add besan once melted.
2Continue on Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) for 16 minutes, stirring frequently, until the besan turns deep golden and gives off a rich, nutty aroma.
3Stir in chopped cashews for the last 2 minutes.
4Cool for 10 minutes, then mix in the powdered sugar and cardamom powder — adding sugar while too hot will melt it.
5Shape into small balls while the mixture is still warm enough to hold together.
6Cool completely before storing in an airtight container.

Tip: Besan scorches quickly once it starts to turn golden — stay close to the jar for the last few minutes.
