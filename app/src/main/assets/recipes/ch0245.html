Ghar ka Chicken Curry (Simple Home-Style)

Chef Magic – Sauté + Boil Auto 30 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • High-Protein • Everyday

Ingredients

  • 500g chicken, curry-cut pieces
  • 2 onions, finely chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste (adrak-lehsun paste)
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder (lal mirch powder)
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric (haldi)
  • 1 tsp coriander powder (dhania powder)
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds (jeera)
  • Salt to taste
  • Coriander leaves (hara dhania) to garnish

Method

1In Chef Magic, add oil; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and crackle the cumin seeds for 30 seconds.
2Add onions and cook for 7 minutes until golden.
3Add ginger-garlic paste and cook for 1 minute, then add tomatoes, chilli powder, turmeric, coriander powder and salt; cook for 6 minutes until the oil separates.
4Add the chicken and sauté for 5 minutes until it changes colour and coats in the masala.
5Add 1 cup water; select Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C) and simmer covered for 12 minutes until the chicken is cooked through.
6Sprinkle garam masala and coriander leaves before serving.

Tip: This is the everyday, no-frills version — thinner gravy, simple spices, and best eaten with plain steamed rice or rotis.
