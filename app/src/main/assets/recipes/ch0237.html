Classic Chicken Nuggets

Airfryer – Fry 190°C 14 min — Makes ~14

High-Protein • Everyday

Ingredients

  • 500g boneless chicken breast or thigh, minced or finely chopped
  • 1/2 cup breadcrumbs (for the mix)
  • 1 egg
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp onion powder
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 1/2 cup all-purpose flour (for dredging)
  • 1 egg, beaten (for dredging)
  • 1.5 cups breadcrumbs (for coating)
  • Oil spray

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 190°C for 4 minutes before adding the food to the basket.
2In a bowl, mix the minced chicken, breadcrumbs, egg, garlic powder, onion powder, salt and pepper until well combined.
3Shape into small nugget-sized ovals.
4Dredge each nugget in flour, dip in the beaten egg, then coat in breadcrumbs, pressing to help them stick.
5Spray lightly with oil and arrange in the basket, spaced apart.
6Air-fry at 190°C for 12–14 minutes, turning halfway, until golden, crisp and cooked through.

Tip: Wetting your hands slightly before shaping the nuggets stops the chicken mixture from sticking to your fingers.
