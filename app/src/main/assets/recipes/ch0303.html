Schezwan Sauce

Chef Magic – Blend + Sauté 15 min — Makes ~1 cup

Vegetarian • Homemade • Indo-Chinese

🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic
IngredientsStepsTips

Ingredients

  • 15–20 dried red chillies (lal mirch), soaked in hot water for 20 minutes
  • 8 cloves garlic (lehsun), chopped
  • 1-inch ginger (adrak), chopped
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 2 tbsp tomato ketchup
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp vinegar
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • Salt to taste

Method

1Drain the soaked chillies and add to the jar with a splash of water; select Whisk/Blend (Speed 8–10) and blend into a smooth paste.
2Add the oil, garlic and ginger to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–150°C) and cook for 2 minutes until fragrant.
3Add the chilli paste and cook, stirring frequently, for 6–8 minutes until the oil separates and the raw smell disappears.
4Stir in the ketchup, soy sauce, vinegar, sugar and salt; simmer on Sauté (Speed 1–2) for 3–4 minutes until glossy and thickened.
5Cool and bottle; refrigerate for up to 3 weeks.

Tip: This is the base for almost every Indo-Chinese dry dish in this book — stir a spoonful into fried rice or noodles for an instant flavour boost.
