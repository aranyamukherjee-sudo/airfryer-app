Cheese Sauce (for Mac & Cheese, Nachos)

Chef Magic – Sauté 10 min — Makes ~2 cups

Vegetarian • Homemade

🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic
IngredientsStepsTips

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 tbsp all-purpose flour (maida)
  • 1.5 cups milk, warmed
  • 1.5 cups grated cheddar cheese
  • 1/4 tsp mustard powder
  • A pinch of nutmeg (jaiphal)
  • Salt and black pepper (kali mirch) to taste

Method

1Add the butter to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 1–2, ~110–120°C) and melt completely.
2Add the flour and whisk continuously for 2 minutes without browning.
3Gradually whisk in the warm milk, a little at a time, until smooth and fully incorporated.
4Cook, stirring constantly, for 4–5 minutes until thickened.
5Turn off the heat and stir in the grated cheese, mustard powder, nutmeg, salt and pepper until melted and smooth.

Tip: Take the sauce off the heat before adding the cheese — direct high heat can make it grainy instead of silky.
