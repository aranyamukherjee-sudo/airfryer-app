Chicken Keema (Minced Chicken Curry)

Chef Magic – Sauté + Boil Auto 26 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • High-Protein • Everyday

Ingredients

  • 500g minced chicken (keema)
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste (adrak-lehsun paste)
  • 1/2 cup green peas
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder (lal mirch powder)
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric (haldi)
  • 1 tsp coriander powder (dhania powder)
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds (jeera)
  • Salt to taste
  • Coriander leaves (hara dhania) to garnish

Method

1In Chef Magic, add oil; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and crackle the cumin seeds for 30 seconds.
2Add the onion and cook for 5 minutes until golden.
3Add ginger-garlic paste and cook for 1 minute, then add tomatoes, chilli powder, turmeric, coriander powder and salt; cook for 5 minutes until soft.
4Add the minced chicken, breaking up any lumps, and sauté for 8 minutes until it changes colour and no longer looks pink.
5Add the peas and 1/2 cup water; select Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C) and simmer covered for 8 minutes until the keema is cooked through and the peas are tender.
6Sprinkle garam masala and coriander leaves before serving.

Tip: Great with pav or as a quick paratha filling — it also freezes well for a fast weeknight dinner.
