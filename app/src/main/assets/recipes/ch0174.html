Penne Arrabbiata (Spicy Tomato Pasta)

Chef Magic – Boil then Sauté Auto 22 min — Serves 3

Vegan • Everyday • Italian

🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic🍽 Serves 3
IngredientsStepsTips

Ingredients

  • 250g penne pasta
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 cloves garlic, finely chopped
  • 1 tsp red chilli flakes (adjust to taste)
  • 400g canned tomatoes, crushed (or 5 fresh tomatoes, pureed)
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1/2 tsp sugar
  • Salt to taste
  • Chopped basil or parsley, to garnish
  • Grated Parmesan (optional)

Method

1Add water and a pinch of salt to the jar; select Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C) and cook penne until al dente, about 11 minutes; drain and reserve a little pasta water.
2Add olive oil to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and cook garlic and chilli flakes for 1 minute until fragrant, not browned.
3Add the crushed tomatoes, oregano, sugar and salt; simmer on Sauté (Speed 1–2, ~100–110°C) for 8 minutes until the sauce thickens and deepens in colour.
4Add the drained penne and a splash of the reserved pasta water; toss on Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) for 2 minutes until well coated.
5Finish with basil or parsley and Parmesan if using; serve hot.

Tip: "Arrabbiata" means angry in Italian — the chilli flakes are meant to bring real heat, so don't be shy with them.
