Crispy Air-Fried Oyster Mushrooms

Airfryer (Bread + Fry) → Chef Magic (Toss batter, no cook step) — Serves 3–4

Vegetarian • Party Snack • Everyday

🔥 Airfryer🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic🍽 Serves 3–4
IngredientsStepsTips

Ingredients

  • 250g oyster mushrooms (dhingri), torn into large strips along the natural grain
  • 1/2 cup maida (plain flour)
  • 2 tbsp cornflour
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper (kali mirch), crushed
  • 1/2 tsp red chilli powder (lal mirch)
  • 1/4 tsp garlic powder
  • Salt (namak), to taste
  • 1/2 cup cold water (or as needed for a thick batter)
  • 1 cup panko or fine breadcrumbs
  • Oil spray

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 200°C for 4 minutes before adding the food.
2In Chef Magic, select Mix (no heat) and combine maida, cornflour, pepper, chilli powder, garlic powder and salt with the cold water into a smooth, thick batter.
3Dip each mushroom strip in the batter, letting excess drip off, then coat generously in breadcrumbs, pressing gently so they stick.
4Arrange in a single layer in the Airfryer basket, leaving space between pieces; spray well with oil.
5Air-fry at 200°C for 12–14 minutes, flipping and re-spraying halfway, until deep golden and crisp.
6Serve immediately with chilli mayo or tomato ketchup — oyster mushrooms turn chewy fast once they cool.

Tip: Tear, don't cut, the mushrooms — torn edges hold more breading and crisp up craggier than a clean knife-cut edge.
