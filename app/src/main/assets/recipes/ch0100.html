Punjabi Chole (Chickpea Curry)

Chef Magic – Sauté + Boil Auto 32 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • Vegan • High-Protein • Everyday

🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic🍽 Serves 4
IngredientsSteps

Prep ahead: Soak dried chickpeas overnight, or use 2 cans of drained, rinsed chickpeas to save time.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups chickpeas, soaked overnight
  • 2 onions, chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, pureed
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste (adrak-lehsun paste)
  • 2 tsp chole masala
  • 1/2 tsp red chilli powder (lal mirch powder)
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tea bag (for colour, optional)
  • Salt to taste
  • Coriander (dhania) to garnish

Method

1Add soaked chickpeas, water, salt and the tea bag (if using) to the jar; select Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C) and cook until fully tender. Discard the tea bag.
2Add oil to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and cook onions until golden.
3Add ginger-garlic paste, tomato puree, chole masala and chilli powder; sauté until the oil separates.
4Stir the cooked chickpeas back in with a little of their cooking water; simmer on Sauté (Speed 1–2, ~100–110°C) for 9 minutes until thick. Garnish with coriander.
