Mirchi ka Salan (Hyderabadi)

Chef Magic – Sauté + Boil Auto 24 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • Vegan • Hyderabadi

🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic🍽 Serves 4
IngredientsSteps

Ingredients

  • 8 large green chillies (hari mirch), slit
  • 3 tbsp peanuts, roasted
  • 2 tbsp sesame seeds, roasted
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 tsp tamarind (imli) paste
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric (haldi)
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt to taste

Method

1Grind the roasted peanuts and sesame seeds with a little water into a smooth paste.
2Add oil to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and cook the onion for 6 minutes until golden, then add the green chillies and cook 4 minutes until blistered.
3Add the peanut-sesame paste, turmeric, tamarind paste and salt with a little water.
4Select Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C) and cook for 10 minutes until the gravy thickens and coats the chillies.
