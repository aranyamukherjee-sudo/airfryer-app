Kadai Paneer

Chef Magic – Sauté + Boil Auto 21 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • Vegetarian • High-Protein • Everyday

🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic🍽 Serves 4
IngredientsSteps

Ingredients

  • 200g paneer, cubed
  • 1 capsicum, cubed
  • 2 onions, chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, pureed
  • 1 tbsp coarsely crushed coriander seeds (sookha dhania) & dried red chilli (kadai masala)
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste (adrak-lehsun paste)
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • Salt to taste

Method

1Add oil to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and cook onions until golden.
2Add ginger-garlic paste and crushed kadai masala; sauté 2 minutes until fragrant.
3Add tomato puree and salt; select Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C) briefly, then return to Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and cook until the oil separates.
4Add capsicum and paneer; sauté gently for 4 minutes, then sprinkle garam masala before serving.
