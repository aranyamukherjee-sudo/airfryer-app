Kukkad Rassa (Kolhapuri Chicken Curry)

Chef Magic – Sauté + Boil Auto 34 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • High-Protein • Everyday

Prep ahead: Dry-roast the Kolhapuri masala spices ahead of time and let them cool before blending.

Ingredients

  • 500g chicken, curry-cut pieces
  • 1/2 cup grated dry coconut (kopra)
  • 2 onions — 1 sliced, 1 chopped
  • 6–8 dried Kashmiri red chillies (lal mirch) (for colour, moderate heat)
  • 4–5 dried spicy red chillies (lal mirch) (adjust to taste)
  • 1 tbsp coriander seeds (sookha dhania)
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds (jeera)
  • 4 cloves (laung), 1 inch cinnamon (dalchini), 2 black cardamom (elaichi)
  • 1 tbsp poppy seeds (khus khus)
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste (adrak-lehsun paste)
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric (haldi)
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • Salt to taste
  • Coriander leaves (hara dhania) to garnish

Method

1In Chef Magic, dry-roast the coconut, dried red chillies, coriander seeds, cumin seeds, cloves, cinnamon, black cardamom and poppy seeds on Sauté (Speed 1–2, low heat) for 4 minutes, stirring often, until fragrant and lightly darkened. Cool slightly, then blend to a coarse masala paste with a little water — this is your Kolhapuri masala.
2Add oil to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and cook the sliced onion until deep golden brown, almost caramelised, about 8 minutes.
3Add the chopped onion, ginger-garlic paste and tomatoes; cook for 6 minutes until soft.
4Add the Kolhapuri masala paste and turmeric; cook for 4 minutes until the oil separates and the raw smell disappears.
5Add the chicken and sauté for 6 minutes until well coated.
6Add 1.5 cups water and salt; select Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C) and simmer covered for 15 minutes until the chicken is tender and the curry is fiery red.
7Garnish with coriander leaves and serve with rice bhakri or steamed rice.

Tip: This is the 'tambda rassa' — deliberately thin, deep red and fiery. If it's too hot, spoon some of the oil off the top rather than adding cream; cream isn't traditional here.
