Crispy Sweet Potato Fries

Airfryer – Fry 200°C 17 min — Serves 4

Ingredients

  • 2 large sweet potatoes, cut into fries
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 tbsp cornflour
  • 1/2 tsp paprika
  • Salt to taste

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 200°C for 4 minutes before adding the food to the basket.
2Toss sweet potato fries with cornflour first to help crisping, then with oil, paprika and salt.
3Arrange in a single layer in the basket.
4Air-fry at 200°C for 17 minutes, shaking every 5 minutes, until crisp at the edges.
