Chicken Korma

Chef Magic – Sauté + Boil Auto 32 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • High-Protein • Mughlai • Everyday

Prep ahead: Soak 15 cashews in warm water for 15 minutes for a smoother gravy.

Ingredients

  • 500g chicken, curry-cut pieces
  • 1/2 cup yogurt
  • 15 cashews, soaked
  • 2 onions, sliced
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste (adrak-lehsun paste)
  • 1 tsp coriander powder (dhania powder)
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric (haldi)
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • 2 green cardamom (elaichi), 1 cinnamon stick (dalchini)
  • 3 tbsp ghee or oil
  • 1/4 cup cream
  • Salt to taste
  • Coriander leaves (hara dhania) to garnish

Method

1In Chef Magic, add ghee; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and fry the onions until golden. Blend with the soaked cashews and a splash of water into a smooth paste; set aside.
2Add a little more ghee to the jar with cardamom and cinnamon; sauté for 1 minute until fragrant.
3Add ginger-garlic paste and cook for 1 minute, then add the chicken; sauté for 6 minutes until lightly browned.
4Add coriander powder, turmeric and salt; cook for 2 minutes.
5Stir in the onion-cashew paste and 1/2 cup water; select Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C) and simmer covered for 15 minutes until the chicken is tender.
6Whisk in the yogurt off direct heat to prevent splitting, then return to Sauté (Speed 1–2) for 3 minutes.
7Stir in cream and garam masala; simmer for 2 minutes and garnish with coriander before serving.

Tip: Korma should be pale and creamy, not red — skip the chilli powder and let the cashew paste carry the richness.
