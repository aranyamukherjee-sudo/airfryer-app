Egg Fried Rice

Chef Magic – Sauté Auto 16 min — Serves 3

High-Protein • Everyday • Indo-Chinese

🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic🍽 Serves 3
IngredientsSteps

Prep ahead: Use day-old, chilled rice — freshly cooked warm rice turns mushy when fried.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups cooked rice, chilled
  • 3 eggs, beaten
  • 1/2 cup mixed vegetables (carrot, beans, spring onion), finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp oil, divided
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1/2 tsp white pepper (safed mirch)
  • Salt to taste

Method

1Add 1 tbsp oil to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and scramble the beaten eggs for 2 minutes until just set; remove and set aside.
2Add the remaining oil to the jar; sauté the mixed vegetables for 4 minutes until just tender.
3Add the chilled rice, soy sauce, white pepper and salt; sauté for 8 minutes, breaking up clumps, until heated through.
4Fold the scrambled egg back in and toss for 2 minutes before serving.
