Honey Chilli Chicken

Airfryer (Fry the chicken) → Chef Magic (Toss in 200°C Fry 16 min + Toss 6 min glaze) — Serves 4

High-Protein • Everyday • Indo-Chinese

🔥 Airfryer🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic🍽 Serves 4
IngredientsStepsTips

Prep ahead: Marinate the chicken pieces for at least 20 minutes for the best flavour.

Ingredients

  • 500g boneless chicken, cubed
  • 3 tbsp cornflour
  • 2 tbsp maida (plain flour)
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • Salt to taste
  • Oil spray
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste (adrak-lehsun paste)
  • 1 capsicum, sliced
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp chilli sauce
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp toasted sesame seeds
  • Spring onion greens to garnish

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 200°C for 4 minutes before adding the food.
2Toss the chicken with cornflour, maida, beaten egg and salt; rest 20 minutes.
3Spray generously with oil and arrange in a single layer in the Airfryer basket.
4Air-fry at 200°C for 16 minutes, shaking halfway, until golden and cooked through.
5In Chef Magic, add a little oil; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and cook ginger-garlic paste and capsicum for 2 minutes.
6Add soy sauce, chilli sauce and honey; simmer on Sauté (Speed 1–2, ~100–110°C) for 2 minutes until it turns into a light, sticky glaze.
7Toss in the crispy chicken just before serving; sprinkle with sesame seeds and spring onion.

Tip: Toss right before serving — the honey glaze softens a crisp coating faster than a standard Manchurian sauce does.
