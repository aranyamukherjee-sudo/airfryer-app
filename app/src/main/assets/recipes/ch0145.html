Dahi Paneer (Yogurt Paneer Curry)

Chef Magic – Sauté Auto 18 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • Vegetarian • High-Protein • Everyday

🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic🍽 Serves 4
IngredientsStepsTips

Ingredients

  • 250g paneer, cubed
  • 1 cup yogurt, whisked smooth
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds (jeera)
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric (haldi)
  • 1 tsp coriander powder (dhania powder)
  • 1/2 tsp red chilli powder (lal mirch powder)
  • 1 tbsp besan (gram flour (besan))
  • 1 tbsp ghee
  • Salt to taste
  • Chopped coriander (dhania) to garnish

Method

1Add ghee and cumin seeds to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) until they splutter.
2Add onion and cook for 5 minutes until soft.
3Add turmeric, coriander powder and chilli powder; sauté 1 minute.
4Whisk the besan into the yogurt until lump-free, then stir it into the jar; select Sauté (Speed 1–2, ~100–110°C) and simmer gently for 8 minutes, stirring often, until slightly thickened.
5Add the paneer cubes and salt; sauté gently for 3 minutes until warmed through. Garnish with coriander.

Tip: Whisking besan into the yogurt before it goes into the hot jar is what keeps this gravy smooth instead of curdled.
