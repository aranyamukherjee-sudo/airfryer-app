Homemade Khoya (Mawa)

Chef Magic – Boil + Sauté 45 min — Makes ~200g

Gluten-Free • Vegetarian • Homemade

🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic
IngredientsStepsTips

Ingredients

  • 1 litre full-fat milk

Method

1Add the milk to the jar; select Boil (Speed 1, 100°C) and bring to a boil.
2Once boiling, switch to Sauté (Speed 1–2, ~110–120°C) and simmer, stirring frequently, as the milk reduces.
3Continue simmering and stirring for 35–40 minutes as the milk thickens into a soft, grainy mass, scraping down the sides regularly to stop it sticking.
4Once the mixture pulls away from the sides and forms a soft, dough-like mass, turn off and let it cool slightly.
5Use immediately in sweets, or cool completely and refrigerate for up to a week.

Tip: Stir constantly in the final 10 minutes — khoya can catch and burn quickly once most of the moisture has evaporated.
