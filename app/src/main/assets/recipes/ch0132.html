Vegetable Manchurian (Dry)

Airfryer (Fry the balls) → Chef Magic (Toss in 200°C Fry 15 min + Toss 9 min sauce) — Serves 4

Vegan • Everyday

🔥 Airfryer🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic🍽 Serves 4
IngredientsSteps

Ingredients

  • 2 cups finely shredded cabbage, carrot & capsicum
  • 3 tbsp cornflour
  • 2 tbsp maida (plain flour)
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste (adrak-lehsun paste), divided
  • Salt to taste
  • Oil spray
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp vinegar
  • 1 tbsp chilli sauce
  • 1/2 cup water mixed with 1 tsp cornflour

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 200°C for 4 minutes before adding the food.
2Mix shredded vegetables with cornflour, maida, half the ginger-garlic paste and salt; shape into small balls.
3Spray the balls generously with oil and arrange in the Airfryer basket.
4Air-fry at 200°C for 15 minutes, shaking halfway, until golden and crisp.
5In Chef Magic, add a little oil; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and cook the chopped onion with the remaining ginger-garlic paste for 2 minutes.
6Add soy sauce, vinegar, chilli sauce and the cornflour-water mix; cook 2 minutes until it thickens slightly, then toss in the fried balls just before serving so they stay crisp.
