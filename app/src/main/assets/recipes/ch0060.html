Kara Prasad (Air Fryer / Chef Magic Adaptation)

Airfryer – Bake (heatproof pan) 160°C ~20 min — Serves 4–6

🔥 Airfryer🍽 Serves 4–6
IngredientsStepsTips

Ingredients

  • 1 cup whole wheat flour (atta)
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 cup ghee
  • 3 cups water
  • 2 tbsp chopped nuts (optional)
  • 2–3 cardamom (elaichi) pods, powdered (optional)

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 160°C for 3 minutes.
2In a suitable heatproof pan, add the ghee and wheat flour. Cook at 160°C for about 8–10 minutes, stirring every 2–3 minutes, until fragrant and evenly roasted.
3Meanwhile, dissolve the sugar in the water and heat until fully dissolved and hot.
4Carefully add the hot sugar-water mixture to the roasted flour and ghee, stirring continuously to prevent lumps.
5Cook at 160°C for another 8–12 minutes, stirring frequently, until the mixture becomes glossy and pulls together.
6Mix in cardamom and nuts if using. Rest for 2–3 minutes before serving warm.

Tip: Kara Prasad is traditionally prepared on the stovetop; this version is adapted for the airfryer — use a heatproof pan and stir
