Masala Egg Omelette

Airfryer – Bake 170°C 9 min — Serves 2

Gluten-Free • Eggetarian • High-Protein • Everyday

Ingredients

  • 4 eggs
  • 1/4 cup chopped onion, tomato, green chilli (hari mirch)
  • 2 tbsp chopped coriander (dhania)
  • 1/4 tsp turmeric (haldi)
  • Salt & pepper to taste
  • 1 tsp oil or butter, for greasing

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 170°C for 4 minutes before adding the food to the basket.
2Whisk eggs with turmeric, salt and pepper; stir in onion, tomato, chilli and coriander.
3Pour into a small greased oven-safe pan that fits the basket.
4Bake at 170°C for 9 minutes until just set and lightly golden at the edges.
5Slide out onto a plate and serve hot with toast.
