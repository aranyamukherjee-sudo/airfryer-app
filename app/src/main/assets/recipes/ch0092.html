Instant Lemon Pickle (Quick Nimbu Achaar)

Chef Magic – Sauté Auto 10 min + rest 1 day makes ~1 cup

Gluten-Free • Vegan • No-Cook Finish • Everyday

🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic
IngredientsStepsTips

Prep ahead: Best made at least a day ahead so the lemons soften and the flavours meld.

Ingredients

  • 4 lemons, cut into small pieces, seeds removed
  • 2 tbsp salt
  • 1 tsp turmeric (haldi)
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder (lal mirch powder)
  • 1/2 tsp fenugreek (methi) seeds, roasted and crushed
  • 2 tbsp mustard oil (sarson ka tel)
  • 1/2 tsp asafoetida (hing)

Method

1Toss the lemon pieces with salt and turmeric; set aside for 10 minutes to release their juices.
2Add mustard oil to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and heat for 1 minute, then add asafoetida and crushed fenugreek; sauté 30 seconds until fragrant.
3Add the salted lemon pieces and chilli powder; sauté gently for 3 minutes to combine, then cool completely.
4Transfer to a clean, dry jar and rest at room temperature for at least a day before serving, stirring once daily.
