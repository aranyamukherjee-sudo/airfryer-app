Rajma Masala (Kidney Bean Curry)

Chef Magic – Sauté + Boil Auto 32 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • Vegan • High-Protein • Everyday

Prep ahead: Soak rajma (kidney beans) overnight for best texture and shorter cooking time.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups rajma (kidney beans), soaked overnight
  • 2 onions, chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, pureed
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste (adrak-lehsun paste)
  • 1.5 tsp rajma/garam masala
  • 1/2 tsp red chilli powder (lal mirch powder)
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt to taste
  • Coriander (dhania) to garnish

Method

1Add soaked rajma, water and salt to the jar; select Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C) and cook until soft and creamy inside.
2Add oil to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and cook onions until golden.
3Add ginger-garlic paste, tomato puree, masala and chilli powder; sauté until the oil separates.
4Stir the cooked rajma back in with a little of its cooking water; simmer on Sauté (Speed 1–2, ~100–110°C) for 10 minutes, mashing a few beans against the side to thicken. Garnish with coriander.
