Tandoori Grilled Chicken

Chef Magic (Mix marinade) → Airfryer 200°C Marinate 1 hr + Grill 22 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • High-Protein • Everyday

🔥 Airfryer🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic🍽 Serves 4
IngredientsStepsTips

Prep ahead: Marinate the chicken for at least 1 hour, or overnight in the fridge for the best flavour and colour.

Ingredients

  • 800g chicken (leg and thigh pieces, or a whole small chicken spatchcocked), skin scored
  • 1 cup thick curd (hung yogurt)
  • 2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste (adrak-lehsun paste)
  • 1 tbsp Kashmiri red chilli powder (lal mirch powder)
  • 1 tsp turmeric powder (haldi)
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp roasted cumin powder (bhuna jeera powder)
  • 1 tsp coriander powder (dhania powder)
  • 2 tbsp mustard oil (or vegetable oil)
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • Salt to taste
  • Lemon wedges and sliced onions, to serve

Method

1Preheat: When you reach the Airfryer step, preheat it to 200°C for 4 minutes before adding the food.
2In Chef Magic, blend the curd, ginger-garlic paste, chilli powder, turmeric, garam masala, cumin powder, coriander powder, mustard oil, lemon juice and salt into a smooth marinade.
3Score the chicken pieces deeply so the marinade penetrates, then coat thoroughly in the marinade. Cover and refrigerate for at least 1 hour, or overnight.
4Arrange the chicken in a single layer in the Airfryer basket, leaving space between pieces for even grilling.
5Grill at 200°C for 22 minutes, turning halfway, until the edges are charred and the chicken is cooked through (internal temperature 75°C).
6Rest for 5 minutes, then serve hot with lemon wedges and sliced onions.

Tip: A squeeze of fresh lemon and a light brushing of butter or ghee right after grilling gives the classic smoky tandoor finish, even without charcoal.
