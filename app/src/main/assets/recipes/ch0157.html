Chef Magic – Boil then Sauté Auto 26 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • Vegan • High-Fibre • Odia

🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic🍽 Serves 4
IngredientsSteps

Ingredients

  • 1 cup toor dal, washed
  • 2 cups mixed vegetables (pumpkin, raw banana, potato, brinjal), chopped
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric (haldi)
  • 1 tsp panch phoron
  • 2 dried red chillies (lal mirch)
  • 1 tbsp ghee
  • 3 cups water
  • Salt to taste

Method

1Add the dal, vegetables, turmeric and water to the jar; select Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C) and cook for 18 minutes until dal and vegetables are fully soft.
2Mash lightly with a spoon for a rustic texture.
3Add ghee, panch phoron and dried red chillies directly into the jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) for 2 minutes to temper.
4Stir well and season with salt before serving.
