Chorchori (Mixed Vegetable Dry Curry)

Chef Magic – Sauté Auto 21 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • Vegan • Bengali • Everyday

Ingredients

  • 2.5 cups mixed vegetables (pumpkin, potato, brinjal, beans), chopped
  • 1/2 tsp panch phoron
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric (haldi)
  • 1 green chilli (hari mirch), slit
  • 1 tbsp mustard oil (sarson ka tel)
  • Salt to taste

Method

1Add mustard oil and panch phoron to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) until fragrant.
2Add all vegetables, turmeric, green chilli and salt.
3Continue on Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) for 19 minutes, stirring occasionally, until vegetables are tender and slightly mashed at the edges.
