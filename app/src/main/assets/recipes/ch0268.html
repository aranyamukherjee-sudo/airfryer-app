Fish Fingers (Fish Sticks)

Airfryer – Fry 190°C 12 min — Serves 4

High-Protein • Everyday

🔥 Airfryer🍽 Serves 4
IngredientsStepsTips

Ingredients

  • 500g boneless white fish, cut into finger-length strips
  • 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 1.5 cups breadcrumbs
  • Oil spray
  • Lemon wedges and tartar sauce, to serve

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 190°C for 4 minutes before adding the food to the basket.
2Season the fish strips with salt and pepper.
3Dredge each strip in flour, dip in the beaten egg, then coat evenly in breadcrumbs, pressing to help them stick.
4Spray lightly with oil and arrange in the basket in a single layer.
5Air-fry at 190°C for 10–12 minutes, turning halfway, until golden and crisp and the fish flakes easily.
6Serve hot with lemon wedges and tartar sauce.

Tip: Freezing the coated fish fingers for 15 minutes before air-frying helps the crumb coating hold together better during cooking.
