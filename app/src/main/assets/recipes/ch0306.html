Sweet Tamarind Chutney (Meethi Chutney)

Chef Magic – Sauté 16 min — Makes ~1.5 cups

Gluten-Free • Vegetarian • Homemade

Ingredients

  • 200g tamarind (imli), soaked in 1.5 cups hot water for 30 minutes
  • 150g jaggery (gud), grated
  • 1 tsp roasted cumin (jeera) powder
  • 1/2 tsp red chilli powder (lal mirch powder)
  • 1/2 tsp dry ginger (saunth) powder
  • 1/4 tsp black salt
  • Salt to taste

Method

1Squeeze the soaked tamarind well to extract the pulp, then strain through a sieve to remove fibres and seeds; discard the solids.
2Add the tamarind pulp and jaggery to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 1–2, ~110–120°C) and simmer for 10–12 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the jaggery dissolves and the mixture thickens slightly.
3Stir in the roasted cumin powder, chilli powder, dry ginger powder, black salt and salt; simmer for another 3–4 minutes.
4Cool completely — the chutney will thicken further as it cools. Bottle and refrigerate for up to a month.

Tip: This chutney freezes well in an ice cube tray — pop out a cube whenever a recipe calls for a spoonful.
