Soft Pretzels

Chef Magic (Knead) → Airfryer (Bake) 190°C — Knead 5 min + Rest 1 hr + Bake 12 min — makes 6

Vegetarian • German

Ingredients

  • 2.5 cups flour
  • 1 tsp instant yeast
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 cup warm water (105–110°F / 40–43°C)
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp butter, melted
  • 1/4 cup baking soda + 2 cups water (for the bath)
  • Coarse salt to sprinkle
  • 1 egg, beaten (for brushing)

Method

1Preheat: When you reach the Airfryer step, preheat it to 190°C for 4 minutes before adding the food.
2In Chef Magic, dissolve yeast and sugar in warm water heated to 105–110°F (40–43°C) — warm to the touch, not hot; anything above 120°F (49°C) will kill the yeast; rest 5–10 minutes until frothy.
3Add flour, salt and melted butter; select Knead (Speed 2–3, no heat) until a smooth, elastic dough forms. Rest covered for 1 hour until doubled.
4Divide into 6 pieces, roll into ropes, and twist into pretzel shapes; dip each briefly in a bowl of baking soda dissolved in warm water.
5Brush pretzels with beaten egg, sprinkle with coarse salt, and arrange in the basket.
6Bake at 190°C for 12 minutes until deep golden-brown.
