Simple Mixed Vegetable Sabzi

Chef Magic – Sauté Auto 16 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • Vegan • Low-Oil • Everyday

🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic🍽 Serves 4
IngredientsSteps

Ingredients

  • 2 cups mixed vegetables, chopped (beans, carrot, potato)
  • 1 tsp mustard seeds (rai)
  • 9 curry leaves (kadi patta)
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric (haldi)
  • 1/2 tsp red chilli powder (lal mirch powder)
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt to taste

Method

1Add oil, mustard seeds and curry leaves to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) until seeds splutter.
2Add vegetables, turmeric, chilli powder and salt.
3Continue on Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) for 14 minutes, stirring occasionally, until vegetables are tender.
