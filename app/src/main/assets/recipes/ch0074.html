Roasted Masala Papad

Airfryer or Chef Magic — pick whichever you have free — 4 min — Serves 4

Ingredients

  • 4 papads
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 1 small tomato, finely chopped
  • 1/2 tsp chaat masala
  • 1 tbsp chopped coriander (dhania)
  • A squeeze of lemon

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 180°C for 4 minutes before adding the food.
2Option A – Airfryer: place papads in the basket in a single layer (roast in batches if needed), roast at 180°C for 4 minutes, flipping halfway, until crisp and bubbled.
3Option B – Chef Magic: select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) with no oil, place a papad flat in the jar, dry-roast 2 minutes per side, pressing gently, until crisp and bubbled; repeat for remaining papads.
4Top with chopped onion, tomato, chaat masala, coriander and lemon juice just before serving.
