Misal Pav

Chef Magic – Sauté + Boil Auto 24 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • Vegan • High-Protein • Maharashtrian

Prep ahead: Sprout the moong beans 1–2 days ahead (soak overnight, then rest in a damp cloth until sprouted).

Ingredients

  • 2 cups sprouted moong beans
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, pureed
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste (adrak-lehsun paste)
  • 2 tbsp misal/goda masala (or garam masala)
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric (haldi)
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 2 cups water
  • Salt to taste
  • Farsan (sev), chopped onion, lemon & pav, to serve

Method

1Add oil to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and cook onion for 5 minutes until golden.
2Add ginger-garlic paste, tomatoes, turmeric and misal masala; sauté for 7 minutes until the oil separates.
3Add the sprouted moong and water; select Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C) and cook for 10 minutes until sprouts are tender.
4Ladle into bowls and top generously with farsan, chopped onion and a squeeze of lemon; serve with pav.
