Paneer Kalakand (Airfryer-Set Milk Fudge)

Chef Magic (Cook mixture) → Airfryer (Set & lightly brown) 150°C — Cook 20 min + Set 12 min — makes ~16 pieces

Gluten-Free • Vegetarian

🔥 Airfryer🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic
IngredientsStepsTips

Prep ahead: Use fresh, soft paneer — firmer paneer won’t break down into the right fudgy texture.

Ingredients

  • 300g paneer, crumbled
  • 1 cup milk powder
  • 1 cup condensed milk
  • 2 tbsp ghee
  • 1/2 tsp cardamom powder (elaichi powder)
  • A few chopped pistachios, to garnish

Method

1Preheat: When you reach the Airfryer step, preheat it to 150°C for 4 minutes before adding the food.
2Add ghee and crumbled paneer to Chef Magic’s jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and cook for 5 minutes until the paneer softens.
3Add milk powder and condensed milk; continue on Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) for 15 minutes, stirring, until the mixture thickens and starts to pull away from the sides.
4Stir in cardamom powder, then spread the mixture into a greased shallow dish that fits the Airfryer basket.
5Bake at 150°C for 12 minutes until the top looks just set and lightly golden at the edges.
6Sprinkle with pistachios, cool completely, then chill for 1 hour before cutting into squares.

Tip: Kalakand firms up as it cools — it will look softer than expected straight out of the Airfryer.
