Classic Sponge Cake

Airfryer – Bake 160°C 20 min — Serves 6

Vegetarian • Everyday

🔥 Airfryer🍽 Serves 6
IngredientsStepsTips

Ingredients

  • 4 eggs, at room temperature
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 tbsp melted butter, cooled

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 160°C for 4 minutes before adding the food to the basket.
2Whisk the eggs and sugar together for 6–8 minutes until pale, thick and tripled in volume — this whipped air is what makes the sponge light.
3Sift the flour and baking powder together, then gently fold into the egg mixture in two batches, taking care not to knock out the air.
4Fold in the vanilla and melted butter until just combined.
5Pour into a greased oven-safe pan that fits the basket.
6Bake at 160°C for 18–20 minutes, until golden and springy to the touch.
7Cool in the pan for 5 minutes before turning out onto a rack to cool completely.

Tip: Don't skip the long whisking time in step 2 — a properly whipped egg-sugar mixture is the only leavening this cake needs.
