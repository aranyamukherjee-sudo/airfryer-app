Homemade Tomato Ketchup

Chef Magic – Boil + Blend + Sauté 45 min — Makes ~500ml

Gluten-Free • Vegetarian • Homemade

Ingredients

  • 1 kg ripe tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic (lehsun)
  • 1-inch ginger (adrak), chopped
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1/3 cup vinegar
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder (lal mirch powder)
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon (dalchini) powder
  • 1/4 tsp cloves (laung) powder
  • Salt to taste

Method

1Add the tomatoes, onion, garlic and ginger to the jar; select Boil (Speed 1, 100°C) and cook for 15 minutes until the tomatoes break down completely.
2Select Whisk/Blend (Speed 8–10) and blend until completely smooth, then pass through a sieve to remove skins and seeds if a very smooth ketchup is desired (optional).
3Return the puree to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 1–2, ~110–130°C) and add the sugar, vinegar, chilli powder, cinnamon, clove powder and salt.
4Simmer, stirring occasionally, for 25–30 minutes until thickened to a ketchup-like consistency that coats the back of a spoon.
5Cool completely, then bottle in a clean, sterilised jar. Refrigerate for up to a month.

Tip: Ketchup thickens further as it cools, so stop simmering when it's still slightly thinner than you want the final result.
