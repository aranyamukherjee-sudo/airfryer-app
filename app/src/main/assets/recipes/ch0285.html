Korean Fried Chicken (Yangnyeom Chicken)

Airfryer + Chef Magic – Fry 20 min + Sauce 2 min — Serves 4

High-Protein • Korean • Everyday

Ingredients

  • 500g boneless chicken thighs, cut into bite-sized pieces
  • 1/2 cup cornflour
  • 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 4 tbsp gochujang
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • Toasted sesame seeds, to garnish

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 200°C for 4 minutes before adding the food to the basket.
2Season the chicken with salt and pepper. Dip each piece in the beaten egg, then dredge in a mix of cornflour and flour, pressing to coat well.
3Arrange the chicken in the basket in a single layer, spraying lightly with oil.
4Air-fry at 200°C for 18–20 minutes, shaking halfway, until deeply golden and crisp.
5While the chicken cooks, combine the gochujang, honey, soy sauce, rice vinegar, garlic and sesame oil in the jar; select Sauté (Speed 1, ~90–100°C) and warm for 2 minutes until glossy.
6Toss the hot chicken in the sauce until well coated, garnish with sesame seeds, and serve immediately.

Tip: For extra-crackly restaurant-style crunch, air-fry the chicken once, let it rest for 5 minutes, then air-fry again for 5 minutes before saucing — the "double-fry" effect works in the airfryer too.
