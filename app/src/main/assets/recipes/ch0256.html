Butter Chicken (Murgh Makhani)

Airfryer (Grill chicken) → Chef Magic (Make 200°C Grill 16 min + Gravy 20 min gravy) — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • High-Protein • Mughlai • Everyday

Prep ahead: Marinate the chicken in yogurt and spices for at least 30 minutes (longer is better) before grilling.

Ingredients

  • 500g boneless chicken, cubed
  • 1/2 cup yogurt
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste (adrak-lehsun paste)
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder (lal mirch powder)
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • 3 tomatoes, pureed
  • 15 cashews, soaked
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 2 tbsp cream
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp kasuri methi (dried fenugreek (methi))
  • Salt to taste

Method

1Preheat: When you reach the Airfryer step, preheat it to 200°C for 4 minutes before adding the food.
2Marinate the chicken with yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, chilli powder and garam masala for at least 30 minutes.
3Arrange the chicken in the Airfryer basket and grill at 200°C for 16 minutes, turning once, until charred at the edges.
4In Chef Magic, add butter; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and cook pureed tomatoes with soaked cashews for 10 minutes, then select Whisk/Blend (Speed 8–10) to purée smooth.
5Return to Sauté (Speed 1–2, ~100–110°C), add sugar, kasuri methi and salt; simmer 4 minutes, then add the grilled chicken and cream, simmering a further 6 minutes.
