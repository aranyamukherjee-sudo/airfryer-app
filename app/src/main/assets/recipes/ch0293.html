Hung Curd (Chakka Dahi)

No-Cook – Hang/Strain 4–6 hrs — Makes ~250g

Gluten-Free • Vegetarian • High-Protein • No-Cook Finish • Homemade

IngredientsStepsTips

Ingredients

  • 500g fresh curd/yogurt (thick, homemade or store-bought)

Method

1Line a strainer or colander with a clean muslin cloth and set it over a bowl.
2Pour the curd into the cloth-lined strainer.
3Gather the corners of the cloth and tie into a bundle; hang over the bowl (or place a light weight on top) and refrigerate for 4–6 hours, or overnight for very thick hung curd.
4Discard or reserve the collected whey — it's great stirred into smoothies or dough.
5Transfer the thick hung curd to a bowl and use for dips, desserts, or as a substitute for cream.

Tip: The longer you hang it, the thicker it gets — go overnight for a Greek-yogurt-style consistency perfect for dips like tzatziki.
