Korean Corn Cheese

Airfryer – Bake 180°C 10 min — Serves 4

Vegetarian • Korean • Everyday

Ingredients

  • 2 cups sweet corn kernels (fresh, frozen or tinned, drained)
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1 cup grated mozzarella
  • 1/4 cup chopped spring onion
  • Black pepper, to taste

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 180°C for 4 minutes before adding the food to the basket.
2Mix the corn, mayonnaise, sugar and half the spring onion in an airfryer-safe dish.
3Top with the grated mozzarella and a good crack of black pepper.
4Air-fry at 180°C for 8–10 minutes until the cheese is melted and bubbling with golden spots.
5Scatter over the remaining spring onion and serve hot, straight from the dish.

Tip: This is best served bubbling hot with a spoon — the cheese firms up quickly as it cools, so don't let it sit around too long.
