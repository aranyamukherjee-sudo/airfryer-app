Catla Fish Fry (Spiced Airfryer Catla)

Airfryer – Marinate 20 min + Fry 200°C 14 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • High-Protein • Everyday

Prep ahead: Marinate the fish for at least 20 minutes before frying.

Ingredients

  • 6 pieces catla fish
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste (adrak-lehsun paste)
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder (lal mirch powder)
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric (haldi)
  • 1/2 tsp ajwain (carom seeds (ajwain))
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 3 tbsp rice flour or semolina
  • Salt to taste
  • Oil spray

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 200°C for 4 minutes before adding the food.
2Mix the fish pieces with ginger-garlic paste, chilli powder, turmeric, ajwain, lemon juice and salt; marinate for at least 20 minutes.
3Coat each piece in rice flour or semolina, pressing gently so it sticks.
4Spray generously with oil and arrange in a single layer in the Airfryer basket.
5Air-fry at 200°C for 14 minutes, flipping halfway, until crisp and golden on both sides.
6Serve hot with onion rings and mint chutney.

Tip: Catla has more bone than rohu around the belly — cutting it into steaks rather than fillets makes it easier to fry evenly and eat safely.
