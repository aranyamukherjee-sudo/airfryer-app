Pizza & Pasta Sauce (Marinara)

Chef Magic – Sauté + Boil 30 min — Makes ~500ml

Gluten-Free • Vegetarian • Homemade

Ingredients

  • 1 kg ripe tomatoes, roughly chopped (or 2 cans crushed tomatoes)
  • 4 cloves garlic (lehsun), sliced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1/2 tsp red chilli flakes (lal mirch flakes)
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • Salt to taste
  • A few fresh basil leaves (optional)

Method

1Add the olive oil and garlic to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 1–2, ~120–140°C) and cook for 1–2 minutes until fragrant, without browning.
2Add the tomatoes, oregano, chilli flakes, sugar and salt; select Boil (Speed 1, 100°C) and bring to a simmer.
3Reduce to Sauté (Speed 1, ~100–110°C) and simmer for 20–25 minutes, stirring occasionally, until thickened and the raw tomato taste has cooked out.
4For a smooth sauce, select Whisk/Blend (Speed 6–8) and blend briefly to the desired consistency; for a chunky sauce, leave as is.
5Stir in fresh basil leaves if using, just before serving.

Tip: A pinch of sugar balances the acidity of the tomatoes — taste and adjust at the end rather than adding it all upfront.
