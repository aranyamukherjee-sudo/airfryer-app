White Sauce (Béchamel)

Chef Magic – Sauté 10 min — Makes ~2 cups

Vegetarian • Homemade

🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic
IngredientsStepsTips

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 tbsp all-purpose flour (maida)
  • 2 cups milk, warmed
  • 1/4 tsp nutmeg (jaiphal), grated
  • Salt and black pepper (kali mirch) to taste

Method

1Add the butter to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 1–2, ~110–120°C) and melt completely.
2Add the flour and whisk continuously for 2 minutes to cook out the raw taste, without letting it brown.
3Gradually add the warm milk in small splashes, whisking constantly on Sauté (Speed 1–2) to avoid lumps, until fully incorporated.
4Continue cooking, stirring constantly, for 5–6 minutes until the sauce thickens enough to coat the back of a spoon.
5Stir in the nutmeg, salt and pepper. Use immediately in bakes, pastas or gratins.

Tip: Warming the milk before adding it prevents lumps and speeds up thickening — cold milk is the most common cause of a lumpy white sauce.
