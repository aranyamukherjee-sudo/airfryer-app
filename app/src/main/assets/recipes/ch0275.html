Chicken Fried Rice

Chef Magic – Sauté Auto 16 min — Serves 3

High-Protein • Everyday • Indo-Chinese

🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic🍽 Serves 3
IngredientsStepsTips

Prep ahead: Use a day-old cooked rice, chilled — fresh hot rice turns mushy when stir-fried.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups cooked basmati rice, chilled
  • 200g boneless chicken, finely diced
  • 1 egg, lightly beaten
  • 1/2 cup mixed vegetables (carrot, beans, capsicum), finely diced
  • 2 spring onions, chopped, greens and whites separated
  • 2 cloves garlic (lehsun), minced
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp vinegar
  • 1/2 tsp white pepper (safed mirch)
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt to taste

Method

1In Chef Magic, add 1 tbsp oil; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and cook the chicken for 6 minutes until cooked through. Remove and set aside.
2Add the remaining oil, garlic and spring onion whites; sauté for 1 minute.
3Add the mixed vegetables and stir-fry for 3 minutes until just tender-crisp.
4Push everything to one side, pour in the beaten egg and scramble for 1 minute, then mix through.
5Add the rice, breaking up any clumps, along with the cooked chicken, soy sauce, vinegar, white pepper and salt; toss on Sauté for 4 minutes until well combined and lightly charred in spots.
6Garnish with spring onion greens and serve hot.

Tip: Keep the flame (Sauté speed) high and toss quickly — fried rice should sizzle, not steam.
