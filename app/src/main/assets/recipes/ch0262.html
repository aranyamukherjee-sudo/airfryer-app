Catla Macher Kalia (Rich Bengali Catla Curry)

Chef Magic – Sauté + Boil Auto 28 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • High-Protein • Bengali • Everyday

Prep ahead: Rub the fish pieces with turmeric and salt and let them sit for 10 minutes before frying.

Ingredients

  • 6 pieces catla fish
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric (haldi), plus extra for rubbing
  • 1 onion, finely chopped or pasted
  • 1 tbsp ginger (adrak) paste
  • 1 tsp garlic (lehsun) paste
  • 1 tomato, chopped
  • 1/2 cup yogurt, whisked
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder (lal mirch powder)
  • 1 tsp cumin (jeera)-coriander powder (dhania powder)
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • 1 bay leaf (tej patta), 2 cloves (laung), 1 inch cinnamon (dalchini), 2 green cardamom (elaichi)
  • 1/2 tsp sugar
  • 4 tbsp mustard oil (sarson ka tel)
  • Salt to taste

Method

1Rub the fish pieces with turmeric and salt; let sit for 10 minutes.
2In Chef Magic, add mustard oil; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and shallow-fry the fish pieces for 2 minutes per side until golden. Remove and set aside.
3In the same oil, add the bay leaf, cloves, cinnamon and cardamom; sauté for 1 minute until fragrant.
4Add the onion paste and cook for 6 minutes until golden.
5Add ginger paste, garlic paste and tomato; cook for 4 minutes until soft.
6Add chilli powder, cumin-coriander powder, turmeric and sugar; cook for 2 minutes, then whisk in the yogurt off direct heat and stir through.
7Add 1 cup water; select Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C) and simmer for 6 minutes until the gravy thickens slightly.
8Slide the fried fish back in and simmer for 4 minutes more; sprinkle garam masala and serve with steamed rice or pulao.

Tip: Kalia is the richer, special-occasion cousin of jhol — the onion-yogurt base and whole garam masala make it thicker and more festive.
