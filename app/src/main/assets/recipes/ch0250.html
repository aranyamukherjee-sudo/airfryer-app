Amritsari Fried Chicken (Punjabi-Style)

Chef Magic (Mix marinade) → Airfryer 200°C Marinate 30 min + Fry 18 min — Serves 4

High-Protein • Everyday

🔥 Airfryer🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic🍽 Serves 4
IngredientsStepsTips

Prep ahead: Marinate the chicken for at least 30 minutes (overnight deepens the flavour).

Ingredients

  • 500g chicken (curry-cut, bone-in works best)
  • 1/2 cup yogurt
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste (adrak-lehsun paste)
  • 1 tsp carom seeds (ajwain)
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder (lal mirch powder)
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric (haldi)
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1/2 cup besan (gram flour (besan))
  • 2 tbsp cornflour
  • 1 egg
  • Salt to taste
  • Oil spray

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 200°C for 4 minutes before adding the food.
2In Chef Magic, mix the yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, ajwain, chilli powder, turmeric, garam masala, lemon juice and salt into a marinade.
3Coat the chicken well and marinate for at least 30 minutes.
4Whisk the besan, cornflour and egg into the marinated chicken to form a thick, clingy coating.
5Spray generously with oil and arrange in a single layer in the Airfryer basket.
6Air-fry at 200°C for 18 minutes, shaking the basket halfway, until deeply golden and crisp on the outside.

Tip: The carom seeds are what make this taste properly Amritsari — don't swap them out, they cut through the fry and settle the stomach too.
