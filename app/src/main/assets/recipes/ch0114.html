Aloo Posto (Potatoes in Poppy Seed Paste)

Chef Magic – Sauté + Boil Auto 21 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • Vegan • Bengali

🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic🍽 Serves 4
IngredientsStepsTips

Prep ahead: Soak poppy seeds (posto) in warm water for 25 minutes before grinding to a smooth paste.

Ingredients

  • 3 medium potatoes, diced
  • 3 tbsp poppy seeds (posto), soaked and ground to a paste
  • 1 tsp kalonji (nigella seeds)
  • 2 green chillies (hari mirch), slit
  • 2 tbsp mustard oil (sarson ka tel)
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric (haldi)
  • Salt to taste

Method

1Add mustard oil and kalonji to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) for 1 minute until fragrant.
2Add potatoes and turmeric; sauté 5 minutes until lightly coated and starting to soften.
3Add the poppy seed paste, green chillies, salt and a little water; select Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C) and cook until potatoes are fully tender and the paste thickens around them.

Tip: Posto paste can stick if rushed — keep the heat gentle and stir occasionally for the smoothest texture.
