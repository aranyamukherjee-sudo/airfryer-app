Chicken Noodle Soup (Everyday)

Chef Magic – Sauté + Boil Auto 24 min — Serves 4

High-Protein • Everyday

Ingredients

  • 200g boneless chicken, shredded or thinly sliced
  • 100g noodles (any thin noodles)
  • 1 carrot, julienned
  • 1/2 cup shredded cabbage
  • 2 cloves garlic (lehsun), minced
  • 1 inch ginger (adrak), julienned
  • 4 cups chicken or vegetable stock
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Spring onion greens to garnish

Method

1In Chef Magic, add oil; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and sauté garlic and ginger for 1 minute until fragrant.
2Add the chicken and cook for 4 minutes until it changes colour.
3Add the stock and soy sauce; select Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C) and bring to a simmer for 6 minutes.
4Add the noodles, carrot and cabbage; continue on Boil for 6 minutes until the noodles are just tender.
5Season with salt and pepper, and garnish with spring onion greens before serving hot.

Tip: A simple, everyday soup — swap in whatever vegetables you have on hand, it's forgiving that way.
