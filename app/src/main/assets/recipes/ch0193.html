Kimchi Fried Rice

Chef Magic – Sauté 10 min — Serves 2

Eggetarian • Korean • Everyday

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked, day-old rice
  • 1 cup kimchi, chopped
  • 2 tbsp kimchi juice (from the jar)
  • 2 tbsp gochujang
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  • 2 tbsp neutral oil
  • 1 fried egg per serving (optional)
  • Toasted sesame seeds, to serve

Method

1Add the oil to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–3, ~140–150°C) and heat.
2Add the kimchi and cook for 2–3 minutes until slightly caramelised at the edges.
3Add the rice, breaking up any clumps, and stir-fry for 3–4 minutes until heated through.
4Stir in the kimchi juice, gochujang and soy sauce, tossing until evenly coated and the rice turns a light red-orange.
5Drizzle with sesame oil, scatter over the spring onions, and cook for 1 more minute.
6Serve hot, topped with a fried egg if using, and a sprinkle of sesame seeds.

Tip: Day-old, cold rice fries up far better than fresh rice — the grains stay separate instead of turning mushy.
