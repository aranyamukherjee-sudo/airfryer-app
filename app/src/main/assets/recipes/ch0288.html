Korean Corn Dogs

Airfryer – Fry 190°C 14 min — Serves 4

High-Protein • Korean • Everyday

Ingredients

  • 4 chicken sausages
  • 4 wooden skewers
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 egg
  • 3/4 cup milk
  • 1 cup breadcrumbs or crushed cornflakes, for coating
  • Ketchup and mustard, to serve

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 190°C for 4 minutes before adding the food to the basket.
2Pat the sausages dry and skewer each one lengthwise.
3Whisk together the flour, sugar, baking powder, salt, egg and milk into a smooth, thick batter.
4Dip each skewered sausage in the batter to coat evenly, then roll in the breadcrumbs, pressing to help them stick.
5Spray lightly with oil and arrange in the basket, spaced apart.
6Air-fry at 190°C for 12–14 minutes, turning halfway, until golden and crisp. Serve hot with ketchup and mustard.

Tip: Popular street-food versions roll the coated corn dog in sugar right after frying for a sweet-savoury crust — try it if you have a sweet tooth.
