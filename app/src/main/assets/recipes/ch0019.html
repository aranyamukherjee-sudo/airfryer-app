Air Fryer Masala Omelette Muffins

Airfryer – Bake 160°C 11 min — Serves 2 (makes 6)

Ingredients

  • 4 eggs
  • 1/4 cup chopped onion, tomato, capsicum
  • 1/4 tsp turmeric (haldi)
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper (kali mirch)
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 tbsp milk
  • Oil spray for muffin cups

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 160°C for 4 minutes before adding the food to the basket.
2Whisk eggs with milk, turmeric, pepper and salt; stir in chopped vegetables.
3Pour into greased silicone or metal muffin cups that fit the basket.
4Bake at 160°C for 11 minutes until set and lightly golden on top.
5Cool slightly before removing from cups.
