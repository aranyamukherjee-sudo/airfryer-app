Rasam

Chef Magic – Boil Auto 16 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • Vegan • Gut-Friendly • Everyday

🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic🍽 Serves 4
IngredientsSteps

Ingredients

  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 tbsp tamarind (imli) paste
  • 1 tbsp rasam powder
  • 1/4 tsp turmeric (haldi)
  • 2 cups water
  • 1 tsp mustard seeds (rai)
  • 9 curry leaves (kadi patta)
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds (jeera)
  • 1 tsp ghee
  • Salt to taste
  • Coriander (dhania) to garnish

Method

1Add tomatoes, tamarind paste, rasam powder, turmeric, water and salt to the jar; select Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C) and cook for 11 minutes until fragrant.
2Add ghee, mustard seeds, cumin seeds and curry leaves directly in; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) for 2 minutes to temper.
3Garnish with coriander and serve hot.
