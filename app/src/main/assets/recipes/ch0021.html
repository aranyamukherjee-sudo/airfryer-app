Stuffed French Toast Sticks

Airfryer – Bake 170°C 9 min — Serves 2

🔥 Airfryer🍽 Serves 2
IngredientsSteps

Ingredients

  • 4 bread slices
  • 2 tbsp cream cheese or nutella
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tbsp milk
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon (dalchini)
  • 1 tsp sugar

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 170°C for 4 minutes before adding the food to the basket.
2Spread filling on two bread slices, top with remaining slices to make sandwiches, then cut into sticks.
3Whisk egg, milk, cinnamon and sugar; dip each stick briefly.
4Arrange in the basket, spaced apart.
5Bake at 170°C for 9 minutes, flipping halfway, until golden.
