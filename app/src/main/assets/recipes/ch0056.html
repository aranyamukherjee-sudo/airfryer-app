Rasmalai (Paneer Dumplings in Saffron Milk)

Chef Magic – Boil then Sauté Auto 34 min makes ~10

🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic
IngredientsStepsTips

Prep ahead: Use paneer made from full-fat milk curdled fresh that day, same as for Rasgulla — the fresher the paneer, the

Ingredients

  • 1 litre full-fat milk
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice or vinegar
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 4 cups water
  • 1 tbsp flour or semolina
  • 2 cups milk (for the rabri)
  • 3 tbsp sugar (for the rabri)
  • A few saffron (kesar) strands
  • 1/2 tsp cardamom powder (elaichi powder)
  • Chopped pistachios & almonds to garnish

Method

1Bring milk to a boil in a separate pan, add lemon juice, and stir until it curdles into paneer and clear whey. Strain, rinse under cold water, and hang in muslin for 30 minutes.
2Knead the paneer with flour on a smooth surface for 8–10 minutes until soft and completely smooth, with no grains left; divide into 10 smooth balls and flatten slightly.
3Add sugar and water to Chef Magic’s jar; select Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C) until the syrup is bubbling well.
4Gently slide in the paneer discs; continue on Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C), covered, for 15 minutes until they roughly double in size. Cool in the syrup.
5Meanwhile, in a clean jar, add the second batch of milk, sugar and saffron; select Sauté (Speed 1–2, ~100–110°C) and simmer for 12 minutes, stirring occasionally, until reduced and slightly thickened. Stir in cardamom powder.
6Squeeze the cooled discs gently to remove excess syrup, then soak them in the warm saffron milk for at least 1 hour. Garnish with pistachios and almonds before serving chilled.

Tip: Rasmalai discs should be flattened, not round like Rasgulla — a flatter shape soaks up the milk better.
