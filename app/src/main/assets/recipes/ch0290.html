Homemade Paneer (Fresh Cottage Cheese)

Chef Magic – Boil + Sauté 15 min + Draining 20 min — Makes ~250g

Gluten-Free • Vegetarian • High-Protein • Homemade

🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic
IngredientsStepsTips

Ingredients

  • 1.5 litres full-fat milk
  • 3 tbsp lemon juice or vinegar, mixed with 3 tbsp water
  • Ice cubes, for shocking (optional)

Method

1Add the milk to the jar; select Boil (Speed 1, 100°C) and bring to a gentle boil, stirring occasionally to stop it catching.
2Once boiling, switch to Sauté (Speed 1, ~90°C) and slowly add the lemon juice-water mixture, stirring gently until the milk curdles and clear whey separates.
3Turn off and let it sit for 2 minutes, then pour through a muslin-lined strainer, collecting the whey separately.
4Rinse the paneer briefly under cold water to remove any sour taste, then gather the muslin cloth and gently squeeze out the excess water.
5Tie the cloth and hang for 20 minutes to drain, or press under a heavy weight for 30 minutes for firmer paneer.
6Unwrap, cut into cubes, and use fresh, or refrigerate for up to 3 days.

Tip: Save the leftover whey — it's great for kneading dough or stirring into dal for extra nutrition.
