Air Fryer Chocolate Lava Cakes

Airfryer – Bake 180°C 11 min makes 4

Prep ahead: Chill the filled ramekins for 15 minutes before baking so the centres stay molten.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup dark chocolate, chopped
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 egg yolks
  • 1/3 cup sugar
  • 1/4 cup flour
  • Pinch of salt
  • Butter & cocoa powder, for greasing ramekins

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 180°C for 4 minutes before adding the food to the basket.
2Melt the chocolate and butter together (double boiler or short bursts in a microwave); cool slightly.
3Whisk eggs, egg yolks and sugar until pale and thick, then fold in the melted chocolate mixture.
4Fold in flour and salt until just combined.
5Divide between 4 greased, cocoa-dusted ramekins that fit the basket; chill 15 minutes.
6Bake at 180°C for 11 minutes until the sides are set but the centre still jiggles slightly.
7Rest 1 minute, then invert onto plates and serve immediately while the centre is molten.

Tip: Every airfryer runs slightly differently — start checking at 9 minutes the first time you make these.
