Naan Dough

Chef Magic (Knead) → Airfryer (Bake) 220°C — Knead 5 min + Rest 1 hr + Bake 6 min — makes 6 naans

🔥 Airfryer🧑‍🍳 Chef Magic
IngredientsStepsTips

Ingredients

  • 2.5 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp instant yeast
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 3/4 cup warm milk (105–110°F / 40–43°C)
  • 1/4 cup plain yogurt
  • 2 tbsp oil, plus extra for greasing
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • Melted butter and chopped coriander, to finish

Method

1In Chef Magic, dissolve yeast and sugar in the warm milk heated to 105–110°F (40–43°C) — warm to the touch, not hot; anything above 120°F (49°C) will kill the yeast; let sit 5–10 minutes until frothy.
2Add the flour, yogurt, oil and salt to the jar with the yeast mixture; select Knead (Speed 2–3, no heat) until a smooth, soft dough forms.
3Lightly grease the dough, cover, and let it rise for about 1 hour, until doubled.
4Divide into 6 portions and roll each into a teardrop or oval shape.
5Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 220°C for 4 minutes before adding the food to the basket.
6Air-fry the naans in batches at 220°C for 5–6 minutes, until puffed and lightly charred in spots.
7Brush with melted butter and scatter over coriander while hot.

Tip: A light brush of water on top of the shaped naan just before air-frying helps sesame or nigella seeds stick if you'd like to add them.
