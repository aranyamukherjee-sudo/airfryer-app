Paneer Momos

Chef Magic – Steam Auto 14 min — Serves 4 (makes ~15)

Vegetarian • High-Protein

Ingredients

  • 15 momo wrappers
  • 200g paneer, grated
  • 1/4 cup finely chopped cabbage & carrot
  • 1 spring onion, chopped
  • 1 tsp soy sauce
  • 1/2 tsp ginger-garlic paste (adrak-lehsun paste)
  • Salt & pepper to taste
  • Water for the steamer base

Method

1Mix grated paneer with vegetables, soy sauce, ginger-garlic paste, salt and pepper for the filling.
2Fill wrappers with the mixture and pleat closed.
3Add water to the jar, fit the steamer tray with momos on top.
4Select Steam (100°C steam, paddle off) and cook for 14 minutes until wrappers turn glossy.
5Serve hot with a spicy chilli-garlic dip or mint chutney.
