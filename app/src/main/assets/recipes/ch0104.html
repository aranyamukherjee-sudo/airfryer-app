Gujarati Dhokla

Chef Magic – Steam Auto Batter rest 15 min + Steam 16 min — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • Vegetarian • Low-Oil • Everyday

Prep ahead: Let the batter rest 15 minutes after adding fruit salt (ENO) or baking soda so it can aerate properly.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups besan (gram flour (besan))
  • 1 tbsp semolina (sooji)
  • 1 tbsp curd
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp ginger (adrak)-green chilli (hari mirch) paste
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 3/4 cup water
  • 1 tsp fruit salt (ENO) or baking soda
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 tsp mustard seeds (rai) & curry leaves (kadi patta) for tempering

Method

1Whisk besan, semolina, curd, oil, ginger-chilli paste, sugar, salt and water into a smooth, lump-free batter.
2Just before steaming, stir in the fruit salt or baking soda; the batter will turn frothy. Pour immediately into a greased dish that fits the steamer tray.
3Add water to the jar and fit the steamer tray; select Steam (100°C steam, paddle off) and cook for 16 minutes until a toothpick comes out clean.
4Temper with mustard seeds and curry leaves in a little hot oil, and pour over the dhokla before cutting into pieces.
