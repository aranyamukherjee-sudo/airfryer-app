Egg Kurma (South Indian Coconut Egg Curry)

Chef Magic – Sauté + Boil Auto 26 min — Serves 3

Gluten-Free • Eggetarian • Everyday

Ingredients

  • 5 eggs, boiled and peeled
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 tomato, chopped
  • 1/2 cup grated coconut
  • 1 tsp fennel seeds (saunf)
  • 4 cashews
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste (adrak-lehsun paste)
  • 1–2 green chillies (hari mirch)
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric (haldi)
  • 1 tsp coriander powder (dhania powder)
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 sprig curry leaves (kadi patta)
  • Salt to taste

Method

1In Chef Magic, blend the coconut, fennel seeds and cashews with a splash of water into a smooth paste; set aside.
2Add oil to the jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and crackle the curry leaves for 30 seconds.
3Add the onion and cook for 5 minutes until soft, then add ginger-garlic paste and green chillies; cook for 1 minute.
4Add the tomato, turmeric, coriander powder and salt; cook for 5 minutes until soft.
5Stir in the coconut paste and 1 cup water; select Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C) and simmer for 8 minutes until the gravy thickens slightly.
6Slit the boiled eggs lightly and slide them into the gravy; simmer for 5 minutes so they soak up the flavour before serving.

Tip: This is a milder, coconut-based cousin of North Indian egg curry — lovely with appams, dosa or plain rice.
