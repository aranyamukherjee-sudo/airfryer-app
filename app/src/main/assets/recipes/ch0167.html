American Chopsuey

Airfryer (Crisp the noodles) → Chef Magic 190°C Crisp 10 min + Gravy 16 min (Make the gravy) — Serves 4

Vegetarian • Everyday • Indo-Chinese

Ingredients

  • 150g thin noodles, boiled and cooled
  • 2 tbsp cornflour, plus extra for tossing
  • Oil spray
  • 1/2 cup finely chopped mixed vegetables (carrot, beans, capsicum, cabbage)
  • 2 tbsp tomato ketchup
  • 1 tbsp chilli sauce
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 cups vegetable stock or water
  • 2 tsp cornflour, mixed with water, for the slurry
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt & pepper to taste

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 190°C for 4 minutes before adding the food to the basket.
2Toss the boiled noodles lightly in cornflour and spray with oil; spread in the basket.
3Air-fry at 190°C for 10 minutes, shaking halfway, until golden and crisp; set aside.
4In Chef Magic, add oil; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and cook the mixed vegetables for 5 minutes.
5Add ketchup, chilli sauce, soy sauce and stock; select Boil (Speed 1–2, 100°C) and simmer for 6 minutes.
6Stir in the cornflour slurry and cook a further 3 minutes until the gravy turns glossy and slightly thick. Season with salt and pepper.
7Ladle the hot sweet-and-tangy gravy over the crispy noodles just before serving so they stay crunchy underneath.

Tip: Keep the noodles and gravy separate until the table — plated too early, the noodles turn soft under the sauce.
