Patatas Bravas

Airfryer (Fry potatoes) → Chef Magic (Make 200°C Fry 18 min + Sauce 10 min sauce) — Serves 4

Gluten-Free • Vegan • Spanish

Ingredients

  • 4 potatoes, cubed
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 can (400g) chopped tomatoes
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/2 tsp cayenne pepper
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil

Method

1Preheat: Preheat the Airfryer to 200°C for 4 minutes before adding the food.
2Toss the potato cubes with oil and salt and arrange in the basket.
3Air-fry at 200°C for 18 minutes, shaking halfway, until golden and crisp.
4Meanwhile, add olive oil to Chef Magic’s jar; select Sauté (Speed 2–4, ~130–160°C) and cook garlic for 1 minute.
5Add chopped tomatoes, smoked paprika and cayenne; simmer on Sauté (Speed 1–2, ~100–110°C) for 9 minutes until thickened. Spoon over the crispy potatoes.
